How to Get Rid of a Couch in Austin (2026)

Austin picks up couches by appointment (up to 3 a year). How to book, set-out rules, who qualifies, and why the Recycle Center won't take furniture.

The short version

Austin Resource Recovery will pick up a couch or other furniture as part of its on-demand bulk collection austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]. You have to book an appointment first; items left at the curb without one won't be collected austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

Book in the Austin Recycles app, on the City's My Schedule page, or by calling Austin 3-1-1 (512-974-2000) austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

There's no extra charge per pickup: bulk is included in the $24.90 monthly base fee on the solid waste part of your City utility bill austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[4].

Who qualifies

  • Single-family homes and buildings with up to four units that get City curbside service austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1] austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[2]
  • Some homes in certain Limited Districts austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
  • Five or more units: not covered. Those properties must use a private hauler austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[2]
  • Items set out in an alley, in front of a vacant lot, or in front of a business won't be collected austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]

How to book couch pickup

  1. Schedule through the Austin Recycles app, online, or by calling 3-1-1 austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].
  2. Select furniture (and every other bulk item you'll set out). The scheduler shows what can be collected austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].
  3. Watch your email for a confirmation and a reminder closer to the date austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

Need a different date? Appointments can't be changed. Cancel and book again, using the same method you booked with or the cancel link in your confirmation email. Call 3-1-1 if canceling gives you trouble austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

You get up to three bulk collections a year per address austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]. For the full bulk rules (tires, appliances, lumber and what isn't taken), see our Austin bulk item collection guide.

How to set the couch out

  • At the curb by 5:30 a.m. on your appointment date austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].
  • Put it in the non-metal pile. Bulk set-out is sorted into three piles: metal items and rigid plastics; non-metal items (including carpeting and nail-free lumber); and passenger car tires austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]. A couch belongs with the non-metal items.
  • Don't bag or box it. Bagged items are treated as extra trash and may bring extra trash fees austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].
  • Keep 5 feet clear of carts, mailboxes, fences and walls, water meters, phone and electric boxes, fire hydrants and parked cars. Nothing under low tree limbs or power lines austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

Missed pickup? Different trucks may come at different times. If the couch is still there after 4 p.m. on your date, call 3-1-1 or email Austin Resource Recovery after 4 p.m. and within 2 business days austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

Donate it if it's still usable

The City says it currently has no recycling or reuse option for furniture collected through bulk pickup, and asks residents to rehome or donate gently used furniture when they can austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]. A City reuse warehouse, funded with a $4 million EPA grant, is planned but not yet open austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

The City points to online marketplaces, neighbors and thrift or reuse stores, and to the Austin Reuse Directory for local reuse groups; contact them to ask whether they offer home pickup austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

Drop-off won't work for a couch

Austin and Travis County residents can use the City's Recycle and Reuse Center by appointment, free except for tires at $6 each austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[3]. It takes household hazardous waste, electronics and many appliances — but it does not accept furniture or mattresses austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[3].

So for a couch, your official options are a bulk appointment, a donation, or a private hauler.

Frequently asked questions

Will Austin pick up an old couch?
Yes. Furniture is on Austin Resource Recovery's accepted bulk list. Book an on-demand bulk appointment in the Austin Recycles app, online, or by calling 3-1-1 at 512-974-2000. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
How many times can I get a couch picked up in Austin?
Each address served by City curbside collection gets up to three bulk collections per calendar year. Brush, household hazardous waste and clothing each have their own separate three. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
Does Austin charge extra to pick up a couch?
No per-appointment charge. Bulk collection is bundled into the $24.90 monthly base fee on the solid waste part of your City utility bill. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[4] austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
Can I drop a couch at the Recycle and Reuse Center?
No. The center doesn't accept furniture, mattresses, toilets or non-recyclable trash. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[3]
I live in an apartment with five or more units. Can the City take my couch?
No. City curbside service, including bulk, is for single-family homes and buildings with four units or fewer. Properties with five or more units must use a private hauler. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[2] austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
Should I donate my couch instead of putting it out for bulk?
If it's usable, yes. The City says it currently has no recycling or reuse option for furniture collected at the curb and asks residents to rehome or donate gently used pieces first. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
