Austin Bulk Item Collection: How to Schedule and What They Take (2026)

Austin bulk pickup is by appointment only: 3 a year for homes up to fourplexes, booked in the app, online or via 3-1-1. What's taken, set-out rules, options.

The short version

Austin Resource Recovery picks up bulk items by appointment only. Each address served by City curbside collection gets up to three bulk collections a year austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]. Book in the Austin Recycles app, on the City's My Schedule page, or by calling Austin 3-1-1 (512-974-2000) austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

There's no extra charge per appointment: bulk collection is bundled into the $24.90 monthly base fee on the solid waste part of your City utility bill, which you pay whether or not you use it austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[4].

Who qualifies

  • Single-family homes and buildings with up to four units that get City curbside service austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1] austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[2]
  • Some homes in certain Limited Districts austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
  • Five or more units: not covered. Those properties must use a private hauler austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[2]
  • Items set out in an alley, in front of a vacant lot, or in front of a business won't be collected austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]

How to book

  1. Schedule through the Austin Recycles app, online, or by calling 3-1-1 austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].
  2. List every item. The scheduler shows what can be collected; select everything you'll set out austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].
  3. Watch your email for a confirmation and, closer to the date, a reminder austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

Need a different date? Appointments can't be changed. Cancel and book again, using the same method you booked with or the cancel link in your confirmation email. Call 3-1-1 if canceling gives you trouble austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

What they take

Accepted austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]:

  • Furniture
  • Appliances (remove the doors)
  • Household carpet
  • Doors
  • Lawn mowers (drain gas and oil)
  • Nail-free lumber
  • Pallets
  • Passenger car tires, rims removed, 8 per household max
  • Railroad ties (cut in half)
  • Rolled fencing

Not accepted austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]:

  • Household trash and cardboard boxes
  • Brush (it has its own separate appointments)
  • Mirrors, sheet glass, tile, and other construction and remodeling debris
  • Carpet from construction or demolition
  • Hazardous materials
  • Tires still on rims, and truck or tractor tires
  • Boats, trailers, motorcycles, and car chassis or bodies

The City says these lists aren't complete. Check a specific item in the Austin Recycles app or the City's What Do I Do With? tool austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]. Mattresses aren't named on either list, so look them up before you book.

How to set it out

Sort everything into three separate piles austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]:

  • Metal and rigid plastic: appliances (doors off), electronics and rigid plastics
  • Non-metal: carpet, nail-free lumber and the like
  • Passenger car tires, rims off

Then:

  • At the curb by 5:30 a.m. on your appointment date austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].
  • Keep 5 feet clear of carts, mailboxes, fences and walls, water meters, phone and electric boxes, fire hydrants and parked cars. Nothing under low tree limbs or power lines austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].
  • No bags, boxes or other containers. Bagged items are treated as extra trash and may bring extra trash fees austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

Missed pickup? Items may be collected by different trucks at different times of day. If anything is still there after 4 p.m. on your date, call 3-1-1 or email Austin Resource Recovery after 4 p.m. and within 2 business days austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

Furniture: donate first if you can

The City says it currently has no recycling or reuse option for furniture collected through bulk pickup, and asks residents to rehome or donate usable furniture instead austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]. A City reuse warehouse, funded with a $4 million EPA grant, is planned but not yet open austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

For donation options, the City points to the Austin Reuse Directory; contact the listed groups to ask whether they offer home pickup austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

Drop-off: the Recycle and Reuse Center

Austin and Travis County residents can drop items at the City's Recycle and Reuse Center, by appointment only (book in the app or online). It's free for residents, except tires at $6 each austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[3].

  • Takes: household hazardous waste, electronics, and appliances such as air conditioners, stoves, washers and dryers, water heaters and microwaves, plus scrap metal and many other recyclables austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[3].
  • Doesn't take: furniture, mattresses, toilets or non-recyclable trash austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[3].

So for a couch, mattress or recliner, your choices are a bulk appointment, a donation pickup, or a hauler.

Want it hauled away?

If you've used your three appointments, live in a building with five or more units, can't get a couch or mattress to the curb yourself, or need it gone before the next open date, a junk-removal crew can carry it out from inside your home. Frequently asked questions

How do I schedule bulk pickup in Austin?
Book an on-demand collection through the Austin Recycles app, the City's My Schedule web page, or by calling Austin 3-1-1 at 512-974-2000. Select every item you'll set out; you'll get a confirmation email and a reminder. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
How many bulk pickups do I get in Austin?
Up to three bulk collections each calendar year per address. Brush, household hazardous waste and clothing collections each have their own separate three. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
Does Austin still do bulk pickup twice a year?
No. Bulk collection is now on demand: you book an appointment instead of waiting for a neighborhood date. Items won't be picked up without an appointment. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
Will Austin bulk pickup take a couch or furniture?
Yes, furniture is on the accepted list. The City says it currently has no recycling or reuse option for furniture collected at the curb, so it asks residents to donate or rehome usable pieces first. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
Can I change my Austin bulk pickup date?
Not directly. You have to cancel and book a new appointment, using the same method you booked with (app or My Schedule page) or the cancel link in your confirmation email. Call 3-1-1 if you have trouble. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
What if the City missed my bulk items?
Different trucks may come at different times. If everything isn't picked up by 4 p.m. on your date, call Austin 3-1-1 or email Austin Resource Recovery after 4 p.m. and within 2 business days. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
Can I drop off furniture or a mattress at the Recycle and Reuse Center?
No. The center doesn't accept furniture, mattresses, toilets or non-recyclable trash. It does take appliances, electronics, household hazardous waste and many recyclables, by appointment, free for Austin and Travis County residents except tires ($6 each). austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[3]
