The short version

Austin's own trash department asks you to donate gently used furniture before using bulk collection, because furniture picked up at the curb currently has no recycling or reuse option austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[6]. Three big Central Texas charities can help:

  1. Habitat ReStore offers a free donation pickup service, booked 3-4 days ahead Austin Habitat for Humanity (austinhabitat.org)[1] Austin Habitat for Humanity (austinhabitat.org)[2].
  2. The Salvation Army schedules truck pickups by phone at 512-416-0607 The Salvation Army Austin Area Command (salvationarmyaustin.org)[4].
  3. Goodwill Central Texas picks up through its partner ReSupply, but fees apply Goodwill Central Texas (goodwillcentraltexas.org)[5].

All three want furniture that's clean, undamaged and sellable. If nobody will take it, the City's bulk collection is the fallback austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[6].

Austin Habitat for Humanity ReStore (free pickup)

If you can't drop off at a ReStore, Habitat offers free donation pick-up Austin Habitat for Humanity (austinhabitat.org)[1].

  • Book with the online scheduler on Habitat's pickup page. Allow 3-4 days of lead time Austin Habitat for Humanity (austinhabitat.org)[2].
  • Condition: all items must be new or gently used Austin Habitat for Humanity (austinhabitat.org)[1], and furniture must be in good, working condition Austin Habitat for Humanity (austinhabitat.org)[3].
  • Furniture it won't take Austin Habitat for Humanity (austinhabitat.org)[3]: used mattresses and box springs, broken or soiled furniture, armoires, entertainment centers, tube TVs, baby furniture, large office furniture such as cubicles, waterbeds and sofa beds. Trundle, bunk and storage beds are accepted at management's discretion.
  • Appliances: new appliances and used ones in good working order are accepted, except wall ovens; cooling appliances such as refrigerators or air conditioners must work Austin Habitat for Humanity (austinhabitat.org)[3].
  • Questions: email procurement@ahfh.org Austin Habitat for Humanity (austinhabitat.org)[1].

Drop off instead at any of three ReStores Austin Habitat for Humanity (austinhabitat.org)[1]:

  • South Austin: 500 W Ben White Blvd, Austin, TX 78704, (512) 478-2165. Mon-Sat 9 a.m.-8 p.m., Sun 11 a.m.-6 p.m.
  • Northwest Austin: 13804 N US-183, Austin, TX 78750, (512) 367-5795. Mon-Sat 10 a.m.-7 p.m., Sun 11 a.m.-6 p.m.
  • San Marcos: 2521 S Interstate 35, San Marcos, TX 78666, (512) 738-8383. Mon-Sat 10 a.m.-6 p.m., Sun 11 a.m.-6 p.m.

Habitat's site gives two cutoffs: one section says the donation drive closes one hour before the store closes, while the store listings say donation doors close 30 minutes before closing. Arrive at least an hour early to be safe. Austin Habitat for Humanity (austinhabitat.org)[1]

The Salvation Army (truck pickup)

The Salvation Army's Austin Area Command, which serves Travis and Williamson counties, takes gently used clothing, furniture, appliances and household items, and says items can be scheduled to be picked up The Salvation Army Austin Area Command (salvationarmyaustin.org)[4].

  • Schedule a pickup with one of its trucks by calling 512-416-0607 or toll-free 1-800-SA-TRUCK (1-800-728-7825) The Salvation Army Austin Area Command (salvationarmyaustin.org)[4].
  • Condition: drivers don't pick up items deemed not resalable. Mattresses and box springs, for example, must have no rips, stains or tears The Salvation Army Austin Area Command (salvationarmyaustin.org)[4].
  • Not accepted (partial list) The Salvation Army Austin Area Command (salvationarmyaustin.org)[4]: furniture with rips, tears or stains; homemade furniture; baby cribs and strollers; office desks, office dividers and modular office furniture; hospital beds; pool tables; swing sets; metal bed springs; unframed mirrors; built-in stoves, built-in microwaves and dishwashers; hot water heaters; and central A/C units and furnaces.

Drop off at a Family Store + Donation Center, open Mon-Tue 10 a.m.-7 p.m., Wed 10 a.m.-8 p.m., Thu-Fri 10 a.m.-7 p.m. and Sat 10 a.m.-8 p.m. The Salvation Army Austin Area Command (salvationarmyaustin.org)[4]:

  • 4216 S. Congress Ave., Austin, TX 78745, 512-447-4044
  • 8801 B Research Blvd., Austin, TX 78758, 512-836-2700
  • 601 W. Louis Henna Blvd., Round Rock, TX 78728, 512-246-7292

Goodwill Central Texas (paid pickup or free drop-off)

Goodwill Central Texas offers at-home pickup through its partner ReSupply (fees apply). Book with the online form or call 812-408-3665 Goodwill Central Texas (goodwillcentraltexas.org)[5]. You can also drop off at a Goodwill donation site; use its store locator to find the nearest one Goodwill Central Texas (goodwillcentraltexas.org)[5].

  • Takes: furniture in good condition, household and kitchen items, electronics (large and small), bikes, sporting goods and more Goodwill Central Texas (goodwillcentraltexas.org)[5].
  • Won't take Goodwill Central Texas (goodwillcentraltexas.org)[5]: mattresses, box springs and pull-out couches; damaged or stained furniture; baby furniture (cribs, strollers, car seats); large appliances such as refrigerators, freezers and air conditioners; large exercise equipment; tube, CRT and plasma TVs; and home building materials such as doors and cabinets.

If no charity wants it: the City options

Curbside bulk collection

Furniture is on Austin Resource Recovery's accepted bulk list. Curbside customers (single-family homes up to fourplexes) can book up to three bulk collections a year in the Austin Recycles app, online, or by calling Austin 3-1-1 at 512-974-2000. Nothing is picked up without an appointment, and items must be at the curb by 5:30 a.m., unbagged austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[6]. See our Austin couch guide for the full set-out rules.

The City is direct about what happens next: there are currently no recycling or reuse options for furniture collected through bulk collection. It is working to open a reuse warehouse, funded by a $4 million EPA grant, that will accept drop-off furniture donations and pass furniture on to nonprofits furnishing homes for people leaving homelessness austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[6]. No opening date is posted.

What the Recycle and Reuse Center won't take

The City's Recycle and Reuse Center (appointment required) lists furniture and mattresses among items it does not accept austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[7].

More places to donate

For a full list of local reuse organizations, the City points residents to the Austin Reuse Directory. Contact the organizations listed to see whether they offer home pickup Austin Reuse Directory (linked by Austin Resource Recovery)[8] austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[6].

Frequently asked questions

Who picks up donated furniture for free in Austin?
Austin Habitat for Humanity's ReStore offers a free donation pickup service; schedule it online with 3-4 days' lead time. Items must meet its donation guidelines and be in new or gently used condition. Austin Habitat for Humanity (austinhabitat.org)[1] Austin Habitat for Humanity (austinhabitat.org)[2]
Does Salvation Army pick up furniture in Austin?
The Salvation Army's Austin Area Command says you can schedule a pickup with one of its trucks by calling 512-416-0607 or 1-800-SA-TRUCK. Drivers don't pick up items they consider not resalable, such as furniture with rips, tears or stains. The Salvation Army Austin Area Command (salvationarmyaustin.org)[4]
Does Goodwill pick up furniture in Austin?

Where can I donate a used mattress in Austin?

Otherwise, see our Austin mattress guide for the City's bulk collection option. Can I drop off furniture at Austin's Recycle and Reuse Center?
