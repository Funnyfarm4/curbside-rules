How to Get Rid of a Mattress in Austin (2026)

Austin's Recycle and Reuse Center won't take mattresses. Curbside customers book on-demand bulk (3 a year); here's how to confirm, book and set it out.

The short version

Austin's Recycle and Reuse Center does not accept mattresses austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[2]. For homes with City curbside service, the City's route for large items is on-demand bulk collection, which you must book first: items left out without an appointment won't be picked up austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

One thing to know before you book: the City's list of accepted bulk items names furniture but not mattresses, and the City says that list is not complete. It tells you to use the Austin Recycles app or the What Do I Do With tool to confirm an item austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]. We couldn't confirm a mattress rule on the City's pages, so check the scheduler before you set one out.

Who qualifies

  • Single-family homes and buildings with up to four units that get City curbside service austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1] austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[3]
  • Some homes in certain Limited Districts austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
  • Five or more units: not covered. Those properties must use a private hauler austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[3]
  • Items set out in an alley, in front of a vacant lot, or in front of a business won't be collected austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]

How to book

  1. Schedule through the Austin Recycles app, the City's online scheduler, or by calling Austin 3-1-1 (512-974-2000) austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].
  2. Select every item you'll set out. The scheduling system shows which items can be collected austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].
  3. Watch your email for a confirmation and a reminder before the date austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

You get up to three bulk collections per calendar year, so bundle the mattress with other large items austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]. Appointments can't be changed. To move a date, cancel and rebook, using the same method you booked with or the link in your confirmation email austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

Cost

The City's page lists no per-appointment fee for curbside customers. Bulk collection is bundled into the $24.90 base fee on the solid waste part of your City utility bill, and you pay it whether or not you use the service austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[4]. Bags set out with bulk items can be charged as extra trash austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1], so don't bag the mattress.

How to set it out

  • Put it at the curb by 5:30 a.m. on the appointment date austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].
  • Don't bag or box it austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].
  • Keep it 5 feet from carts, mailboxes, fences or walls, water meters, utility boxes, fire hydrants and parked cars, and out from under low tree limbs or power lines austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].
  • Bulk is sorted into piles: metal and rigid plastics, non-metal items, and passenger car tires. Ask the scheduler which pile your mattress belongs in austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

Missed pickup? If items aren't collected by 4 p.m. on your day, call 3-1-1 or email after 4 p.m. that day, and within 2 business days austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

If it's not picked up, or you don't qualify

  • Larger buildings: use a private hauler austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[3].
  • Drop-off: not at the City's center austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[2].
  • Still in good shape? The City asks residents to consider reuse and donation before bulk collection. Donation rules vary by charity, so call first austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1].

Want it hauled away?

The City's drop-off center says no to mattresses, and bulk pickup only covers homes of up to four units, one appointment at a time. If you live in a larger building, have used your bulk appointments, or don't want to drag a mattress to the curb, a junk-removal crew can carry it out. Confirm they serve your ZIP and check the price before you book.

Frequently asked questions

Will Austin pick up an old mattress?
Austin's on-demand bulk collection is the City's curbside option for large items, but its published accepted list names furniture and not mattresses, and says the list isn't complete. Select the mattress in the booking tool, which shows what can be collected, or use the What Do I Do With tool to confirm before you set it out. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
Can I take a mattress to the Recycle and Reuse Center in Austin?
No. The center lists furniture, mattresses, non-recyclable trash, styrofoam packing peanuts and toilets as not accepted. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[2]
How do I schedule bulk pickup in Austin?
Use the Austin Recycles app, the City's online scheduler, or call Austin 3-1-1 at 512-974-2000. You get an email confirmation and a reminder to set items out by 5:30 a.m. on the appointment date. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
How many bulk pickups do I get in Austin?
Up to three bulk collections per calendar year for each address with City curbside service. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
Should I put the mattress in a bag?
No. Don't put bulk items in bags, boxes or other containers. Bags are treated as extra trash and may incur additional trash fees. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[1]
I live in an apartment with five or more units. Can the City take my mattress?
City curbside service, including bulk, is for single-family homes and buildings with four units or fewer. Properties with five or more units must use a private hauler. austintexas.gov (Austin Resource Recovery)[3]
