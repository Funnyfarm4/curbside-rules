Indianapolis Heavy Trash Pickup: Schedule, 2-Item Limit and Rules (2026)

Indianapolis picks up 2 heavy trash items a month on your assigned day, no booking needed. How to find your day, what's accepted, and where to take the rest.

Last checked against official sources: Oct 24, 2026 · Updated Oct 24, 2026

Quick answer

  • No booking needed. Every home on City trash service gets a heavy trash day once a month. Look up your day by address with the City's trash day tool. indy.gov[1]
  • Limit: 2 items a month. Put up to two heavy or bulky items at the curb next to your trash cart on your heavy trash day. indy.gov[1]
  • Accepted: furniture, mattresses and box springs (laid flat), appliances without Freon, drained lawnmowers, swing sets, bundled limbs. indy.gov[2]
  • Not accepted: construction debris, TVs with electronics inside, tires, and fridges, freezers or AC units (those need a separate Freon pickup). indy.gov[2] indy.gov[4]
  • Cost: no separate charge is listed. Monthly heavy trash is one of the services covered by property taxes and the $32 a year solid waste user fee. indy.gov[3]
  • Missed it or have more? Call the Mayor's Action Center at 317-327-4622, or haul extra items to the Citizens' Transfer Station ($2 a carload, $5 an SUV or pickup, cash only). indy.gov[4] indy.gov[6]

The short version

Indianapolis doesn't make you book bulky item pickup. Homes on City trash service get heavy trash pickup once a month on an assigned day, and you can put out up to two heavy or bulky items next to your trash cart that day indy.gov[1]. The limit is two items per month indy.gov[1].

Monthly heavy trash pickup is one of the services paid for through property taxes and the $32 a year solid waste user fee, which is billed in two $16 installments on your property tax bill indy.gov[3]. The City doesn't list any separate charge for it.

Step 1: Find your heavy trash day

  1. Open the City's trash day lookup and enter your address. It shows your regular trash day and your heavy trash day indy.gov[1].
  2. Note the date. Your heavy trash day isn't the same every week; it comes once a month, and the day varies by address indy.gov[1].
  3. Can't find it? The Mayor's Action Center (MAC) can look up your trash day. Call 317-327-4622, Monday to Wednesday and Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., or Thursday 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. and 3 p.m. to 5 p.m. RequestIndy is available online and in the app around the clock indy.gov[5] indy.gov[4].

Holidays: pickup slides one day later in a week with an observed holiday: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas. Holidays that fall on a weekend aren't observed indy.gov[1].

Who picks it up: since January 1, 2026, Lakeshore Recycling Services (LRS) collects trash in solid waste districts 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9 and 10, and DPW collects in districts 5, 7, 8 and 11. The City says heavy trash days and set-out rules did not change with the switch indy.gov[3].

Step 2: Check that your items are accepted

Heavy trash means bulky items that won't fit in your cart indy.gov[2]. Common accepted items indy.gov[2]:

  • Household furniture: couch, desk, dresser, table, chairs
  • Mattresses and box springs: lay them flat on the ground, not leaning on other objects
  • Appliances without Freon, such as a stove or water heater
  • Lawnmowers, with gas and oil drained
  • Swing sets and disassembled bicycles (an assembled bike can count if it's properly labeled)
  • Yard waste: bundled limbs 3 feet in diameter and 3 feet long count as one item

The City's 2026 cart FAQ also lists a TV shell, with all electronic components removed, as acceptable indy.gov[3].

Not accepted as heavy trash:

  • Construction debris indy.gov[2]
  • TVs with electronics still inside. Haulers won't take them, and Indiana's e-waste law bans electronics from the trash indy.gov[2].
  • Tires. Indiana landfills can't accept whole waste tires, so the City says you can't get rid of tires through curbside or heavy trash pickup indy.gov[2]. (One City FAQ lists tires mounted on rims as heavy trash indy.gov[3], but the City's main Trash 101 page says tires can't go out by law, so we follow the stricter rule. See our appliance and tire guide.)
  • Refrigerators, freezers, AC units and other Freon appliances. These need a separate Freon pickup through the MAC indy.gov[2] indy.gov[4].
  • Household hazardous waste and electronics go to the City's ToxDrop program instead indy.gov[2].

Step 3: Set it out the right way

  1. When: on your heavy trash day. Collection starts at 7 a.m., and your cart has to be at the curb before then indy.gov[1]. The City doesn't list a separate set-out time for heavy items, so have them out by 7 a.m. too.
  2. Where: at the curb, next to your trash cart indy.gov[1]. Carts go two to three feet from the curb, arrows facing the street or alley, at least five feet from other objects indy.gov[1].
  3. How many: no more than two items indy.gov[1].
  4. If something is rejected, your service provider leaves a tag explaining how to meet the pickup requirements indy.gov[1].

Missed pickup

Don't report a missed pickup the same day. Crews have until 6 p.m., and the MAC call center closes at 5 p.m. Report it the next business day through the MAC at 317-327-4622, then allow three business days for service indy.gov[4].

More than two items? Haul it yourself

The Marion County Citizens' Transfer Station takes extra household trash, heavy trash and bags of leaves indy.gov[6]:

  • Where: 2324 S. Belmont Ave.
  • When: Saturdays only, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. Closed the Saturdays closest to Christmas Day and New Year's Day.
  • Cost: $2 per carload or $5 per SUV or pickup truckload, cash only.
  • Limits: up to two trips a day. No moving trucks, dual axles, trailers, haulers, stake-bed trucks, or truckloads larger than 3/4 ton.

If that doesn't work, the City says you can take trash directly to South Side Landfill, 2561 Kentucky Ave., open Monday to Friday 5 a.m. to 7 p.m. and Saturday 5 a.m. to 3 p.m. Pricing is based on volume; the City links to the landfill for current rates indy.gov[2].

Still in good shape? The City suggests donating usable items first and lists local donation centers at indy.gov/recycle indy.gov[7].

Hiring a hauler

The City's own advice for bigger cleanouts is to rent a dumpster or hire a responsible hauler indy.gov[8]. Before you pay anyone:

  • Ask to see their Waste Hauler License and don't pay without a disposal receipt indy.gov[7].
  • The City notes that properly disposing a truckload costs contractors at least $60, so be suspicious of anyone charging less indy.gov[7].
  • Ask how they plan to dispose of the trash. Some people who offer cheap removal dump it illegally indy.gov[8].

Don't dump it

Leaving trash or unwanted items on public or private property without permission violates Sec. 601-2 of the municipal code, with fines of up to $2,500 per instance indy.gov[8]. Furniture, mattresses and garbage left at City recycling drop-off sites count as illegal dumping too indy.gov[7]. Report dumping through RequestIndy or the MAC at 317-327-4622 indy.gov[8].

Frequently asked questions

How do I schedule heavy trash pickup in Indianapolis?
You don't need to. Heavy trash is collected once a month on a set day for your address. Enter your address in the City's trash day lookup to see both your regular and heavy trash days. indy.gov[1]
How many heavy trash items can I put out in Indianapolis?
Two. The City says you can place up to two heavy or bulky items next to your trash cart on your heavy trash day, and you're limited to two a month. indy.gov[1]
Will Indianapolis pick up a mattress or couch?
Yes, as heavy trash. Household furniture like couches, desks, dressers, tables and chairs is accepted, and mattresses and box springs are accepted if laid flat on the ground, not leaning on anything. Each counts toward your two-item monthly limit. indy.gov[2]
Does heavy trash pick up refrigerators?
Not on your heavy trash day. Appliances with Freon, such as refrigerators, freezers and AC units, need a separate pickup scheduled through the Mayor's Action Center. Allow ten business days. Appliances without Freon, like a stove or water heater, can go out as heavy trash. indy.gov[2] indy.gov[4]
Did heavy trash day change when LRS took over in 2026?
No. The City says pickup days, including heavy trash, stayed the same, and there was no change in what can be set out. LRS serves solid waste districts 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9 and 10, and DPW still serves districts 5, 7, 8 and 11. indy.gov[3]
What if my heavy trash wasn't picked up?
Wait until the next business day, because crews have until 6 p.m. Then report it to the Mayor's Action Center at 317-327-4622 and allow three business days. If an item wasn't acceptable, the crew leaves a tag explaining why. indy.gov[4] indy.gov[1]
