How to Get Rid of a Mattress in Indianapolis (2026)

Indianapolis picks up mattresses and box springs as heavy trash once a month, laid flat, 2 items max. Find your day or drop it off for $2 to $5.

The short version

Indianapolis picks up old mattresses for free as heavy trash. Box springs and mattresses are on the City's heavy trash list, and the one rule specific to them is to lay them flat on the ground, not leaning against your cart, a tree or anything else indy.gov[1]. Homes on City trash service get heavy trash pickup once a month on an assigned day, with a limit of two heavy or bulky items a month indy.gov[2].

There's nothing to book. The catch is timing: if your heavy trash day just passed, or you're tossing more than two pieces, you'll need to drop it off yourself or pay someone to take it.

Option 1: Heavy trash day (free, 2 items a month)

  1. Find your day. Enter your address in the City's trash day lookup. It shows both your regular and heavy trash days indy.gov[2]. Or call the Mayor's Action Center (MAC) at 317-327-4622, Monday to Wednesday and Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Thursday 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. and 3 p.m. to 5 p.m. indy.gov[4]
  2. Count your items. You can set out up to two heavy items, and that's your limit for the month indy.gov[2]. A mattress plus its box spring is two.
  3. Set it out flat. Put the mattress at the curb next to your trash cart, lying flat on the ground indy.gov[1] indy.gov[2]. Collection starts at 7 a.m. indy.gov[2], so have it out before then.
  4. Holiday weeks: pickup slides one day later in weeks with an observed holiday, including Thanksgiving and Christmas. Holidays on a weekend aren't observed indy.gov[2].

If the crew can't take something, your service provider leaves a tag that explains how to meet the pickup requirements indy.gov[2].

Missed pickup

Don't report it the same day. Crews have until 6 p.m. and the MAC call center closes at 5 p.m. Call 317-327-4622 the next business day and allow three business days for service indy.gov[4].

Option 2: Drop it off yourself

Marion County Citizens' Transfer Station indy.gov[3]:

  • Address: 2324 S. Belmont Ave.
  • Hours: Saturdays only, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. Closed the Saturday closest to Christmas Day and the Saturday closest to New Year's Day.
  • Cost: $2 per carload or $5 per SUV or pickup truckload, cash only.
  • Limits: up to two trips a day. Moving trucks, dual axles, trailers, haulers, stake-bed trucks and truckloads larger than 3/4 ton aren't permitted.

If the only way you can move your mattress is on a trailer or in a rented moving truck, the transfer station won't take that load.

South Side Landfill, 2561 Kentucky Ave., is the City's last-resort option. It's open Monday to Friday 5 a.m. to 7 p.m. and Saturday 5 a.m. to 3 p.m., closed Sunday, with pricing based on volume indy.gov[1].

Option 3: Donate it

If the mattress is in good condition, the City suggests donating it and keeps a list of local donation centers on indy.gov's Recycling 101 page indy.gov[5]. Each charity sets its own rules, so call before you load it.

Hiring a hauler

For a mattress stuck on an upper floor, several beds from a move, or anything past your two-item limit, the City's advice is to rent a dumpster or hire a responsible trash hauler indy.gov[6]. Before you pay:

  • Ask to see their Waste Hauler License and don't pay without a disposal receipt indy.gov[5].
  • The City says properly disposing a truckload costs contractors at least $60, so be suspicious of a much lower price indy.gov[5].
  • Check references, whether they're licensed by the City, and how they plan to dispose of it. Some cheap removal offers end with the load dumped illegally indy.gov[6].

Don't dump it

Mattresses are named on the City's list of things that count as illegal dumping when left at recycling drop-off sites indy.gov[5]. Leaving one on public or private property without permission breaks Sec. 601-2 of the municipal code, with fines up to $2,500 per instance indy.gov[6]. If someone dumps on your property, you're still responsible for the cleanup, and if a City contractor has to do it you're billed at least $418 indy.gov[6].

Frequently asked questions

Does Indianapolis pick up mattresses?
Yes. Mattresses and box springs are accepted as heavy trash. Lay them flat on the ground next to your trash cart on your monthly heavy trash day. indy.gov[1] indy.gov[2]
Do I need to schedule mattress pickup in Indianapolis?
No. Heavy trash is collected once a month on a set day for your address. Look up the day with the City's trash day tool or call the Mayor's Action Center at 317-327-4622. indy.gov[2] indy.gov[4]
Does a box spring count as a separate item?
The City limits you to two heavy or bulky items a month and lists box springs and mattresses as heavy trash, so plan on a mattress and its box spring being your two items for that month. indy.gov[1] indy.gov[2]
Where can I drop off a mattress in Indianapolis?
The Marion County Citizens' Transfer Station at 2324 S. Belmont Ave. takes heavy trash on Saturdays from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., for $2 a carload or $5 for an SUV or pickup truckload, cash only. Trailers and moving trucks aren't allowed. South Side Landfill, 2561 Kentucky Ave., also takes trash, priced by volume. indy.gov[3] indy.gov[1]
Can I leave a mattress at a recycling bin site?
No. The City specifically lists mattresses among the items that count as illegal dumping at recycling drop-off sites, with fines up to $2,500. indy.gov[5]
What if my mattress wasn't picked up?
Crews have until 6 p.m., so wait until the next business day, then call the Mayor's Action Center at 317-327-4622 and allow three business days. If the item isn't acceptable, the crew leaves a tag explaining why. indy.gov[4] indy.gov[2]
