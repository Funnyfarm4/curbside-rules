Free Furniture Donation Pickup in Indianapolis (2026)

Who picks up donated furniture in Indianapolis: Habitat ReStore and SVdP do it free, Mustard Seed charges a fee. What each refuses, lead times and the fallback.

Last checked against official sources: Oct 26, 2026 · Updated Oct 26, 2026

Quick answer

  • Free pickup: Habitat for Humanity ReStore picks up donations at no cost. Call a store's donation line or use its online request form, and ask about two weeks ahead. Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2]
  • Also free: SVdP Indy picks up furniture at no cost, typically within two weeks, if it's disassembled and on the main level, in a garage or outside. Call 317-687-1006. SVdP Indy[4]
  • Paid but flexible: The Mustard Seed of Central Indiana charges a pickup service fee, can't do same-day, and only travels up to 25 miles from its Fishers warehouse. The Mustard Seed of Central Indiana[6] The Mustard Seed of Central Indiana[7]
  • Condition is everything: all three turn away stained, torn, damaged or smelly pieces, and Habitat can refuse items right at your door. Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2] SVdP Indy[4] The Mustard Seed of Central Indiana[7]
  • Turned down? Put up to two items out on your monthly heavy trash day, or take them to the Citizens' Transfer Station on Saturdays ($2 a carload, $5 an SUV or pickup, cash only). indy.gov[8] indy.gov[9]

The short version

If your furniture is clean and in good shape, two Indianapolis charities will pick it up for free: Habitat for Humanity's ReStore Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2] and SVdP Indy SVdP Indy[4]. Both are on the City's list of local donation centers indy.gov[1]. The catches are condition and timing. Each turns away stained, torn or damaged pieces, and free pickups are often about two weeks out Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2] SVdP Indy[4].

If you're moving this week, the pieces are upstairs, or a charity said no, skip to what to do if they won't take it.

Option 1: Habitat for Humanity ReStore (free pickup)

Greater Indy Habitat runs four ReStores and picks up donations at no cost Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2].

  • What they take: sofas, sectionals, chairs, recliners, tables, benches, trunks, bedroom furniture (including bunk beds, day beds and trundle beds), office furniture, patio furniture and consoles. They also take appliances such as refrigerators, freezers, stoves, washers and dryers, plus cabinets, lighting, tools and home decor Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2].
  • Condition: items must be in excellent working condition, with no rips, tears, stains, mold or odors, and no broken or missing parts. They can refuse donations at any point, including on-site, because of condition or disposal costs Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2].
  • How to schedule: call a store's donation line or fill out the online request form. A coordinator asks about condition, size and location Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2]. Habitat suggests using the online form, since the phone lines often go to voicemail Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2].
  • Lead time: contact them about two weeks before the date you want. Shorter notice is still worth a call, since the schedule changes daily Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2].
  • When: routes typically run Monday to Saturday, around 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., and each pickup gets a specific time. They aim to arrive within 15 minutes of it Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2].
  • Receipt: yes, listing your name, the date and the items Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2].

Donation lines Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2]:

  • West Washington (4129 W. Washington St., Indianapolis): 317-969-8791 Habitat for Humanity[3]
  • Avon (1099 N. Avon Ave.): 317-707-7530 Habitat for Humanity[3]
  • Fishers (7998 Centerpoint Dr., Suite 100): 317-896-9423 Habitat for Humanity[3]
  • Greenfield (1141 W. US 40): 317-318-9458 Habitat for Humanity[3]

Dropping off instead? All four stores take drop-offs Tuesday to Saturday, 10 a.m. to 7 p.m., no appointment needed. Call ahead if you're bringing a big truckload Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2].

Option 2: SVdP Indy (free pickup, main level only)

The Society of St. Vincent de Paul gives donations first to neighbors in need, then sells the rest in its Mission 27 stores SVdP Indy[4].

  • Free pickup: typically within two weeks. Items must be disassembled and placed on the main level, in a garage or outside, and SVdP only takes items it can give away or resell. Small items must be in boxes, not bags SVdP Indy[4].
  • How to schedule: call 317-687-1006 SVdP Indy[4]. SVdP's pickup post lists pickup days as Tuesday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. SVdP Indy[5]
  • Faster, paid option: SVdP's partner ReSupply picks up within 48 hours, from any floor, will disassemble furniture, and accepts items in any condition. It charges a fee, which is not tax deductible SVdP Indy[4]. Items SVdP can't use go to other local nonprofits or are disposed of SVdP Indy[5].
  • Drop-off: SVdP's Distribution Center, 1201 Maryland St., Dock Door 3 (south side of the building), Monday to Saturday 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. SVdP Indy[4]

Option 3: The Mustard Seed of Central Indiana (paid pickup)

This small nonprofit furnishes homes for families in need indy.gov[1]. It charges a pickup service fee to cover its trucks and warehouse, and it doesn't post the base amount on its pages The Mustard Seed of Central Indiana[7].

  • Booking: pickups are scheduled online only. They reply by email within about 24 hours, can't do same-day pickups, and don't send reminders The Mustard Seed of Central Indiana[6].
  • Distance: they don't travel more than 25 miles from their Fishers location, 10080 E. 121st St. The Mustard Seed of Central Indiana[6]
  • They take: twin, full and queen mattresses and box springs with no stains, sofas, loveseats, armchairs, recliners, ottomans, dining tables and chairs, dressers, nightstands, and coffee and end tables The Mustard Seed of Central Indiana[7].
  • They don't take: glass-top dining tables, large appliances, filing cabinets or medical items. Sleeper sofas cost an extra $100, armoires $100, headboards and footboards $25, and like-new king beds $50 The Mustard Seed of Central Indiana[7].
  • Access: no pickups from the third floor or higher without an elevator, and no items from homes with pests The Mustard Seed of Central Indiana[7].

Not on this list?

The City's donation list also includes Diversion Indy (high-quality, nearly new furniture and home goods), Thrifty Threads (furniture and home goods benefiting the Julian Center), Coburn Place and Exodus Refugee indy.gov[1]. Check each charity's own site for pickup options before you plan around them.

If no charity will take it

  1. Heavy trash day (free): put up to two pieces at the curb next to your trash cart on your monthly heavy trash day. Couches, desks, dressers, tables, chairs and mattresses (laid flat) are all accepted indy.gov[8]. See our Indianapolis heavy trash guide.
  2. Citizens' Transfer Station: 2324 S. Belmont Ave., Saturdays 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., $2 a carload or $5 an SUV or pickup, cash only, up to two trips a day. No trailers or moving trucks indy.gov[9].
  3. Hire a hauler: ask to see their Waste Hauler License, don't pay without a disposal receipt, and be suspicious of anyone charging under $60 a truckload indy.gov[10].

Charity said no, or can't come in time?

Frequently asked questions

Who picks up donated furniture for free in Indianapolis?
Habitat for Humanity's ReStore and SVdP Indy both run free donation pickups. Habitat takes furniture and appliances in excellent condition, and SVdP takes items it can give to neighbors in need or sell in its Mission 27 stores. Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2] SVdP Indy[4]
How far ahead should I schedule a furniture donation pickup?
About two weeks. Habitat recommends calling around two weeks before the date you want, though openings change daily, and SVdP's free pickups are typically scheduled within two weeks. Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2] SVdP Indy[4]
Will a charity pick up furniture from upstairs or a basement?
SVdP's free pickup only takes items on the main level, in a garage or outside, already disassembled. Its paid partner ReSupply picks up from any floor within 48 hours. The Mustard Seed won't pick up from the third floor or higher without an elevator. SVdP Indy[4] SVdP Indy[5] The Mustard Seed of Central Indiana[7]
Can I donate a sleeper sofa or sectional in Indianapolis?
Habitat's ReStore lists sofas and sectionals among the furniture it accepts, in excellent condition. The Mustard Seed charges an extra $100 to remove a sleeper sofa. Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2] The Mustard Seed of Central Indiana[7]
Do I get a tax receipt for donated furniture?
Habitat and The Mustard Seed both say they provide donation receipts. SVdP's partner ReSupply charges a pickup fee that is not tax deductible. Greater Indy Habitat for Humanity[2] The Mustard Seed of Central Indiana[7] SVdP Indy[4]
What if no charity will take my furniture?
Use your monthly heavy trash day for up to two items, take it to the Citizens' Transfer Station on a Saturday, or hire a licensed hauler. Don't leave it at a City recycling drop-off: that counts as illegal dumping, with fines up to $2,500. indy.gov[8] indy.gov[9] indy.gov[10]
