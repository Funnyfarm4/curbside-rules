Indianapolis Yard Waste and Leaf Collection Rules (2026)

2026 leaf season is Nov 9–Dec 4 with 40 bags a week. Year-round: bag yard waste in the cart, heavy-trash limbs, or drop at GreenCycle and the transfer station.

The short version

Indianapolis treats leaves and other yard waste differently depending on the season and how much you have.

  • Leaf collection season (2026: November 9–December 4): put up to 40 bags of leaves out each week with regular trash indy.gov[3].
  • The rest of the year: bagged leaves and small yard waste go inside your trash cart with the lid closed; bags outside the cart aren't picked up indy.gov[1] indy.gov[3].
  • Brush/limbs: a bundle 3 feet in diameter and 3 feet long counts as one heavy trash item (two items a month) indy.gov[1] indy.gov[2].

Never rake leaves into the street, and don't burn them — burning in Marion County can mean fines up to $2,500 indy.gov[3].

Option 1: Fall leaf collection (40 bags a week)

The City's 2026 leaf collection season begins November 9 and ends December 4 indy.gov[3]. During that window you can dispose of 40 bags of leaves each week with your regular trash indy.gov[3] indy.gov[1].

How to set leaves out

  1. Bag them. Use large plastic trash bags. The City asks residents not to use paper bags because they fall apart in wet weather indy.gov[3].
  2. Place bags on the street by 7 a.m. on your collection day indy.gov[3].
  3. Keep leaf bags 3–5 feet from your cart collection area and out of sidewalks and public rights-of-way indy.gov[3].
  4. Never rake leaves into the street. Clear nearby storm drain inlets to help prevent flooding indy.gov[3].

Find your regular trash day with the City's trash day lookup or call the Mayor's Action Center at 317-327-4622 indy.gov[2] indy.gov[6].

Thanksgiving week 2026

There is no trash, heavy trash, leaf service or curbside recycling on Thursday, November 26 (Thanksgiving). Thursday routes run Friday, November 27, and Friday routes run Saturday, November 28 indy.gov[3]. Tuesday, November 10 (Veterans Day) is not a solid-waste holiday; routes run as normal indy.gov[3].

Option 2: Year-round yard waste in the trash cart

Outside leaf season — and for small amounts any time — you can put bagged or bundled yard waste in the 96-gallon trash cart if it fits with the lid closed. That includes small twigs, leaves and grass indy.gov[1].

Rules that trip people up indy.gov[1] indy.gov[3]:

  • Bags outside the cart will not be picked up after the leaf window.
  • Leaves should never be placed loose inside the cart.
  • Loose yard waste or yard waste that doesn't fit in the cart is on the City's "cannot go in your trash cart" list.

On pickup day the cart must be at the curb before 7 a.m., two to three feet from the curb with arrows pointing to the street or alley, and at least five feet from objects and buildings indy.gov[2].

Option 3: Bundled limbs as heavy trash

Yard waste that's too big for the cart can count as heavy trash. The City lists bundled limbs 3 feet in diameter and 3 feet long as one heavy trash item indy.gov[1]. You may set out up to two heavy or bulky items on your monthly heavy trash day, and the limit is two a month indy.gov[2].

Put heavy items at the curb next to your trash cart on that day; collection starts at 7 a.m. indy.gov[2]. Look up your heavy trash day on the trash day tool indy.gov[2]. For the full heavy trash list (furniture, mattresses, appliances without Freon, and more), see our Indianapolis heavy trash guide.

Missed pickup

Don't report a miss the same day. Crews have until 6 p.m. Report the next business day to the Mayor's Action Center at 317-327-4622 and allow three business days indy.gov[6].

Option 4: Drop-off and composting

Citizens' Transfer Station

2324 S. Belmont Ave. — Saturdays 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. (closed the Saturdays closest to Christmas and New Year's) indy.gov[4].

  • Takes bags of leaves, extra household trash and heavy trash indy.gov[4] indy.gov[1].
  • $2 per carload or $5 per SUV or pickup truckload, cash only; up to two trips a day indy.gov[4].
  • Moving trucks, dual axles, trailers, haulers, stake-bed trucks and loads larger than 3/4 ton aren't allowed indy.gov[4].
  • Leaves dropped here go to the landfill, not compost indy.gov[3].

GreenCycle (compost)

The City says residents can take brush, logs, leaves, grass and mixed yard waste to GreenCycle at several Indy-area locations for a fee; those materials are turned into mulch, compost and soil blends indy.gov[5] indy.gov[1]. For leaves, the City lists $35 for pickup-truck drivers and $80 for pickups with a trailer; plastic bags are not accepted, and leaves brought there are composted indy.gov[3]. Check current site hours and rates before you go (the City points you to GreenCycle's own rates page) indy.gov[5].

South Side Landfill

2561 Kentucky Ave., phone 317-247-6808. Hours listed on Trash 101: Monday–Friday 5 a.m.–7 p.m., Saturday 5 a.m.–3 p.m., closed Sunday indy.gov[1]. Resident leaf drop-off is composted and used as daily cover; pricing is by volume on their site indy.gov[3] indy.gov[5].

Home compost / mow in place

The City also suggests home composting and mowing leaves in place (mower height around 3 inches) so they break down on the lawn indy.gov[3] indy.gov[5]. See the City's Composting page for what to compost and local subscription options indy.gov[5].

If you need more hauled than the City will take

For a storm cleanup, a full yard of brush beyond two heavy-trash bundles, or a load that won't fit at the transfer station, the City's usual advice for oversized waste is a dumpster or a responsible hauler — same idea as other bulk cleanouts. Don't burn or dump leaves

Burning leaves is illegal in Marion County and may result in fines up to $2,500 indy.gov[3]. Raking leaves into the street is also prohibited indy.gov[3].

Frequently asked questions

When is Indianapolis leaf collection in 2026?
The City's 2026 leaf collection season runs from November 9 through December 4. During that window you can dispose of 40 bags of leaves each week with your regular trash. indy.gov[3]
How many bags of leaves can I put out in Indianapolis?
During leaf collection season, 40 bags a week. Outside that window, only bags that fit inside your trash cart with the lid closed are picked up; bags left outside the cart are not. indy.gov[3] indy.gov[1]
Can I put yard waste in my Indianapolis trash cart?
Yes for small amounts that are bagged or bundled and fit with the lid closed (small twigs, leaves, grass). Loose yard waste or yard waste that doesn't fit inside the cart cannot go in the cart. indy.gov[1]
Does Indianapolis pick up brush and limbs?
Bundled limbs that are 3 feet in diameter and 3 feet long count as one heavy trash item. You get up to two heavy or bulky items a month on your assigned heavy trash day. indy.gov[1] indy.gov[2]
Where can I take leaves and yard waste in Indianapolis?
Citizens' Transfer Station at 2324 S. Belmont Ave. (Saturdays 8 a.m.–4 p.m., $2 carload or $5 SUV/pickup, cash) takes bags of leaves, but those go to the landfill, not compost. GreenCycle sites around Indy compost leaves and accept brush, logs, grass and mixed yard waste for a fee; the City lists $35 for a pickup and $80 with a trailer for leaf drop-off, and plastic bags are not accepted. South Side Landfill at 2561 Kentucky Ave. also takes resident leaf drop-off (composted; volume pricing). indy.gov[4] indy.gov[3] indy.gov[5] indy.gov[1]
Is it legal to burn leaves in Indianapolis?
No. Burning leaves is illegal in Marion County and may result in fines up to $2,500. indy.gov[3]
