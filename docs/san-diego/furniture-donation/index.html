The short version

In San Diego, donating is often the cheapest way to get rid of good furniture, because the City has no bulky item pickup right now. Its planned bulky pickup is paused until further notice sandiego.gov (Environmental Services)[10], and couches, chairs and anything over 50 pounds can never go in a City bin sandiego.gov (Environmental Services)[11].

Two local charities will pick up for free:

  1. Father Joe's Villages: free pickup on a scheduled day, from outside your home only Father Joe's Villages (my.neighbor.org)[1].
  2. Veterans Thrift Store: free pickups across San Diego County Veterans Thrift Store (veteransthriftstores.com)[3].

You can also drop off at Goodwill stores and Salvation Army thrift stores and donation trailers Goodwill Industries of San Diego County[5] The Salvation Army San Diego Adult Rehabilitation Center[8]. Habitat for Humanity's ReStores are closed San Diego Habitat for Humanity[9].

Every charity here wants furniture that's clean, complete and sellable, and none of them take mattresses.

Father Joe's Villages (free pickup)

Father Joe's says its donation pickup service is free. Drivers carry envelopes if you'd like to give cash toward gas Father Joe's Villages (my.neighbor.org)[1].

  • Book: call 619-446-2100 (1-619-HOMELESS) or fill out the pickup form on its Donate Goods page. An operator calls to set a date Father Joe's Villages (my.neighbor.org)[1].
  • Timing: drivers pick up between 8 a.m. and 4 p.m. You get a day, not a time. Ask for a call-ahead and the driver will phone about 30 minutes before arriving Father Joe's Villages (my.neighbor.org)[1].
  • You don't have to be home. You can leave donations outside Father Joe's Villages (my.neighbor.org)[1].
  • Entry level only: drivers can't move furniture up or down stairs. Everything must be outside in an accessible spot on the entry level of your home Father Joe's Villages (my.neighbor.org)[1].
  • Two-person rule: any furniture that no more than two people can carry, including couches, love seats, armchairs, dining sets, tables, dressers, nightstands, headboards and entertainment centers Father Joe's Villages (my.neighbor.org)[1].
  • Condition: gently used or new. Nothing torn, stained, broken or needing repair Father Joe's Villages (my.neighbor.org)[1].
  • Bag and box small items. Loose items that aren't boxed or bagged won't be picked up Father Joe's Villages (my.neighbor.org)[1].

Not accepted (partial list): beds, futons, mattresses and box springs, conference tables, credenzas, cribs, large wooden desks, large one-piece wall units, kitchen cabinets, pianos and pool tables Father Joe's Villages (my.neighbor.org)[1].

In a hurry? Father Joe's also offers a Priority Pickup through ReSupply for a fee, within 72 hours, from anywhere in the home, with disassembly included (except doors, cabinets and lighting) Father Joe's Villages (my.neighbor.org)[1].

Drop-off

Father Joe's takes donations at its 815 33rd St. warehouse (Monday to Friday 9:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Saturday 9:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.) and at its Hillcrest (1457 University Ave.), Imperial Beach (1555 Palm Ave., Suite F) and Chula Vista (405 Broadway) thrift stores. Call ahead to confirm they have room Father Joe's Villages (my.neighbor.org)[1] Father Joe's Villages (my.neighbor.org)[2].

Veterans Thrift Store (free pickup)

Veterans Thrift Store (veteransthriftstores.com)[3] Veterans Thrift Store (veteransthriftstores.com)[4]

  • Cost: "Free Pickups-No Charge," and donations are tax deductible.
  • Area: San Diego County, including North County, Ramona, Oceanside, Vista, San Marcos, Escondido, Camp Pendleton, Fallbrook and Bonsall.
  • Book: online form, or call 619-337-9244 or 800-894-2486.
  • Not accepted: mattresses, paint, chemicals, solvents, cleaning products and toxic waste.
  • Stores: Spring Valley (1049 Elkelton Blvd.), El Cajon (1130 E. Main St.), Chula Vista (881 Broadway) and Oceanside (1624 S. Coast Hwy.).

Its pages don't list furniture size or stair limits, so describe your pieces and where they are when you book.

Goodwill Industries of San Diego County (drop-off)

  • Where: large furniture is accepted at Goodwill's retail stores and donation storefronts. All locations take smaller pieces such as chairs and side tables Goodwill Industries of San Diego County[5] Goodwill Industries of San Diego County[6].
  • Condition: clean, complete, structurally sound and sellable. Disassembled furniture is fine if all the parts are there and it's free of major damage, stains, cracks or excessive wear Goodwill Industries of San Diego County[6].
  • Not accepted: mattresses, and ripped, stained or unsellable furniture Goodwill Industries of San Diego County[5].
  • Questions: 619-225-2200, ext. 197, or donate@sdgoodwill.org, Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Goodwill Industries of San Diego County[5]

The Salvation Army (drop-off)

The Salvation Army's San Diego Adult Rehabilitation Center accepts small furniture and upholstered items that are gently used The Salvation Army San Diego Adult Rehabilitation Center[7].

Not accepted: damaged furniture; sectional sofas; hide-a-beds; entertainment centers, china cabinets and armoires; mattresses, box springs and bed frames; and large office furniture The Salvation Army San Diego Adult Rehabilitation Center[7].

Drop off at its thrift stores, such as 3240 Sports Arena Blvd. (619-758-1716) or 6875 El Cajon Blvd. in the College Area (619-466-0489), or at its donation trailers at the Park & Ride at 16785 W. Bernardo Drive and at I-805 and Governor Drive, open 9:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., seven days a week The Salvation Army San Diego Adult Rehabilitation Center[8]. Questions: 1-800-728-7825 The Salvation Army San Diego Adult Rehabilitation Center[7].

Habitat for Humanity ReStore: closed

San Diego Habitat for Humanity closed both of its ReStore locations effective October 31, 2024 San Diego Habitat for Humanity[9], so it no longer takes furniture.

What about mattresses?

None of the charities above take them Father Joe's Villages (my.neighbor.org)[1] Veterans Thrift Store (veteransthriftstores.com)[3] Goodwill Industries of San Diego County[5] The Salvation Army San Diego Adult Rehabilitation Center[7]. The City runs a free mattress drop-off at Miramar Landfill; see our San Diego bulky item and junk disposal guide for details.

If no charity will take it

  • Don't put it in your bin. The City lists couches, chairs, major appliances and items over 50 pounds among the things that can never go in any City bin sandiego.gov (Environmental Services)[11].
  • Don't leave it on the street. Leaving furniture on a public road, right-of-way or someone else's property without permission is illegal dumping under San Diego Municipal Code section 54.0209 sandiego.gov (Environmental Services)[12].
  • Haul it to Miramar Landfill yourself. A car, SUV, minivan, pickup or van (Tier 1) of City general refuse costs $75 a load, or $86 from outside the City. Loads must be covered, or you pay an extra $50 sandiego.gov (Environmental Services)[13].
