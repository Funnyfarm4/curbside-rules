How to Get Rid of a Mattress in NYC: DSNY Rules (2026)

DSNY picks up mattresses free at the curb, but only sealed in a plastic bag and set out the night before trash day. Bag rules, fines, and what to do instead.

The short version

New York City will take your old mattress or box spring for free, right from the curb, but only if you follow two rules: seal it in a plastic bag, and put it out on the right night NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1]. There's no appointment to book. DSNY stopped offering pick-up appointments for large items nyc.gov (DSNY)[4].

Get either rule wrong and the mattress stays where it is, and you may get a ticket NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1] nyc.gov (DSNY)[3].

Option 1: Free DSNY curbside collection

DSNY gives NYC residents free curbside removal of large items, and mattresses and box springs are collected with your trash, not your recycling nyc.gov (DSNY)[2]. This service is for residential buildings NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[5].

  • Cost: free nyc.gov (DSNY)[2].
  • Appointment: none. Appointments are no longer offered and can't be scheduled nyc.gov (DSNY)[4].
  • Limit: up to 6 bulk items per collection day. If you're clearing out a lot, spread it over several collection days NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1] NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[5].
  • Also accepted the same way: futons. NYC311 lists mattresses, box springs and futons together as items you can put out with your trash NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1].

Find your night

  1. Look up your address in DSNY's collection schedule tool nyc.gov (DSNY)[7] NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[6].
  2. Find your trash (bulk or "large item") collection day. Mattresses go out the night before that day, not before your recycling day NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1] nyc.gov (DSNY)[2].
  3. Check for holidays. Collection can change when your day falls on a Sanitation holiday. The rest of 2026's list includes Election Day (Tuesday, November 3), Veterans Day (Wednesday, November 11), Thanksgiving (Thursday, November 26) and Christmas (Friday, December 25) NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1].

How to prepare it

  1. Bag it completely. Any mattress or box spring set out for collection must be fully contained in a plastic bag that's securely sealed, to stop the spread of bed bugs nyc.gov (DSNY)[3]. DSNY doesn't collect uncovered or partly covered mattresses NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1].
  2. Use a clear bag. DSNY's collection laws say the bag must be clear nyc.gov (DSNY)[3], while NYC311 says any color except red or orange NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1]. A clear bag meets both.
  3. Buy the bag yourself. The City doesn't provide mattress bags or covers. You can get them at department stores, home improvement stores and moving supply centers NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1].
  4. Set it out between 6 p.m. and midnight the night before collection NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1]. All waste has to be out by midnight NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[6].
  5. Put it in the right spot. Place it at the curb or in your alley if that's your collection area. Don't put it in the street, in front of another property, or where it blocks people walking. Items left on private property are "unserviceable" and won't be picked up NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[5].

The bed frame

Bed frames are bulk items too. A frame that's mostly metal or plastic goes out the night before your recycling day. A wood frame goes out the night before your trash day. If it's in pieces, tie them in bundles no larger than 2 by 4 feet NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1]. If a frame or other bulk item has bed bugs, attach a sign saying so, so nobody takes it NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1] NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[5].

Fines

If you set out a mattress or box spring the wrong way, DSNY's collection laws list these fines nyc.gov (DSNY)[3]:

  • $50 for the first offense
  • $100 for the second offense
  • $200 for the third and later offenses

Fines can increase with each violation within 12 months nyc.gov (DSNY)[3]. DSNY's furniture page puts the top fine for incorrect mattress disposal at up to $300 nyc.gov (DSNY)[2].

Missed pickup or a dumped mattress

  • Not collected? Report it on NYC311's missed collection page. First make sure it was bagged and out on the right night, because unbagged mattresses aren't collected NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1].
  • Neighbor's unbagged mattress at the curb? NYC311 has a Trash or Recycling Disposal Complaint page for that, and an Illegal Dumping page for mattresses dumped on the street NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1].
  • Wrong schedule for your address? Call 311 or 212-NEW-YORK (212-639-9675) NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[6].

Option 2: When DSNY pickup won't work

DSNY's free bulk collection is for residential buildings. NYC311 points non-residential buildings to its separate "Waste Disposal for Businesses" rules NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[5]. If you're a business, or you can't get the mattress bagged and down to the curb on the right night, a paid junk-removal crew can carry it out for you.

Frequently asked questions

Can I just leave a mattress on the curb in NYC?
Only if it's sealed in a plastic bag and you put it out between 6 p.m. and midnight the night before your bulk trash collection day. Uncovered mattresses aren't collected, and you can be fined. NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1]
Does DSNY charge to pick up a mattress?
No. NYC residents get free curbside removal of large items, including mattresses and box springs, from residential buildings. nyc.gov (DSNY)[2] NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[5]
Do I need to schedule a mattress pickup in NYC?
No, and you can't. DSNY says pick-up appointments for large items are no longer offered and cannot be scheduled. You just set it out on the right night. nyc.gov (DSNY)[4]
What kind of bag do I need for a mattress in NYC?
A plastic bag that fully contains the mattress or box spring and is securely sealed. DSNY's collection laws say the bag must be clear. NYC311 says any color except red or orange. A clear bag satisfies both. The City doesn't hand out bags; they're sold at department stores, home improvement stores and moving supply centers. nyc.gov (DSNY)[3] NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1]
What is the fine for not bagging a mattress in NYC?
DSNY's collection laws list $50 for a first offense, $100 for a second and $200 for the third and later offenses. DSNY's furniture page says fines can go up to $300. nyc.gov (DSNY)[3] nyc.gov (DSNY)[2]
How do I throw out a bed frame in NYC?
Bed frames are bulk items. Put a mostly metal or plastic frame out the night before your recycling day, and a wood frame the night before your trash day. Tie loose pieces into bundles no larger than 2 by 4 feet. NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1]
What if my mattress has bed bugs?
Seal it in a plastic bag like any other mattress. For other bulk items with bed bugs, such as a bed frame, NYC311 says you must attach a sign saying they have bed bugs so no one takes them. NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[1] NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[5]
