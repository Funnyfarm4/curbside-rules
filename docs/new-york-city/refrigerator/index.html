How to Get Rid of a Refrigerator in New York City (2026)

NYC fridges, freezers and ACs need a free DSNY CFC (Freon) removal appointment first. How to book, set-out rules, the R600a catch, and washers and stoves.

The short version

In New York City, a refrigerator can't just go out with the trash. Appliances that cool or chill contain CFC gas (Freon), and trained DSNY staff must remove it before the appliance can be collected and recycled nyc.gov (DSNY)[3]. That service is free for residents, but you must make an appointment nyc.gov (DSNY)[1].

On the appointment day, DSNY removes the CFC and puts a tag with a six-digit number on the appliance. Sanitation workers then pick up tagged items on your next recycling day. Only tagged appliances are collected nyc.gov (DSNY)[1].

Which appliances need an appointment

  • Refrigerators and freezers with non-flammable Freon
  • Air conditioners
  • Dehumidifiers
  • Water or wine coolers NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[2]

You can book an appointment for a residential address or a non-profit institution serviced by DSNY NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[2].

How to book and set it out

  1. Schedule online yourself or through 311 (212-NEW-YORK, 212-639-9675). DSNY doesn't do same-day appointments. You can book up to 10 items per appointment. Keep your appointment number NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[2]. Appointments can't be made anonymously nyc.gov (DSNY)[1].
  2. Remove the doors, hinges and locks from refrigerators and freezers. This protects children and animals NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[2] nyc.gov (DSNY)[1].
  3. Put it at the curb between 6 p.m. and midnight the evening before your appointment, with the back facing the street NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[2].
  4. DSNY removes the CFC on the appointment day and tags the appliance NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[2].
  5. Collection workers pick it up on your next recycling day NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[2].

Got more items after booking, or someone already booked your preferred date? You need a separate appointment on a different date NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[2].

Cancel or reschedule

Use your service request number to cancel or reschedule, up to 24 hours before the appointment. If the appliance is already outside, bring it back in. If you don't cancel and DSNY can't find it, the appointment is closed as "Not On Location" NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[2].

When DSNY won't take it

  • Flammable refrigerant (R600a or R32). Some newer appliances use it. Look for a warning sticker with a yellow triangle and black flames. DSNY tags these with a red sticker and won't collect them. Contact the manufacturer or a private carter nyc.gov (DSNY)[1] NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[2].
  • Oversized or commercial-size appliances won't fit in the garbage truck. You must hire a private removal service or carter NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[2].
  • Businesses must use a private carter. Appointments made by a business may result in a Notice of Violation nyc.gov (DSNY)[1].
  • Soda machines need a special request by phone through 311 NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[2].

Want it hauled away?

DSNY won't take a fridge with R600a or R32 refrigerant, an oversized or commercial unit, or anything from a business, and you still have to get the appliance down to the curb yourself with the doors off. A junk-removal crew can carry it out of the apartment for you. Confirm they serve your address and check the price before you book.

Washers, dryers, stoves and other big appliances

Dishwashers, microwaves, ovens and ranges, washers and dryers, and water heaters don't need a CFC appointment. They follow the large (bulk) item rules nyc.gov (DSNY)[3]:

  • Items that are mostly metal or plastic go out with your recycling.
  • Other non-recyclable large items go out with trash, on your trash-only day, not your recycling day.
  • You can set out up to 6 large items at the curb per collection day.

Small appliances like blenders, coffee makers, toaster ovens and corded vacuums go out with recycling if they're mostly metal or plastic. Take rechargeable batteries out of cordless tools and handheld vacuums first nyc.gov (DSNY)[3].

Frequently asked questions

Can I put a refrigerator out with the trash in NYC?
No. You must make a CFC removal appointment first. DSNY removes the Freon, tags the fridge, and collects only tagged appliances on your next recycling day. nyc.gov (DSNY)[1]
How much does DSNY charge to pick up a refrigerator?
Nothing. DSNY provides free curbside CFC removal for residents, except for appliances with R600a or R32 refrigerant. nyc.gov (DSNY)[1] nyc.gov (DSNY)[3]
Do I have to take the doors off my fridge?
Yes. NYC311 says to remove the doors, hinges and locks from refrigerators and freezers, and 311 takes complaints about fridges set out with doors still attached. NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[2]
What if my fridge has a flammable refrigerant sticker?
DSNY can't collect appliances with R600a or R32 refrigerant (yellow triangle with black flames). If you set one out, DSNY tags it with a red sticker and leaves it. Contact the manufacturer or a private carter or waste removal vendor. nyc.gov (DSNY)[1] NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[2]
How do I cancel or reschedule a CFC appointment?
You need your service request number, and you can cancel or reschedule up to 24 hours before the appointment, online or through 311. If the appliance is already at the curb, bring it back inside. NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[2]
Can a business use DSNY's fridge pickup?
No. Stores and other commercial locations must hire a private carter, and appointments made by a business may result in a Notice of Violation. nyc.gov (DSNY)[1] NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov)[2]
