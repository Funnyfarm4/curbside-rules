How to Get Rid of a Mattress in Nashville (2026)

In Nashville, the Sheriff's Office picks up mattresses free at the curb (call 615-880-3897), or drop one at a Metro Convenience Center for $12.

The short version

You have two official ways to get rid of a mattress in Nashville:

  1. Free curbside pickup from the Davidson County Sheriff's Office. Mattresses and box springs are on its list of items it removes Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[1]. Call 615-880-3897 to schedule Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[2].
  2. Drop it off at a Metro Convenience Center for $12 per mattress or box spring nashville.gov (Nashville Department of Waste Services)[4].

For anything too bulky to fit in your curbside cart, Metro points residents to its Convenience Centers nashville.gov (Nashville Department of Waste Services)[3].

Option 1: Free Sheriff's Office pickup

  1. Call 615-880-3897. You'll be asked for the items you're setting out, the address, a phone number and the resident's name. The homeowner or tenant has to make the request Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[1] Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[2].
  2. Put it at the front curb by 6:30 a.m. on your date. Front curb only Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[1].
  3. Don't bag it. The Sheriff's Office doesn't take anything in bags Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[1].
  4. Give it time. Crews have up to seven business days from your date, not counting weekends and holidays Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[1].

Apartment, condo or townhome? Crews can't come onto private complexes, private roads, gated communities or business property. Put it at the front curb by the main entrance instead Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[1].

After an eviction: the Sheriff's Office says it's prohibited by law from removing bulk items that resulted from an eviction Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[1].

For everything else the Sheriff's crews take, see our Nashville bulk trash pickup guide.

Option 2: Drop it off ($12 each)

Metro takes mattresses and box springs at three of its four Convenience Centers, for $12 each, up to four a month nashville.gov (Nashville Department of Waste Services)[4] nashville.gov (Nashville Department of Waste Services)[3]. All are open Tuesday to Saturday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. and closed on Metro holidays nashville.gov (Nashville Department of Waste Services)[5]:

  • East: 943A Doctor Richard G. Adams Dr, Nashville, TN 37207
  • Ezell Pike: 3254 Ezell Pk, Nashville, TN 37211
  • Omohundro: 1019 Omohundro Pl, Nashville, TN 37210

The Anderson Lane center in Madison isn't listed for mattresses nashville.gov (Nashville Department of Waste Services)[4].

Before you go nashville.gov (Nashville Department of Waste Services)[4]:

  • Bring a Tennessee driver's license showing you live in Davidson County.
  • You unload it yourself.
  • No commercial vehicles, including any vehicle with company advertising or commercial tags.
  • The free daily bulk item doesn't cover mattresses, so expect to pay.
  • Cash (not at Omohundro), check or card; cards add 2.8%, minimum $1.95.

Donate it if it's still good

Metro asks residents to consider donating furniture to secondhand stores or offering it online before sending it to the landfill nashville.gov (Nashville Department of Waste Services)[3]. Many charities won't take used mattresses, so call first.

Frequently asked questions

Will Nashville pick up an old mattress?
Yes, through the Davidson County Sheriff's Office, which lists mattresses and box springs among the items it removes free from the front curb. Call 615-880-3897 to get a date. Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[1] Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[2]
How long does Sheriff's mattress pickup take in Nashville?
Put the mattress out by 6:30 a.m. on the date you're given. Crews have up to seven business days from that date to pick it up, not counting weekends and holidays. Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[1]
Can I bag or wrap a mattress for Sheriff's pickup?
The Sheriff's Office says it takes nothing in bags, so leave the mattress unbagged at the curb. Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[1]
How much does it cost to drop off a mattress in Nashville?
$12 per mattress or box spring at the East, Ezell Pike or Omohundro Convenience Centers, with a limit of four a month. Card payments carry a 2.8% fee with a $1.95 minimum, and Omohundro doesn't take cash. nashville.gov (Nashville Department of Waste Services)[4]
Can I use the free bulk item at the Convenience Center for a mattress?
No. The free daily allowance of one bulk item and three bags of trash specifically excludes mattresses. nashville.gov (Nashville Department of Waste Services)[4]
I live in an apartment. Can the Sheriff's Office take my mattress?
Crews aren't allowed on private property such as apartment, condo or townhome complexes. The Sheriff's Office asks residents of those places to put items at the front curb by the main entrance. Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[1]
