The short version

Three Nashville organizations will send a truck for good furniture: Habitat ReStore and Goodwill Middle Tennessee both offer free pickup, and ThriftSmart takes free large-item pickup requests Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville[1] Goodwill Industries of Middle Tennessee[3] ThriftSmart[5]. The catch: free pickups are ground-floor only, can take up to two weeks, and the furniture has to be clean, undamaged and sellable Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville[1] Goodwill Industries of Middle Tennessee[3] ThriftSmart[5].

Metro itself says to consider donating furniture before sending it to the landfill nashville.gov (Nashville Department of Waste Services)[6]. If no one wants it, Metro's convenience centers and the Sheriff's curbside pickup are the fallback nashville.gov (Nashville Department of Waste Services)[7] Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[9].

Habitat for Humanity ReStore (free pickup)

Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville offers free pickup for large or heavy items located on the ground floor. Items must be disassembled, and the donation ambassador has final say on what's accepted Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville[1].

  • Book in Davidson County online or by calling the Nashville donation hotline at 615-942-1290 Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville[1].
  • Timing: scheduling depends on demand, but pickups generally happen within two weeks Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville[1].
  • Priority paid pickup: through Habitat's partner ReSupply, for items that need to go within 48 hours, need disassembly, or aren't on the first floor. ReSupply charges a fee that depends on the number of items Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville[1].
  • Furniture it takes: living room, dining room, bedroom and outdoor/patio furniture; complete bed frames (headboard, footboard, side rails and hardware); office furniture such as file cabinets, credenzas and office chairs Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville[1].
  • Appliances: 7 years old or newer, clean and fully working with all parts; no gas appliances or dishwashers Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville[1].
  • Won't take: cribs, highchairs and other infant items; headboards on their own; cubicles and office systems; and mattresses or box springs unless new in the packaging Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville[1].

Drop off at the Nashville ReStore, 414 Harding Place, Ste 110, Nashville, TN 37211 (615-942-1291), Tuesday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville[2].

Goodwill Industries of Middle Tennessee (free pickup with a minimum)

Goodwill offers free pickup from your home or business if you have a minimum of 5 boxes or bags of home goods and clothing and/or 3-4 acceptable furniture pieces Goodwill Industries of Middle Tennessee[3].

  • Schedule online or call 615-425-0100 Goodwill Industries of Middle Tennessee[3].
  • Service area: parts of Davidson, Williamson, Rutherford, Sumner, Wilson, Montgomery and Madison counties Goodwill Industries of Middle Tennessee[3].
  • Condition: gently used or working, with no damage, breaks, tears, stains or pet hair Goodwill Industries of Middle Tennessee[3].
  • Moving or estate? Goodwill also runs moving pickups and free estate pickups for families settling an estate or downsizing Goodwill Industries of Middle Tennessee[3].
  • Won't take Goodwill Industries of Middle Tennessee[4]: mattresses, box springs, sleeper sofas and futon cushions; broken, damaged or stained furniture; cribs and other baby furniture; large corporate-style furniture (L- and U-shaped desks, conference tables, cubicles); hospital beds and waterbeds; pianos; standard-size pool tables; and hot tubs.

Goodwill says furniture acceptance at its drop-off centers depends on local space and needs, so call your nearest donation center before hauling a piece in Goodwill Industries of Middle Tennessee[4].

ThriftSmart (free large-item pickup)

ThriftSmart takes free large item or bulk donation pickup requests through a form on its donation page and says it will reach out within 24 hours to schedule ThriftSmart[5].

  • Furniture it typically takes: couches, sectionals, recliners, futons, dressers, chests, armoires, dining tables and chairs, bookshelves, coffee and end tables, complete beds, mattresses and box springs, cribs (not drop-side), rugs and outdoor furniture ThriftSmart[5].
  • Won't take: sleeper sofas, large office desks and conference tables, L-shaped desks, pianos, TV armoires and large entertainment centers, hospital beds, gas appliances and dishwashers ThriftSmart[5].
  • Pickup rules: everything must be on the ground floor, no more than five steps into the home (apartment buildings need an elevator that fits everything), donations should be packed in bags or boxes before the driver arrives, and beds must be fully disassembled with hardware bagged. Drivers can refuse anything broken, stained, ripped, smelly or covered in pet fur ThriftSmart[5].
  • Drop off at the Tusculum store, 4890 Nolensville Road, Nashville 37211, or Rivergate, 2117 Gallatin Pk N, Madison 37115: Monday to Saturday 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., Sunday noon to 6 p.m. ThriftSmart[5].

If no charity will take it: the Metro options

Free convenience center drop-off

Davidson County residents can bring 3 bags of trash and 1 bulk item a day free to any of Metro's four convenience centers (mattresses are $12 each). Bring a Tennessee driver's license showing a Davidson County address, and plan to unload it yourself nashville.gov (Nashville Department of Waste Services)[7].

  • Anderson Lane, 939A Anderson Ln, Madison 37115
  • East, 943A Doctor Richard G. Adams Dr, Nashville 37207
  • Ezell Pike, 3254 Ezell Pk, Nashville 37211
  • Omohundro, 1019 Omohundro Pl, Nashville 37210
  • Hours: Tuesday to Saturday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.; closed Metro holidays nashville.gov (Nashville Department of Waste Services)[8]

Sheriff's curbside bulk pickup

The Davidson County Sheriff's Office removes old furniture, sofas and couches, lawn and baby furniture, mattresses and rugs from the front curb Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[9]. Call 615-880-3897 Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[10], set items out by 6:30 a.m., and allow up to seven business days from your date. Nothing in bags, and no glass or mirrored pieces Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[9]. See our Nashville couch guide for the full rules.

Frequently asked questions

Who picks up donated furniture for free in Nashville?
Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville's ReStore offers free pickup of large or heavy items on the ground floor (call 615-942-1290 in Davidson County). Goodwill Industries of Middle Tennessee picks up free if you have at least 3-4 acceptable furniture pieces or 5 boxes or bags (615-425-0100). ThriftSmart also takes free large-item pickup requests online. Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville[1] Goodwill Industries of Middle Tennessee[3] ThriftSmart[5]
Will a charity pick up furniture from upstairs in Nashville?
Not for free. Habitat's free pickup is ground-floor only and items must be disassembled, and ThriftSmart's items must be on the ground floor no more than five steps in, or in an apartment building with an elevator. Habitat's paid priority pickup through ReSupply handles items that aren't on the first floor or need disassembly. Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville[1] ThriftSmart[5]
Where can I donate a used mattress in Nashville?
Goodwill Middle Tennessee doesn't accept mattresses, box springs, sleeper sofas or futon cushions, and Habitat ReStore takes mattresses only if they're new in the packaging. ThriftSmart lists mattresses and box springs among the furniture it typically accepts. Otherwise, Metro convenience centers take mattresses for $12 each. Goodwill Industries of Middle Tennessee[4] Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville[1] ThriftSmart[5] nashville.gov (Nashville Department of Waste Services)[7]
How long does Habitat ReStore pickup take in Nashville?
Habitat says scheduling depends on the volume of requests but pickups generally happen within two weeks. Its paid priority pickup through ReSupply is for items that need to go within 48 hours. Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville[1]
Can I donate a crib or baby furniture in Nashville?
Habitat ReStore doesn't accept cribs, highchairs or other infant items, and Goodwill Middle Tennessee doesn't accept cribs, car seats, high chairs or potty chairs. ThriftSmart lists baby cribs as typically accepted but not drop-side cribs. Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville[1] Goodwill Industries of Middle Tennessee[4] ThriftSmart[5]
What if no charity will take my furniture in Nashville?
Davidson County residents can bring one bulk item a day free to any of Metro's four convenience centers, Tuesday to Saturday from 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. The Davidson County Sheriff's Office also removes old furniture and sofas from the front curb; call 615-880-3897. nashville.gov (Nashville Department of Waste Services)[7] nashville.gov (Nashville Department of Waste Services)[8] Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[9] Davidson County Sheriff's Office (sheriff.nashville.gov)[10]
