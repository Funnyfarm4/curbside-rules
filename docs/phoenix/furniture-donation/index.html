The short version

Phoenix has several charities that will come get good furniture. St. Vincent de Paul picks up free across greater Phoenix, and Habitat ReStore, Goodwill and Christian Family Care also schedule pickups St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[3] Habitat for Humanity Central Arizona[4] Goodwill of Central and Northern Arizona[5] Christian Family Care[7]. The catch: free pickups are outside and ground-level only, and the furniture has to be clean, sturdy and sellable St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[1] Goodwill of Central and Northern Arizona[5].

If no charity wants it, the City's bulk trash appointments and free transfer station drop-off are the fallback phoenix.gov (Public Works)[8] phoenix.gov (Public Works)[9].

St. Vincent de Paul (free pickup)

St. Vincent de Paul says pickups are free within greater Phoenix St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[3].

  • Standard pickup (free): curbside, driveway or an open garage with someone home. Drivers don't come inside or go to second floors, and small items must be boxed or bagged St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[1].
  • Book at least 3 days ahead online or at 602-254-3338 (Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.). Call for an earlier date, subject to availability St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[1].
  • Pickup window: weekdays 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., with a 30-minute heads-up call from the driver. No disassembly St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[1].
  • Mattresses: acceptable mattresses and box springs are $50 each, up to 3 St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[1].
  • Priority pickup (paid): from inside the home, starting at $110 depending on size, booked 2 days ahead. Disassembly is available, and they'll go to the third floor or higher only with an elevator St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[1].

What it won't take St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[2]:

  • Flimsy particle-board or pressed-wood furniture
  • Oversized pieces such as desks, armoires, bunk beds, headboards, bed frames, entertainment centers and wall units (a full matching bedroom set is the exception)
  • Anything damaged, ripped, stained, foul-smelling or with pet hair, or missing parts
  • Office furniture, cribs, lift chairs and recliners, pool tables, and non-working appliances
  • Dishwashers, water heaters, spas, toilets and construction materials

Drop-off in Phoenix: Last Chance Thrift Store, 420 W. Watkins Rd. (85003), or Sunnyslope Thrift Store, 8231 N. 7th St. (85020) St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[3].

Habitat for Humanity ReStore (Central Arizona)

Habitat's ReStores take furniture, appliances and building materials in good, clean, working and sellable condition Habitat for Humanity Central Arizona[4].

  • Schedule a pickup through the ReStore donations page or call 623-551-6000, option 1. Appointments fill quickly, and you need to be at the address during the arrival window Habitat for Humanity Central Arizona[4].
  • Furniture it lists: sofas, bed frames, hutches, sectionals, dressers, TV stands and credenzas in sellable condition, plus clean, undamaged patio furniture Habitat for Humanity Central Arizona[4].
  • Appliances: 8 years old or newer, clean and fully working; heating and cooling units 5 years old or newer Habitat for Humanity Central Arizona[4].
  • Won't take: office furniture (cubicles, conference tables), water beds, water heaters, spas, paint and chemicals Habitat for Humanity Central Arizona[4].
  • Drop off at the Phoenix ReStore (115 E. Watkins St., 85004) or West Phoenix ReStore (4344 W. Indian School Rd., Ste. 120, 85031). The page lists store hours of 9 a.m. to 7 p.m. and donation drop-off hours that differ by location, so call ahead Habitat for Humanity Central Arizona[4].
  • Drivers can decline items they can't retrieve safely or that don't meet the guidelines Habitat for Humanity Central Arizona[4].

Goodwill of Central and Northern Arizona

Goodwill's home pickup for large items runs through its partner ReSupply: you list your items and book online Goodwill of Central and Northern Arizona[6]. Its pickup page doesn't list a price, so check the cost when you book.

  • Takes: sofas, loveseats, sectionals (without a bed), bed frames, chairs, end tables, patio furniture and large appliances like stoves, fridges and washers Goodwill of Central and Northern Arizona[5].
  • Rules: items must be in good to gently used condition with no large rips or bugs, gathered and boxed or bagged, and at ground level; drivers can't use stairs or elevators Goodwill of Central and Northern Arizona[5].
  • Won't take: any mattress or box spring, including futons and sleeper sofas, or trash and hazardous materials Goodwill of Central and Northern Arizona[5].
  • You can also drop off at any Goodwill store during business hours, which vary by location Goodwill of Central and Northern Arizona[6].

Christian Family Care Thrift Store

If you're in Phoenix and have a larger furniture item in good condition, Christian Family Care will arrange a pickup. Call 602-971-0241 Christian Family Care[7].

  • Drop off at 3121 E. Greenway Rd., Phoenix 85032, Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Christian Family Care[7]
  • Takes furniture and large appliances (washers, dryers, stoves and refrigerators) Christian Family Care[7].
  • Won't take mattresses and box springs, CRT TVs, car seats or toxic materials Christian Family Care[7].

If no charity will take it: the City options

Bulk trash appointment

Phoenix collects bulk trash by appointment only, four times a year per household, booked online or at 602-262-6251 at least 3 business days ahead phoenix.gov (Public Works)[8]. Set items out no more than 7 days before your date and by 5:30 a.m. on the day, within 5 feet of the curb phoenix.gov (Public Works)[8]. The scheduler blocks Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day plus the 7 days after each, so book early in the holiday season phoenix.gov (Public Works)[8].

Furniture is listed as typical bulk trash on the City's page phoenix.gov (Public Works)[8], but our Phoenix couch guide explains a conflict with City Code; confirm with Public Works when you book.

Free transfer station drop-off

Phoenix residential trash customers can drop off up to one ton a month free at either transfer station with a city services (water) bill and photo ID phoenix.gov (Public Works)[9]:

  • 27th Avenue Transfer Station, 3060 S. 27th Ave., Phoenix 85009
  • North Gateway Transfer Station, 30205 N. Black Canyon Hwy., Phoenix 85085
  • Hours: Monday to Friday 5:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., Saturday 6 a.m. to 3 p.m., closed Sunday and City holidays, including Thanksgiving and the day after phoenix.gov (Public Works)[9].
  • Anything over a ton is $55 a ton, and loads must be tied down or you'll pay a $20 penalty phoenix.gov (Public Works)[9].
