How to Get Rid of a Couch in Phoenix (2026)

Getting rid of a couch in Phoenix: bulk trash appointments (call first), free transfer station drop-off, free St. Vincent de Paul and Habitat pickups.

The short version

Phoenix has three free ways to get rid of a couch: a bulk trash appointment (but call first, see below), a free drop-off at a City transfer station, or a free donation pickup if the couch is in good shape phoenix.gov (Public Works)[1] phoenix.gov (Public Works)[3] St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[4].

None of the free options will carry a couch out of your house. If you need that, or need it gone before your next open bulk date, a paid pickup is the fallback.

Option 1: City bulk trash appointment (confirm first)

Phoenix collects bulk trash by appointment only, up to four times a year per household phoenix.gov (Public Works)[1].

Call before you set out a couch. The Public Works Bulk Trash page says furniture is often considered bulk trash phoenix.gov (Public Works)[1], but City Code Section 27-22 lists "all mattresses or furniture" among items not acceptable for normal residential bulk collection Phoenix City Code[2]. Ask Public Works at 602-262-6251 to confirm your couch will be taken when you book.

  • Book with the online tool on the City's Bulk Trash page or by calling 602-262-6251, at least 3 business days ahead and up to a year out phoenix.gov (Public Works)[1].
  • Cancel at least 2 business days ahead and it won't count against your four phoenix.gov (Public Works)[1].
  • Set it out no more than 7 days before your date and by 5:30 a.m. on the day, in a single pile of up to 10 cubic yards on your property, within 5 feet of the curb phoenix.gov (Public Works)[1] Phoenix City Code[2].
  • Holiday blackouts: the scheduler blocks Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day plus the 7 days after each phoenix.gov (Public Works)[1].

Option 2: Haul it yourself to a transfer station (free up to one ton a month)

Phoenix residential trash customers can drop off up to one ton of items each calendar month at no charge. Show your city services (water) bill and photo ID at the scale house phoenix.gov (Public Works)[3].

  • 27th Avenue Transfer Station, 3060 S. 27th Ave., Phoenix 85009
  • North Gateway Transfer Station, 30205 N. Black Canyon Hwy., Phoenix 85085
  • Hours: Monday to Friday 5:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., Saturday 6 a.m. to 3 p.m., closed Sunday and on City holidays, including Thanksgiving and the day after phoenix.gov (Public Works)[3].
  • Over a ton: $55 a ton, with a cash deposit when you weigh in phoenix.gov (Public Works)[3].
  • Tie it down. Loads must be secured; the unsecured-load penalty is $20 phoenix.gov (Public Works)[3].

A single couch is far under a ton, so this is usually the fastest free option if you have a truck or trailer.

Option 3: Donate it (free pickup if it's in good shape)

St. Vincent de Paul

St. Vincent de Paul says pickups are free within greater Phoenix St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[6].

  • Standard pickup (free): outside only, from the curb, driveway or an open garage with someone home. They don't come inside or go to second floors St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[4].
  • Book at least 3 days ahead online or at 602-254-3338 (Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.). Pickups run weekdays 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., with a 30-minute heads-up call from the driver St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[4].
  • Priority pickup (paid): they'll take items from inside the home, starting at $110, booked 2 days ahead St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[4].
  • Won't take: damaged, ripped, stained, foul-smelling or pet-hair furniture, oversized pieces, and lift chairs or recliners St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[5].
  • Drop-off in Phoenix: Last Chance Thrift Store, 420 W. Watkins Rd. (85003), or Sunnyslope Thrift Store, 8231 N. 7th St. (85020) St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[6].

Habitat for Humanity ReStore (Central Arizona)

Habitat's ReStores accept furniture, including sofas and sectionals, in good, clean, sellable condition Habitat for Humanity Central Arizona[7].

  • Schedule a pickup through the ReStore donations page or call 623-551-6000, option 1. Be at the address during the arrival window Habitat for Humanity Central Arizona[7].
  • Drop off at the Phoenix ReStore (115 E. Watkins St., 85004) or West Phoenix ReStore (4344 W. Indian School Rd., Ste. 120, 85031). Stores are open 9 a.m. to 7 p.m., seven days a week; the page lists different donation drop-off hours in two places, so call ahead Habitat for Humanity Central Arizona[7].
  • Drivers can decline items they can't retrieve safely or that don't meet the guidelines Habitat for Humanity Central Arizona[7].

Frequently asked questions

Will Phoenix bulk trash pick up a couch?
Check first. The Public Works Bulk Trash page names furniture as an example of bulk trash, but City Code Section 27-22 lists "all mattresses or furniture" among items not acceptable for normal residential bulk collection. Call Public Works at 602-262-6251 when you book and confirm a couch will be taken. phoenix.gov (Public Works)[1] Phoenix City Code[2]
Can I take a couch to the dump in Phoenix for free?
Phoenix residential trash customers can drop off up to one ton of items a month free at the 27th Avenue or North Gateway transfer station. Bring your city services (water) bill and a photo ID. Anything over a ton is $55 a ton. phoenix.gov (Public Works)[3]
Who picks up donated couches for free in Phoenix?
St. Vincent de Paul offers a free standard pickup within greater Phoenix from your curb, driveway or open garage, booked at least 3 days ahead online or at 602-254-3338. Will St. Vincent de Paul take a recliner?
No. Its list of items it can't accept includes lift chairs and recliners, along with furniture that is damaged, stained, ripped, foul-smelling or has pet hair. St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[5]
Can someone carry my couch out of the house in Phoenix?
St. Vincent de Paul's Priority Pickup removes items from inside the home for a fee starting at $110, booked 2 days ahead on weekdays. Its free pickup is outside only. A paid junk-removal company is the other option. St. Vincent de Paul of Arizona[4]
How early can I put a couch out for bulk trash in Phoenix?
Only after you've booked an appointment, and no more than 7 days before it. Have it out by 5:30 a.m. on the day, on your property within 5 feet of the curb. phoenix.gov (Public Works)[1] Phoenix City Code[2]
