{{site_name}} answers one boring, everyday question: **how do I get rid of this big thing in my city?** Mattresses, couches, fridges, TVs, piles of junk. The answers are usually scattered across city websites, apps and PDFs. We put them on one plain page per city and item.

## How we research

- Every rule, fee, schedule and phone number comes from an **official source**: the city's own website, its public works or sanitation department, or a state environmental agency.
- We link each fact to the page it came from and show the date we last checked it.
- If we can't confirm something on an official page, we leave it out instead of guessing.
- We re-check guides regularly, and whenever a city announces a change.

We are an independent site. We are not affiliated with any city, county, state agency or trash hauler. Rules change, so always confirm with your city before you set anything on the curb.

## How we make money

Some guides include a "Book a pickup" box with paid links to junk-removal services. If you book through one of those links we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. Those partners never pay to change what a guide says, and the free city option is always listed first. Read our full [affiliate disclosure](/disclosure/).
