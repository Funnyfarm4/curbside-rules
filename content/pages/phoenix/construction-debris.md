## The short version

Phoenix's appointment-based bulk trash pickup is **not** a way to clear out a remodel. It won't take masonry, roofing or concrete at all, and it caps other construction and demolition material at **25 pounds and 4 feet long** [[phx-bulk]].

Your realistic options are: **haul it to a City transfer station** (one load up to a ton a month is free for Phoenix trash customers) [[phx-ts]], **rent a City dumpster** [[phx-dr]], **donate** what's reusable [[phx-ts]] [[stardust]], or **hire a crew** to load and haul it.

## What bulk trash won't take

The City's bulk trash page tells you **not** to include [[phx-bulk]]:

- **Bricks, concrete, dirt, rocks, asphalt and roofing shingles or tiles**
- **Construction or demolition materials over 25 pounds or over 4 feet long**
- **Glass** such as windows, shower doors, patio doors, mirrors and fluorescent tubes
- More than **2 wooden pallets**
- Metal or car parts over 20 pounds or over 4 feet long
- Paint and other household hazardous waste
- Materials placed in **Bagster bags**
- More than **10 cubic yards** in total

Small, light leftovers that fit the limits can go in a regular bulk pile. Book an appointment online or at **602-262-6251** at least 3 business days ahead; each household gets **4 pickups a year** [[phx-bulk]]. Heads-up: the scheduler blocks Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day plus the 7 days after each [[phx-bulk]]. Full details: [Phoenix bulk trash pickup guide](/phoenix/bulk-trash/).

## Option 1: Haul it to a City transfer station

Phoenix residential trash customers paying full-service trash fees can dispose of **one load of up to 1 ton (2,000 pounds) each calendar month free** [[phx-ts]]. At the scale house, show:

1. A copy of your **city services (water, sewer, trash) bill** showing an active, current account, and
2. A **photo ID** with a name or address matching the bill. No substitutions [[phx-ts]].

**Locations** [[phx-ts]]:

- **27th Avenue Transfer Station,** 3060 S. 27th Ave., Phoenix 85009 (south of Lower Buckeye Rd.)
- **North Gateway Transfer Station,** 30205 N. Black Canyon Hwy., Phoenix 85085 (half a mile northeast of the Dixileta Dr. exit, exit 220)

**Hours:** Monday through Friday 5:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., Saturday 6 a.m. to 3 p.m., closed Sunday [[phx-ts]]. Both are closed on Thanksgiving and the day after, Christmas and New Year's Day, among other holidays [[phx-ts]].

**Fees beyond the free load** [[phx-ts]]:

- Over 1 ton: leave a deposit, weigh out, and pay **$55 a ton** for the extra.
- All other loads: **$55 a ton, $40 minimum.** Cash and cards accepted; no checks.
- **Cash deposits** are required when a vehicle and trailer weigh more than 9,000 pounds for a resident with their water bill (6,000 pounds otherwise). Residential deposits usually run **$65 to $180**, cash only.
- Cover your load: the unsecured-load penalty is **$20**.

> **Asbestos:** no friable asbestos is accepted. Non-friable asbestos waste needs prior approval; call 602-262-6251 [[phx-ts]]. Regulated hazardous waste is prohibited, so leftover paint and chemicals go to the City's household hazardous waste program instead. See [Phoenix hazardous waste and electronics drop-off](/phoenix/hazardous-waste-electronics/).

## Option 2: Rent a dumpster from the City

Phoenix Public Works rents roll-off dumpsters to residents with an **active city services account** that's current with no past-due amount [[phx-dr]].

- **Price:** **$165 per pull** (to pick up and empty it) plus **$55 per ton** at pickup [[phx-dr]]
- **Sizes:** 10 yard (13'x8'x4'), 20 yard (23'x8'x5'), 30 yard (23'x8'x6') or 40 yard (23'x8'x8'); a customer service specialist confirms the size after you request [[phx-dr]]
- **Rental period:** up to **10 days**, with an extra fee beyond that [[phx-dr]]
- You must sign a liability release and service contract before delivery, and **hazardous waste is prohibited** in the bin [[phx-dr]]

Want a private dumpster in the street instead? See [Do you need a dumpster permit in Phoenix?](/phoenix/dumpster-permit/)

## Option 3: Donate reusable materials

**At the transfer station.** Bring gently used construction items to the **27th Ave. Transfer Station** and ask the scale house attendant about the reusable building materials drop-off, collected by nonprofit **Stardust Building Supplies** [[phx-ts]]. Accepted items include [[phx-ts]]:

- Cabinets (sets, singles, doors and drawers) and stone, granite or marble countertops
- Interior, exterior, security and screen doors
- Tile with no grout, new wood flooring, ceiling tile, new sheetrock and new insulation
- Bricks, pavers, shingles, lumber and siding
- Sinks, tubs, toilets, faucets and PVC pipe
- Working light fixtures and ceiling fans; dual-paned windows, blinds, shutters and screens

**Directly to Stardust.** Stardust accepts new, excess or gently used building materials, appliances and furniture in resalable condition, and gives a receipt for tax purposes [[stardust]]. You can drop off at its **Glendale or Mesa** reuse centers six days a week (call ahead for large gifts), or book a **home pickup** in Maricopa County, Monday to Friday, for a **$25 nonrefundable fee** on gifts worth at least **$100**: call **480-964-0177** [[stardust]]. Planning a remodel? Stardust also offers deconstruction services at the same number [[stardust]].

## Don't dump it

The City asks residents to report illegal dumping in alleys, dumpsters, vacant lots and open desert. If it's in progress, call **911** for emergencies or Crime Stop at **602-262-6151**; otherwise call Public Works at **602-262-6251** [[phx-bulk]].
