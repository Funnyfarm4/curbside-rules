## The short version

Phoenix has three free ways to get rid of a couch: a **bulk trash appointment** (but call first, see below), a **free drop-off at a City transfer station**, or a **free donation pickup** if the couch is in good shape [[phx-bulk]] [[phx-ts]] [[svdp-pickup]].

None of the free options will carry a couch out of your house. If you need that, or need it gone before your next open bulk date, a paid pickup is the fallback.

## Option 1: City bulk trash appointment (confirm first)

Phoenix collects bulk trash **by appointment only**, up to **four times a year** per household [[phx-bulk]].

> **Call before you set out a couch.** The Public Works Bulk Trash page says furniture is often considered bulk trash [[phx-bulk]], but City Code Section 27-22 lists "all mattresses or furniture" among items not acceptable for normal residential bulk collection [[phx-code]]. Ask Public Works at **602-262-6251** to confirm your couch will be taken when you book.

- **Book** with the online tool on the City's [Bulk Trash page](https://www.phoenix.gov/administration/departments/publicworks/residential-trash-recycling/bulk-trash-pickup.html) or by calling 602-262-6251, at least **3 business days** ahead and up to a year out [[phx-bulk]].
- **Cancel at least 2 business days ahead** and it won't count against your four [[phx-bulk]].
- **Set it out** no more than 7 days before your date and by 5:30 a.m. on the day, in a single pile of up to 10 cubic yards on your property, within 5 feet of the curb [[phx-bulk]] [[phx-code]].
- **Holiday blackouts:** the scheduler blocks Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day plus the 7 days after each [[phx-bulk]].

Our [Phoenix bulk trash guide](/phoenix/bulk-trash/) has the full set-out rules and banned items.

## Option 2: Haul it yourself to a transfer station (free up to one ton a month)

Phoenix residential trash customers can drop off **up to one ton of items each calendar month at no charge**. Show your **city services (water) bill** and **photo ID** at the scale house [[phx-ts]].

- **27th Avenue Transfer Station,** 3060 S. 27th Ave., Phoenix 85009
- **North Gateway Transfer Station,** 30205 N. Black Canyon Hwy., Phoenix 85085
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday 5:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., Saturday 6 a.m. to 3 p.m., closed Sunday and on City holidays, including Thanksgiving and the day after [[phx-ts]].
- **Over a ton:** $55 a ton, with a cash deposit when you weigh in [[phx-ts]].
- **Tie it down.** Loads must be secured; the unsecured-load penalty is $20 [[phx-ts]].

A single couch is far under a ton, so this is usually the fastest free option if you have a truck or trailer.

## Option 3: Donate it (free pickup if it's in good shape)

### St. Vincent de Paul

St. Vincent de Paul says **pickups are free within greater Phoenix** [[svdp-stores]].

- **Standard pickup (free):** outside only, from the curb, driveway or an open garage with someone home. They don't come inside or go to second floors [[svdp-pickup]].
- **Book at least 3 days ahead** online or at **602-254-3338** (Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.). Pickups run weekdays 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., with a 30-minute heads-up call from the driver [[svdp-pickup]].
- **Priority pickup (paid):** they'll take items from inside the home, starting at **$110**, booked 2 days ahead [[svdp-pickup]].
- **Won't take:** damaged, ripped, stained, foul-smelling or pet-hair furniture, oversized pieces, and **lift chairs or recliners** [[svdp-no]].
- **Drop-off in Phoenix:** Last Chance Thrift Store, 420 W. Watkins Rd. (85003), or Sunnyslope Thrift Store, 8231 N. 7th St. (85020) [[svdp-stores]].

### Habitat for Humanity ReStore (Central Arizona)

Habitat's ReStores accept furniture, including **sofas and sectionals**, in good, clean, sellable condition [[hab-caz]].

- **Schedule a pickup** through the ReStore donations page or call **623-551-6000, option 1.** Be at the address during the arrival window [[hab-caz]].
- **Drop off** at the Phoenix ReStore (115 E. Watkins St., 85004) or West Phoenix ReStore (4344 W. Indian School Rd., Ste. 120, 85031). Stores are open 9 a.m. to 7 p.m., seven days a week; the page lists different donation drop-off hours in two places, so call ahead [[hab-caz]].
- Drivers can decline items they can't retrieve safely or that don't meet the guidelines [[hab-caz]].
