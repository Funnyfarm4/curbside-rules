## The short version

Refrigerators **can't go out with Phoenix bulk trash** [[phx-bulk]]. You have three official routes:

1. **City appliance pickup** for **$25 per refrigerator or freezer**, added to your city services bill. Call **602-262-6251** [[phx-app]].
2. **Drop it at a transfer station**, where your **first refrigerant appliance each year is free** [[phx-app]].
3. **Donate it** if it still works: the City recommends Stardust, and St. Vincent de Paul and Goodwill pick up appliances too [[phx-app]] [[svdp-pickup]] [[gw-pickup]].

Don't count on a utility buyback: SRP says its refrigerator recycling program is **no longer available** [[srp]].

## Option 1: The City's paid appliance pickup

Phoenix Public Works runs a **scheduled curbside pickup of large appliances for a fee**, separate from bulk trash [[phx-app]].

**Cost** [[phx-app]]:

- **Refrigerators, freezers and air conditioners:** **$25 each**, on your next city services bill. Two fridges cost $50.
- **Non-refrigerant appliances** (dishwashers, dryers, microwaves, stoves, washers, water heaters, evaporative coolers): **$30** for up to five. More than five is priced case by case.
- **Mixing them:** a stove, a dryer and a fridge cost **$55** ($30 + $25).
- **Cancel before the confirmed day**, or you'll be charged a cancellation fee.

**How to book:** call the Public Works Customer Care Center at **602-262-6251** [[phx-app]].

**Prep rules** [[phx-app]]:

1. **Empty** the appliance.
2. **Remove the doors, hinges, lids, glass shelves and latches** from refrigerators and freezers. Tape any remaining doors shut or take them off completely.
3. Put it **in front of your property, just behind the sidewalk** (or behind the curb if there's no sidewalk).
4. **Not** in the alley, and not in your garage or carport.

The door rule isn't just City policy. Under **A.R.S. 36-1651**, leaving a discarded refrigerator or freezer (1.5 cubic feet or larger) anywhere children can reach it **without removing the doors, hinges, lids or latches** is a **class 3 misdemeanor** [[ars]].

The City hauls collected appliances to a facility that removes regulated materials such as refrigerants, then sends the metal to a scrap dealer [[phx-app]].

## Option 2: Free drop-off at a transfer station

Phoenix residential solid waste customers can drop off **one appliance with refrigerant per year at no cost**. After that, there's a **$20 environmental fee per appliance**, plus disposal fees of **$55 per ton ($40 minimum)** [[phx-app]]. To skip the refrigerant fee, have the Freon removed by a certified reclaimer and bring the **certificate of removal** to the scale house [[phx-app]].

Bring a copy of your **city services (water) bill** and a **photo ID** whose name or address matches the bill [[phx-ts]].

- **North Gateway Transfer Station:** 30205 N. Black Canyon Hwy., Phoenix, AZ 85085 [[phx-ts]]
- **27th Avenue Transfer Station:** 3060 S. 27th Ave., Phoenix, AZ 85009 [[phx-ts]]
- **Hours:** Monday-Friday 5:30 a.m.-5 p.m., Saturday 6 a.m.-3 p.m., closed Sunday and on City holidays including **Thanksgiving and the day after** [[phx-ts]]

Secure your load: the unsecured-load penalty is **$20** [[phx-ts]].

## Option 3: Donate a working fridge

- **Stardust Building Supplies.** The City's appliance page suggests donating working appliances to Stardust, a local nonprofit, and notes donations are tax-deductible [[phx-app]]. Stardust's **home pickup** runs Monday to Friday in Maricopa County for a **$25 nonrefundable fee**, and your donation must be worth at least **$100**. Call **480-964-0177** [[stardust]].
- **St. Vincent de Paul.** Its **standard pickup is free** and accepts most gently used appliances, but drivers only collect from outside the home (curb, driveway or open garage with someone home) on the first floor, and you need to book **at least 3 days ahead**. Call **602-254-3338** [[svdp-pickup]]. **Non-working appliances** aren't accepted [[svdp-no]]. For removal from inside the home, its **Priority Pickup** starts at **$110** [[svdp-pickup]].
- **Goodwill Arizona.** Its home pickup form lists **large appliances (stove, fridge, washer)**. Items must be in good condition and at **ground level**, since drivers can't use stairs or elevators [[gw-pickup]].

## What about the Freon?

Federal rules put the duty to make sure refrigerant is recovered on the **final person in the disposal chain**, such as the scrap metal recycler or landfill. If an appliance arrives with no refrigerant left, that facility must keep a **signed statement** naming who recovered it and when [[epa-608]]. The City's transfer stations charge a $20 environmental fee for refrigerant appliances beyond your free one unless you bring a certificate of removal [[phx-app]].
