## The short version

Phoenix gives you two routes to a dumpster:

1. **Rent one from the City.** Public Works rents roll-off dumpsters to residents for **$165 per pull plus $55 per ton**, for up to 10 days [[phx-dr]]. You request it online and City staff work out the size, date and placement with you [[phx-ro]].
2. **Rent from a private company.** If the container will sit in a **public street, sidewalk or bike lane**, that's temporary use of the City's right-of-way, which City Code handles with a **right-of-way temporary use permit** from the Street Transportation Department [[phx-3180]]. There's no application fee [[phx-3181]].

> **Phoenix doesn't publish a dumpster-specific permit page.** The City Code rules below cover any temporary use of a street or sidewalk. Before a private dumpster is dropped in the street, confirm with Right-of-Way Management at **602-262-6235** or rmp@phoenix.gov [[phx-rowm]].

## Option 1: Rent a dumpster from the City of Phoenix

### Who can rent

- Residents with an **active city services account** [[phx-dr]]
- The account must be **current, with no past-due amount** [[phx-dr]]
- You'll **sign a liability release and a service contract** before the dumpster is delivered [[phx-dr]]

### What it costs

- **$165 per pull.** That's what the City charges to pick up and empty the dumpster [[phx-dr]].
- **$55 per ton,** based on what the dumpster weighs at pickup [[phx-dr]].
- **Up to 10 days.** There's an additional fee if you need it longer [[phx-dr]].

So a load that weighs 2 tons comes to $165 + $110 = **$275** at the City's posted rates. Heavy material like dirt, roofing or tile adds up fast at $55 a ton.

### Sizes

- **10 yard** (13 x 8 x 4 feet)
- **20 yard** (23 x 8 x 5 feet)
- **30 yard** (23 x 8 x 6 feet)
- **40 yard** (23 x 8 x 8 feet) [[phx-dr]]

You don't pick the size on the form. A customer service specialist decides it with you after you submit your request [[phx-dr]].

### How to request

1. Fill out the [Roll-Off Rental Request form](https://myphx311.phoenix.gov/rolloffrental/) on myPHX311. It asks for your address, desired delivery date, and whether the bin should go **on your property** or **in front of your property** [[phx-ro]].
2. A Public Works employee contacts you **within 48 hours** to set the bin size, delivery date and placement [[phx-ro]].
3. Your request isn't confirmed until that call happens [[phx-ro]].

Questions about a request: **602-262-6251** [[phx-ro]].

**Not allowed:** hazardous waste [[phx-dr]].

## Option 2: A private dumpster in the street or on the sidewalk

### Which permit applies

City Code lets the City issue **right-of-way temporary use permits** for the temporary use or restriction of the public right-of-way, "including, but not limited to, a street, sidewalk, or designated bicycle lane" [[phx-3180]]. Street Transportation's **Right-of-Way Management Office** coordinates temporary street restrictions [[phx-rowm]].

### Cost and lead time

- **Application fee: none** for a right-of-way temporary use permit [[phx-3181]].
- **Partial street restriction or sidewalk restriction:** apply at least **2 business days** before the first day [[phx-3181]].
- **Full restriction (closing the street):** at least **3 business days** ahead [[phx-3181]].
- The City's TRACS application form says permits take **48 hours to process**; submit by email to RMP@phoenix.gov [[phx-tracs]].

### What permit holders must do

- Carry **insurance naming the City as an additional insured,** and agree to indemnify the City [[phx-3180]].
- Follow the **City of Phoenix Traffic Barricade Manual.** Cones, barricades and other temporary traffic control devices must be put up and taken down by someone with the proper certification [[phx-3180]].
- The City can **suspend or revoke** a temporary use permit [[phx-3180]].

In practice, that means the dumpster company (or the contractor running your job) is usually better placed to apply than a homeowner. Ask whoever is renting you the dumpster whether they'll handle the right-of-way permit and barricades.

### Contact

- **Right-of-Way Management Office:** 602-262-6235, Monday to Friday except City holidays, **6 a.m. to 2:30 p.m.** [[phx-rowm]]
- **Email:** rmp@phoenix.gov [[phx-rowm]]

## Remodeling? Debris can't go in your City trash cart

Phoenix City Code bars construction and demolition debris from City trash, recycling and green organics containers. If you generate it, you're responsible for removing it promptly, and it can't be stored where it might blow off the site [[phx-2714]]. The City may provide containers for construction and demolition debris for a separate fee [[phx-2714]], which is the dumpster rental above.

Bulk trash pickup won't solve it either: it doesn't take construction or demolition material over 25 pounds or longer than 4 feet, or bricks, concrete, dirt, rocks, asphalt, or roofing shingles and tiles [[phx-bulk]].
