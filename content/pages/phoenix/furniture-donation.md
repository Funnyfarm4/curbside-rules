## The short version

Phoenix has several charities that will come get good furniture. **St. Vincent de Paul** picks up free across greater Phoenix, and **Habitat ReStore**, **Goodwill** and **Christian Family Care** also schedule pickups [[svdp-stores]] [[hab-caz]] [[gw-pickup]] [[cfc]]. The catch: free pickups are **outside and ground-level only**, and the furniture has to be clean, sturdy and sellable [[svdp-pickup]] [[gw-pickup]].

If no charity wants it, the City's bulk trash appointments and free transfer station drop-off are the fallback [[phx-bulk]] [[phx-ts]].

## St. Vincent de Paul (free pickup)

St. Vincent de Paul says **pickups are free within greater Phoenix** [[svdp-stores]].

- **Standard pickup (free):** curbside, driveway or an open garage with someone home. Drivers don't come inside or go to second floors, and small items must be boxed or bagged [[svdp-pickup]].
- **Book at least 3 days ahead** online or at **602-254-3338** (Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.). Call for an earlier date, subject to availability [[svdp-pickup]].
- **Pickup window:** weekdays 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., with a 30-minute heads-up call from the driver. No disassembly [[svdp-pickup]].
- **Mattresses:** acceptable mattresses and box springs are **$50 each**, up to 3 [[svdp-pickup]].
- **Priority pickup (paid):** from inside the home, starting at **$110** depending on size, booked 2 days ahead. Disassembly is available, and they'll go to the third floor or higher only with an elevator [[svdp-pickup]].

**What it won't take** [[svdp-no]]:

- Flimsy particle-board or pressed-wood furniture
- Oversized pieces such as desks, armoires, bunk beds, headboards, bed frames, entertainment centers and wall units (a full matching bedroom set is the exception)
- Anything damaged, ripped, stained, foul-smelling or with pet hair, or missing parts
- Office furniture, cribs, lift chairs and recliners, pool tables, and non-working appliances
- Dishwashers, water heaters, spas, toilets and construction materials

**Drop-off in Phoenix:** Last Chance Thrift Store, 420 W. Watkins Rd. (85003), or Sunnyslope Thrift Store, 8231 N. 7th St. (85020) [[svdp-stores]].

## Habitat for Humanity ReStore (Central Arizona)

Habitat's ReStores take furniture, appliances and building materials in **good, clean, working and sellable condition** [[hab-caz]].

- **Schedule a pickup** through the ReStore donations page or call **623-551-6000, option 1**. Appointments fill quickly, and you need to be at the address during the arrival window [[hab-caz]].
- **Furniture it lists:** sofas, bed frames, hutches, sectionals, dressers, TV stands and credenzas in sellable condition, plus clean, undamaged patio furniture [[hab-caz]].
- **Appliances:** 8 years old or newer, clean and fully working; heating and cooling units 5 years old or newer [[hab-caz]].
- **Won't take:** office furniture (cubicles, conference tables), water beds, water heaters, spas, paint and chemicals [[hab-caz]].
- **Drop off** at the Phoenix ReStore (115 E. Watkins St., 85004) or West Phoenix ReStore (4344 W. Indian School Rd., Ste. 120, 85031). The page lists store hours of 9 a.m. to 7 p.m. and donation drop-off hours that differ by location, so call ahead [[hab-caz]].
- Drivers can decline items they can't retrieve safely or that don't meet the guidelines [[hab-caz]].

## Goodwill of Central and Northern Arizona

Goodwill's home pickup for large items runs through its partner **ReSupply**: you list your items and book online [[gw-guide]]. Its pickup page doesn't list a price, so check the cost when you book.

- **Takes:** sofas, loveseats, sectionals (without a bed), bed frames, chairs, end tables, patio furniture and large appliances like stoves, fridges and washers [[gw-pickup]].
- **Rules:** items must be in good to gently used condition with no large rips or bugs, gathered and boxed or bagged, and at **ground level**; drivers can't use stairs or elevators [[gw-pickup]].
- **Won't take:** any mattress or box spring, including futons and sleeper sofas, or trash and hazardous materials [[gw-pickup]].
- You can also drop off at any Goodwill store during business hours, which vary by location [[gw-guide]].

## Christian Family Care Thrift Store

If you're in Phoenix and have a **larger furniture item in good condition**, Christian Family Care will arrange a pickup. Call **602-971-0241** [[cfc]].

- **Drop off** at 3121 E. Greenway Rd., Phoenix 85032, Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. [[cfc]]
- **Takes** furniture and large appliances (washers, dryers, stoves and refrigerators) [[cfc]].
- **Won't take** mattresses and box springs, CRT TVs, car seats or toxic materials [[cfc]].

## If no charity will take it: the City options

### Bulk trash appointment

Phoenix collects bulk trash **by appointment only**, four times a year per household, booked online or at **602-262-6251** at least 3 business days ahead [[phx-bulk]]. Set items out no more than 7 days before your date and by 5:30 a.m. on the day, within 5 feet of the curb [[phx-bulk]]. The scheduler blocks Halloween, Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day plus the 7 days after each, so book early in the holiday season [[phx-bulk]].

> Furniture is listed as typical bulk trash on the City's page [[phx-bulk]], but our [Phoenix couch guide](/phoenix/couch/) explains a conflict with City Code; confirm with Public Works when you book.

### Free transfer station drop-off

Phoenix residential trash customers can drop off **up to one ton a month free** at either transfer station with a city services (water) bill and photo ID [[phx-ts]]:

- **27th Avenue Transfer Station,** 3060 S. 27th Ave., Phoenix 85009
- **North Gateway Transfer Station,** 30205 N. Black Canyon Hwy., Phoenix 85085
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday 5:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., Saturday 6 a.m. to 3 p.m., closed Sunday and City holidays, including Thanksgiving and the day after [[phx-ts]].
- Anything over a ton is **$55 a ton**, and loads must be tied down or you'll pay a $20 penalty [[phx-ts]].
