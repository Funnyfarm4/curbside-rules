## The short version

Phoenix collects bulk trash **by appointment only**. You get **four pickups a year**, each a single pile of up to **10 cubic yards** at the edge of your property [[phx-bulk]].

Book on the City's [Bulk Trash page](https://www.phoenix.gov/administration/departments/publicworks/residential-trash-recycling/bulk-trash-pickup.html) or by calling **602-262-6251** [[phx-bulk]].

> **Heads-up for November:** the scheduler blocks Halloween and the 7 days after it, and does the same for Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Day [[phx-bulk]]. If you need something gone during those weeks, the transfer stations or a hauler are your options.

## What counts as bulk trash

The City describes bulk trash as items too large for your garbage or recycling container, such as **furniture, small appliances, toilets and large amounts of vegetation** [[phx-bulk]]. Small appliances without refrigerant, cardboard boxes and up to 25 pounds of household construction debris are also allowed under City Code [[phx-code]].

**Mattresses: call first.** City Code Section 27-22 lists "all mattresses or furniture" among items not acceptable for normal residential bulk collection [[phx-code]], while the Public Works page names furniture as typical bulk trash [[phx-bulk]]. Before putting a mattress or couch out, confirm with Public Works at 602-262-6251.

## How to book

1. **Check for a community appointment.** Some HOAs and neighborhoods have a group date already set. The City posts the list on its Bulk Trash page; check it before booking your own [[phx-bulk]].
2. **Book online or call 602-262-6251.** Appointments can be made as late as **3 business days ahead** and as far as **one year out**. Most areas have openings within a couple of weeks [[phx-bulk]].
3. **Watch for your confirmation email.** It has the link to cancel [[phx-bulk]].

You get **four collections a year**. If you cancel **at least 2 business days ahead**, using the link in your confirmation email or by calling, the appointment doesn't count against your four [[phx-bulk]].

## How to set out your pile

- **Book first, then set out.** Put items out **no more than 7 days** before your appointment. Setting out earlier violates City ordinance [[phx-bulk]] [[phx-code]].
- **Out by 5:30 a.m.** on your appointment day [[phx-bulk]].
- **One pile per household, up to 10 cubic yards.** The City's flyer puts that at under 17 feet long, 4 feet wide and 4 feet high [[phx-bulk]] [[phx-flyer]].
- **Place it curbside on your own property,** parallel to the street, no more than 5 feet from the curb or front edge of the property [[phx-bulk]].
- **Keep it 5 feet from fixed objects,** and not on top of a water meter or in the alley [[phx-bulk]] [[phx-flyer]].
- **Bag and tie** grass, leaves, weeds, twigs and hedge clippings. Cut branches to under 12 inches across and 4 feet long [[phx-bulk]].
- **Don't combine piles** with neighbors. Each household needs its own appointment [[phx-bulk]].
- Sweep or rake the spot after pickup if needed [[phx-bulk]].

## What the City won't take

- More than 10 cubic yards, loose litter or regular household trash
- Loose grass, leaves, weeds, twigs and clippings
- **Household hazardous waste** such as antifreeze, pool chemicals, oil, batteries and paint
- Anything in a Bagster bag, or more than 2 wooden pallets
- **Bricks, concrete, dirt, rocks, asphalt and roofing shingles or tiles**
- Construction or demolition material over 25 pounds or longer than 4 feet
- Metal or car parts over 20 pounds or longer than 4 feet
- **Tires** of any size
- **Glass** such as windows, shower doors, patio doors, mirrors and fluorescent tubes
- Tree trunks or stumps longer than 4 feet and thicker than 12 inches
- **Refrigerators, freezers and air conditioners** [[phx-bulk]]

Under City Code, if you set out banned items or an oversized pile, you're responsible for removing and disposing of it at your own expense [[phx-code]].

## Appliances: a separate paid pickup

Refrigerant appliances aren't bulk trash, but the City will pick up large appliances for a fee if you call **602-262-6251** [[phx-app]]:

- **Up to 5 non-refrigerant appliances** (washers, dryers, stoves, dishwashers, water heaters, microwaves, evaporative coolers): **$30** total on your next city services bill.
- **Refrigerators, freezers and AC units:** **$25 each.** Remove the doors, hinges, lids, glass shelves and latches on fridges and freezers first, as state law requires.
- **TVs:** $30 for up to 2 CRT sets; no more than 2 TVs per pickup.
- A cancellation fee applies if you don't cancel before the confirmed day.

## Haul it yourself: free monthly transfer station drop-off

Phoenix residential trash customers can drop off **up to one ton a month free** at either City transfer station. Bring your **city services (water) bill** and a **photo ID** with a matching name or address [[phx-ts]].

- **27th Avenue Transfer Station,** 3060 S. 27th Ave., Phoenix 85009
- **North Gateway Transfer Station,** 30205 N. Black Canyon Hwy., Phoenix 85085
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday 5:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., Saturday 6 a.m. to 3 p.m., closed Sunday and on City holidays, including **Veterans Day, Wednesday, November 11**, and Thanksgiving and the day after [[phx-ts]].
- **Over one ton:** $55 a ton. Secure your load or pay a $20 penalty [[phx-ts]].
- **Appliances and TVs:** one refrigerant appliance a year and 2 CRT TVs a month are free; after that it's $20 each plus disposal fees [[phx-app]] [[phx-ts]].
- **Tires:** up to 5 car tires a year, rims removed [[phx-ts]].
- **Spas and boats** must be cut into fourths or you pay a $50 penalty, and you need the registration or title [[phx-ts]].
