## The short version

Phoenix doesn't run a walk-in hazardous waste drop-off. Instead, **Public Works picks up household hazardous waste (HHW) at your home, free, once per calendar year**, if you have a City residential solid waste account [[phx-hhw]].

That pickup covers paint, chemicals, automotive fluids, batteries, propane cylinders and a long list of **electronics** [[phx-list]]. Bigger items like **TVs and appliances** can also go to the City's two **transfer stations** [[phx-ts]]. Don't put any of it in your trash or recycling container [[phx-hhw]].

## Option 1: Free home pickup (once a year)

### Who qualifies

Only customers with a **residential solid waste account** with the City. You'll need the **account number** from the upper right corner of your City Services Bill. If you're not sure you qualify, email **hhwcollection@phoenix.gov** [[phx-hhw]].

### How to book

1. Check the accepted items list below [[phx-hhw]].
2. Fill out the City's **HHW collection request form**, which shows open appointments **within the next 30 days**. Have your current bill, account number and service address ready [[phx-hhw]].
3. **No dates showing?** Email **hhwcollection@phoenix.gov**; the City says its team will work with you on collection accommodations [[phx-hhw]].
4. You'll get an **email reminder 1-2 business days** before pickup [[phx-hhw]].

General Public Works questions go to **602-262-6251** or solid.waste@phoenix.gov [[phx-hhw]].

### How to set it out

[[phx-hhw]]

- Put everything in **sealed, leakproof containers**, then in a **box clearly labeled "HHW."** Put a leaking bucket inside a labeled, sealed, leakproof container.
- Place the box **next to your garage or front door**, where staff can see and reach it. **Not at the curb, in the street or on the sidewalk.**
- Have it out by **7 a.m.** Crews come between 7 a.m. and 5 p.m. and don't give a set time.

### What it takes, and how much

[[phx-list]]

- **Paint (10 gallons total):** household, acrylic, enamel, oil or latex paint; paint thinner and stripper; stains.
- **Chemicals (5 gallons total):** cleaning chemicals, small batches of photochemicals, pool chemicals, aerosols.
- **Automotive (5 gallons total):** antifreeze, motor oil and other fluids, gasoline, diesel and kerosene, car wash and wax, tire cleaner.
- **Garden (5 gallons total):** fertilizers and pesticides.
- **Batteries:** single-use alkaline, rechargeable lithium-ion, and lead-acid car batteries.
- **Cylinders:** camp stove and grill propane tanks, fire extinguishers.
- **Miscellaneous:** fluorescent and CFL bulbs, mercury thermostats, circuit boards, road flares (no shooting flares).
- **Electronics:** phones, computers and hard drives, tablets and e-readers, printers, scanners and fax machines, keyboards and mice, modems and routers, cameras, video game equipment, DVD and Blu-ray players, speakers, cables, remotes, power strips, ink cartridges and holiday lights. **One monitor** and **one TV under 50 inches**, both with unbroken glass.

### What it won't take

Ammunition or explosives, biomedical waste, volatile materials, compressed gases like MAPP or acetylene, **medications and sharps**, radioactive waste, business or commercial waste, and regular or bulk trash [[phx-list]].

## Option 2: Transfer stations for TVs and appliances

Phoenix's two transfer stations take **appliances and televisions**, including old **CRT** TVs [[phx-ts]]:

- **North Gateway Transfer Station:** 30205 N. Black Canyon Hwy, Phoenix, AZ 85085
- **27th Avenue Transfer Station:** 3060 S. 27th Ave, Phoenix, AZ 85009

**Hours:** Monday to Friday 5:30 a.m. to 5 p.m., Saturday 6 a.m. to 3 p.m., closed Sunday and City holidays. Both are closed **Wednesday, November 11** for Veterans Day [[phx-ts]].

**Cost** [[phx-ts]]:

- Residents may bring **two CRT units per household per month free**. After that it's **$20 per CRT** plus disposal at **$55 a ton ($40 minimum)**. CRTs aren't accepted from businesses or nonprofits.
- Residential customers paying full trash fees get **one free load of up to 1 ton a month**. Bring your City Services Bill and a photo ID that matches it.
- Up to **five car tires a year**, rims removed.

The transfer stations **don't accept regulated hazardous waste**, so paint and chemicals still go through the home pickup [[phx-ts]]. For appliances and bulk items, see our [Phoenix bulk trash guide](/phoenix/bulk-trash/).

## Old medications

Medications and sharps can't go in the HHW pickup [[phx-list]]. The City suggests pharmacy disposal kiosks, drug take-back events, or a **Phoenix Police medication drop box**, which takes pills, patches, ointments, vitamins, pet medications and prescription drugs, but **not** needles, inhalers, aerosols, liquid medicines or thermometers [[phx-meds]]. Drop boxes include [[phx-meds]]:

- Police Downtown Headquarters, 620 W. Washington St.
- Cactus Park Precinct, 12220 N. 39th Ave.
- Central City Precinct, 1902 S. 16th St.
- Mountain View Precinct, 2075 E. Maryland Ave.
- South Mountain Precinct, 400 W. Southern Ave.
