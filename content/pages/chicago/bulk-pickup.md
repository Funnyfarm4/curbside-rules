## The short version

If you live in a **single-family home or a building with four units or fewer**, Chicago's Department of Streets and Sanitation (DSS) can pick up **large items** and **extra loads of trash** as a bulk collection [[chi-san]] [[chi-grid]].

**Call first.** Arrange the pickup with your **ward Streets and Sanitation superintendent** (or through **311** / the CHI 311 app) **before** you put anything out [[chi-san]] [[chi-ward18]] [[chi-311]]. Putting bulk items out without arranging pickup can get you a ticket [[chi-ward18]].

There's **no extra charge** for bulk waste beyond the City's monthly garbage fee [[chi-fee]].

## Who qualifies for City pickup

City refuse collection covers [[chi-code-235]] [[chi-san]] [[chi-grid]]:

- A **single dwelling**
- A multiple dwelling with **four or fewer** dwelling units
- Certain townhouses and small bed-and-breakfasts that meet the code's rules

DSS says it collects from about **600,000** households citywide under the grid garbage system, with weekly pickups Monday through Friday [[chi-grid]].

**Five or more units:** City service doesn't apply. The owner or agent of a multiple dwelling with five or more units must have refuse removed at least once a week **at their own cost** by a private licensed scavenger [[chi-code-240]] [[chi-san]]. Ask your property manager how the building handles furniture, mattresses and other bulk items.

## How to schedule a bulk pickup

1. **Find your ward.** Use the City's [ward and alderman lookup](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/mayor/iframe/lookup_ward_and_alderman.html), then open your ward's chicago.gov page for the Streets and Sanitation / ward yard phone number [[chi-grid]].
2. **Call the ward yard or 311.** Tell them you need a **bulk pickup** for large items or an extra load of trash. Ward pages list refuse and bulk pickup as a Streets and Sanitation service; for example the **44th Ward Yard** is at **1501 W. School St.**, **312-744-5028**, open 7 a.m. to 3 p.m. weekdays [[chi-ward44]]. The **18th Ward** Sanitation Office is at **1756 W. 74th St.**, **312-747-3655**, open 6 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. weekdays, and asks residents to contact them before putting bulk items out [[chi-ward18]]. Outside Chicago you can reach 311 at **312-744-5000** [[chi-311]].
3. **Wait for instructions** on when to set the items out. Don't put them at the curb until the ward has arranged the pickup [[chi-san]] [[chi-ward18]].

DSS district offices are also listed on the City's [Grid Garbage Collection](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/streets/provdrs/streets_san/svcs/grid-garbage-collection.html) page if you need a starting point for your area [[chi-grid]].

## What it costs

Residents on City garbage service pay **$9.50 per dwelling unit per month** (for example $9.50 for a single-family home, $19 for a two-flat) [[chi-fee]] [[chi-code-235]]. Eligible seniors can get a **50%** discount ($4.75 per unit) [[chi-fee]] [[chi-code-235]].

The City's Garbage Fee FAQ says that fee covers garbage collection and that there is **no additional cost for lawn waste or bulk waste collection** [[chi-fee]]. You can't opt out of City garbage service for a four-unit-or-fewer property; DSS is the exclusive provider under the Municipal Code [[chi-fee]] [[chi-code-235]].

## What you can put out

Official Chicago pages do not publish a single citywide list of every item DSS will take as bulk. What they do say:

- Arrange a bulk pickup before setting out **any large item** or an **extra load of trash**, such as after cleaning a basement or attic [[chi-san]].
- **Refrigerators and freezers** need doors or lids removed, and you still have to arrange the bulk pickup before set-out [[chi-san]].
- Keep regular garbage in carts with lids closed; don't leave raw garbage outside the container [[chi-san]].
- **Remodeling debris** is your responsibility to remove, and dumpsters can be rented for that [[chi-san]]. For dumpster rules on the public way, see our [Chicago dumpster permit guide](/chicago/dumpster-permit/).

For item-specific rules we have already verified, see [how to get rid of a couch in Chicago](/chicago/couch/) and [how to get rid of a mattress in Chicago](/chicago/mattress/).

## If your building or address doesn't qualify

- **5+ unit buildings and commercial properties** must use a **private licensed scavenger** [[chi-code-240]] [[chi-san]].
- For chemicals, oil paint and electronics (not furniture), Chicago's Household Chemicals & Computer Recycling Facility on Goose Island is the City drop-off — see our [Chicago hazardous waste and electronics guide](/chicago/hazardous-waste-electronics/).
