## The short version

If a dumpster or roll-off box will sit on a Chicago **street, alley, parkway or sidewalk**, it needs a **dumpster permit** from the Chicago Department of Transportation (CDOT) [[chi-code]] [[cdot-permits]].

The good news if you're the customer: **the permit is the dumpster company's job.** City code makes it unlawful for any *provider*, meaning the company that leases you the dumpster, to place one on the public way without first getting the permit [[chi-code]]. If the dumpster goes on **private property**, like your driveway, no dumpster permit is needed, although other permits may apply [[cdot-app]].

## Do you need one?

- **Street, curb lane, alley, parkway or sidewalk:** yes. These are all public way placements with a set fee [[cdot-app]].
- **Your own driveway, yard or private lot:** no dumpster permit, but other permits may apply [[cdot-app]].
- **Downtown:** yes, at double the rate. The Central Business District runs roughly from Division Street south to Roosevelt Road, and from Lake Michigan west to Halsted Street (west only to LaSalle Street between Division Street and Chicago Avenue) [[chi-cbd]] [[chi-code]].

## What it costs

These are the usage fees in City code and CDOT's dumpster application guide [[chi-code]] [[cdot-app]]. They're the same for an alley, curb lane, parkway or sidewalk.

- **Class A (30 cubic yards or less), outside downtown:** $50 for up to 3 days, or $100 per month
- **Class B (over 30 cubic yards), outside downtown:** $100 for up to 3 days, or $200 per month
- **Inside the Central Business District:** double those amounts, so $100 or $200 for Class A, and $200 or $400 for Class B

**On top of that:**

- **An application fee.** CDOT sets it, and it can rise each January with inflation (up to 2.5% a year) [[chi-code]]. CDOT posts the current amount in its yearly [Permit Fee Schedule](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/depts/cdot/provdrs/construction_information/svcs/permit-fee-schedule.html), and says fees may change from year to year [[cdot-fees]] [[cdot-permits]].
- **Parking meters, if any are blocked.** There's a monthly surcharge for each disabled meter or metered space. If a meter has to be pulled out, the fee is $150 to remove and reinstall it, or $1,000 for a meter that serves several spaces [[cdot-app]].

Ask your dumpster company whether its quote includes the permit and any meter charges. Either way, the provider files and pays CDOT online [[cdot-app]].

## Where the dumpster can't go

City code bans dumpster placement [[chi-code]]:

- within **40 feet** of a bus stop
- within **20 feet** of a fire hydrant
- within **10 feet** of an intersection, a crosswalk, or a parking space for people with disabilities
- on a **parkway or sidewalk**, unless the provider shows CDOT there's no workable alternative and it won't create a hazard or block traffic

**Winter warning:** from **November 1 to April 1**, a dumpster on an **arterial street** has to come off once CDOT gives the provider notice of a Phase 3 snow emergency, with 12 hours to remove it [[chi-code]].

The dumpster also has to sit **immediately next to the address** on the permit [[chi-code]]. If a hydrant or another obstacle blocks that spot, the provider can amend the permit to change the location for **no extra fee** [[cdot-app]].

## How the permit works

1. **The provider is licensed first.** Before CDOT issues any dumpster permit, the provider needs **$1,000,000** of liability insurance naming the City as additional insured, plus a **$5,000 letter of credit** [[chi-code]] [[cdot-app]]. This one-time license part stays on file with CDOT [[cdot-app]].
2. **It applies for each job online.** Dumpster permit applications and payments are only accepted online, through the City's permit portal at ipi.cityofchicago.org [[cdot-app]] [[cdot-permits]]. The application lists the provider, how many dumpsters, the class, the street address, what it's for and how long it'll stay [[chi-code]].
3. **Changes need an amendment.** A bigger dumpster or a longer stay needs an amended permit and an adjusted fee. A different number of dumpsters, a different address (other than a hydrant or obstacle move) or a different use needs a new permit [[chi-code]].
4. **Canceled job?** If you cancel before delivery, the provider can return the permit with a cancellation request and get a credit for the permit fee by emailing CDOT's permit office [[cdot-app]].

## What to check before you book

- **Ask if they hold a CDOT dumpster license** and will pull the permit for your address. Unpermitted dumpsters can be fined **$250 to $1,500** per offense, or **$500 to $3,000** downtown, with each day a separate offense, and the City can haul one away at the provider's cost [[chi-code]].
- **Look at the dumpster when it arrives.** The provider's name and phone number must be on both long sides in letters at least 3 inches tall, along with an ID number, and there must be reflective strips on all four corners [[chi-code]].
- **Know who to ask.** CDOT's right-of-way permit questions go to **CDOTPermitsupport@cityofchicago.org**, and CDOT publishes permit data on ChiStreetWork and the Chicago Data Portal [[cdot-permits]].
