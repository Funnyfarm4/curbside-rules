## The short version

Renting a dumpster for a cleanout or remodel? Two City rules matter:

1. **If the dumpster will sit in the street, a parking space, an alley or over the sidewalk,** you need a **Traffic Control Permit** from the Development Services Department [[sd-ib718]] [[sd-tcp]]. It costs **$176.57 for up to 4 weeks plus an $87.68 processing fee** [[sd-ib718]] [[sd-ib177]].
2. **The company hauling it must hold a City of San Diego franchise.** City code bars anyone from collecting and hauling solid waste in the City without one [[sd-6601]].

> **Start early.** The bulletin asks for at least 3 working days' notice, but the City warns that new Traffic Control Permits can take **about 15 business days** to process [[sd-ib718]] [[sd-tcp]].

## When you need a permit

The City requires a Traffic Control Permit for a temporary storage container or dumpster that **encroaches into the public right of way, including the sidewalk area** (San Diego Municipal Code §129.0702) [[sd-ib718]]. The Traffic Control Permit page lists "the installation of a temporary dumpster or storage container in the public right of way" as one of the things that needs one, and says there are **no exemptions** [[sd-tcp]].

The City's homeowner permit guide puts it plainly: moving pods, dumpsters and other temporary structures "require a permit if they encroach on the public right of way, such as sidewalks or streets" [[sd-wipr]]. A dumpster that sits entirely on your own driveway or lot, without hanging over the sidewalk, isn't what these rules describe.

## What it costs

[[sd-ib718]] [[sd-ib177]]

- **Traffic Control dumpster fee: $176.57**, covering **up to 4 weeks** (the July 2026 bulletin lists it as "per month")
- **Traffic Control processing fee: $87.68**, flat
- **Metered parking:** **$20 per meter, per day** for each meter the dumpster blocks

For one dumpster that doesn't block a meter, that's **$264.25** in City fees. Pay online by e-check or credit card (bank and processing fees apply), by check made out to "City Treasurer" in the drop-off safe at the downtown Development Services Center, or at an in-person payments appointment [[sd-ib718]].

**A moving pod or storage container** is a different, shorter permit: **$87.68 per week, for a maximum of one week**, plus the same processing fee [[sd-ib718]].

## How to apply

1. **Fill out the Traffic Control Plan/Permit Form (DS-269)** and the **Project Contacts Information form (DS-345)** [[sd-tcp]].
2. **Draw a traffic control plan.** For a dumpster it needs the start and end dates, the street and nearest cross streets, the owner's name, address, phone and email, and a plan view showing exactly where the dumpster goes, with distances to the nearest driveways, a north arrow, any parking meters (with meter numbers), painted curb zones, bus stops and driveway openings, plus the street width, parking lane width and the dumpster's dimensions [[sd-ib718]].
3. **Apply online.** Everything is submitted electronically through the City's Traffic Control Permit page, where you create an account [[sd-ib718]] [[sd-tcp]]. PDFs must meet the City's upload requirements or review is delayed [[sd-tcp]].
4. **Questions:** contact Traffic Control by virtual appointment or email **DSDTrafficSafety@sandiego.gov** [[sd-ib718]].

Ask your dumpster company whether it will prepare and file the permit for you before you book.

### Placement rules

[[sd-ib718]]

- **Not in front of a painted curb:** no red (no parking), white (passenger loading) or blue (accessible parking) zones.
- **At least 10 feet** of clearance from the dumpster door to the nearest obstruction.
- **At least 25 feet** from the dumpster door to the alley or curb return.
- **At least 15 feet** between the dumpster door and a fire hydrant.
- Other cars can't park in the leftover space of a dumpster zone; violators can be cited.

### Before and during the rental

- **Post Tow-Away/No Parking signs 72 hours** before the dumpster is placed, and deactivate any parking meters where applicable [[sd-ib718]].
- **Extensions:** submit a new request with your latest approved, stamped permit. After three extensions, the City requires a new signed DS-269 [[sd-tcp]].
- **Inspections:** City staff can inspect without notice and change the approved plan if field conditions call for it [[sd-ib718]].
- **No permit?** Work in the right of way without an approved permit can bring a stop-work order, a notice of violation and fines, and the City back-bills the fees [[sd-ib177]].

## Pick a City franchised hauler

San Diego Municipal Code §66.0108 says no person may collect and haul residential or commercial solid waste in the City without a current City franchise agreement, and every collection vehicle must carry a City-issued tag or decal [[sd-6601]]. The City's Environmental Services Department publishes a **Franchise Hauler Directory** listing the approved companies, their phone numbers and the equipment they offer, roll-offs included. The City doesn't assign haulers or set their rates, so call a few for prices [[sd-fhd]].

There are exceptions. You can haul your own waste to a licensed facility yourself, and a licensed contractor can remove the construction debris from its own job with its own employees and equipment [[sd-6601]].

## Remodeling? The debris rules

- **Not in your City bins.** Building and construction debris, including dirt, rocks, carpet and large scraps, can never go in a City trash, recycling or organics bin [[sd-general]].
- **Permitted projects must recycle.** Most construction, demolition and remodeling projects that need a building, combination or demolition permit fall under the City's C&D Debris Deposit Ordinance: submit a Waste Management Form, pay a refundable deposit, divert at least **65%** of the debris by weight (for permits issued since July 1, 2016), and turn in weight receipts within 180 days of final inspection to get the deposit back. The City recommends using a franchised hauler to help ensure compliance [[sd-cd]].
- **Hauling it yourself?** At the Miramar Landfill, a car, SUV, pickup or trailer up to 5 ft x 9 ft x 4 ft (Tier 1) of **C&D debris from inside the City is $108** a load, or **$122** from outside the City, through June 30, 2027. Uncovered loads cost an extra $50 [[sd-feepdf]].
