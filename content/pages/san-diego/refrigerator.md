## The short version

In the City of San Diego, getting rid of a fridge right now mostly means doing the hauling yourself or paying someone:

1. **The City isn't picking up bulky items.** Its planned bulky item pickup is **paused until further notice** [[sd-faq]], and major appliances can **never** go in a City bin [[sd-general]].
2. **You can drop it off** at the **Miramar Recycling Center** for **$25** [[sd-mrc]], if you can get it there.
3. **To donate it,** Goodwill's **Otay Mesa Outlet** takes refrigerators [[gw-sd]].

## Why the City won't take it

When San Diego cut its solid waste fees for fiscal years 2028 and 2029, it paused two planned upgrades, weekly recycling and **bulky item pickup**, until further notice. Regular trash, recycling and organics collection continues [[sd-faq]].

Your bins don't help either. The City's list of things that can **never go in any bin** includes major appliances, water heaters and **items over 50 pounds** [[sd-general]].

**Don't leave it on the curb.** San Diego Municipal Code section 54.0209 bans placing waste in public spaces without authorization. Fines run **up to $1,000 per violation**, and each day it stays can count as a separate violation [[sd-illegal]].

## Option 1: The Miramar Recycling Center ($25)

[[sd-mrc]]

- **Where:** Miramar Landfill entrance, **5165 Convoy St., San Diego, CA 92111**, north of State Route 52
- **Hours:** **Monday to Saturday, 7 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.** Closed Sundays and on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, **Thanksgiving Day** and Christmas Day.
- **Run by:** Allan Company, for the City
- **Questions and current prices:** **858-268-8971**

### What it costs

[[sd-mrc]]

- **Refrigerators: $25 each**
- **Freezers: $25 each**
- **Air conditioners** and other appliances containing CFCs: **$25 each**
- **Washers, dryers, dishwashers, stoves, microwaves, hot water heaters** and other appliances: **$12 each**

The City says all pricing is subject to change, so call before you go [[sd-mrc]].

### Don't drive it into the landfill

The Miramar Landfill fee sheet says **appliances, CRTs, TVs, e-waste and household hazardous materials must be properly disposed of before entering the landfill** [[sd-feepdf]]. Stop at the Recycling Center first; it's at the same entrance [[sd-mrc]]. If you have other junk in the load, a car, SUV, pickup or small trailer of general refuse from inside the City is **$75** at the landfill, and uncovered loads cost an extra **$50** [[sd-feepdf]].

## Option 2: Donate it

- **Goodwill Industries of San Diego County** accepts **large household appliances (ovens, stoves, refrigerators)** at its **Otay Mesa Outlet**, not at its regular donation centers [[gw-sd]]. Questions: **619-225-2200, ext. 197**, Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. [[gw-sd]]
- **San Diego Habitat for Humanity** closed both of its ReStores (Kearny Mesa and National City) in **October 2024**, so it no longer takes appliance donations [[hab-sd]].

## Not a City of San Diego resident?

The Recycling Center page doesn't list a residency requirement; call **858-268-8971** to confirm before you go [[sd-mrc]]. At the landfill itself, loads from outside the City pay more: **$86** instead of $75 for a Tier 1 load of general refuse [[sd-feepdf]].

## Hiring someone to haul it

San Diego Municipal Code §66.0108 bars anyone from collecting and hauling solid waste in the City for hire without a **City franchise agreement**, and their vehicles must carry a City-issued tag or decal [[sd-6601]]. You can always haul your own fridge to a licensed facility yourself [[sd-6601]].

## Is there an SDG&E rebate?

Not that SDG&E's website shows right now. Its **Rebates** page lists offers for smart thermostats, heat pump water heaters and room air conditioners, and **no refrigerator recycling or pickup program** [[sdge-reb]].

## What about the Freon?

Under federal rules, the **final person in the disposal chain**, such as a scrap metal recycler or landfill, is responsible for making sure refrigerant is recovered before a household refrigerator or freezer is disposed of. If an appliance arrives with its refrigerant already gone, that facility must keep a **signed statement** from whoever dropped it off saying who recovered it and when [[epa-608]].
