## The short version

The City of San Diego doesn't currently pick up bulky items. A bulky item pickup service was planned, but the City **paused it until further notice** when it lowered solid waste fees for fiscal years 2028 and 2029 [[sd-feeupd]] [[sd-faq]]. The City's own mattress page puts it plainly: it "does not provide bulky item pickup or curbside pick-up services" [[sd-mattress]].

Your regular trash, recycling and organics pickup continues as normal [[sd-faq]]. But big items can't ride along: the City says furniture, appliances and auto parts (water heaters, major appliances, couches and chairs) and **anything over 50 pounds** must never go in any City bin [[sd-general]].

So you'll need to drop it off, catch a community cleanup event, or hire a hauler. Here's how each works.

## Option 1: Mattresses and box springs (free drop-off)

Mattresses are the easiest. Through the Bye Bye Mattress program, the City takes mattresses and box springs **for free** at the Miramar Landfill [[sd-mattress]]:

- **Where:** Mattress Collection Site, east side of the entrance at **5161 Convoy St., San Diego, CA 92111**, next to the Household Hazardous Waste Transfer Facility.
- **When:** Monday to Friday, **7 a.m. to 3 p.m.** Closed Saturdays, Sundays and six holidays (New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas).
- **Limit:** none.
- **Futons:** futon mattresses count, but only if they come off the frame.

Not accepted: mattresses that are severely damaged, wet, twisted, frozen, soiled or infested with bedbugs; toppers and pads; air mattresses, waterbeds and rollaways; crib mattresses; sofa beds and futon frames; and mattresses still in a box or plastic bag [[sd-mattress]]. The City says mattresses too soiled to recycle go in the trash, and landfill fees may apply if you bring a mattress in with other waste [[sd-mattress]].

The Bye Bye Mattress program doesn't do curbside pickup either. It's a network of drop-off points and recyclers; the City links to its [find-a-facility map](https://byebyemattress.com/find-a-facility/) for other locations [[sd-mattress]].

## Option 2: Haul it to Miramar Landfill

For couches, tables, carpet and general junk, the City's landfill is the main option. **Miramar Landfill**, 5180 Convoy St., is open **Monday to Saturday, 7 a.m. to 4 p.m.**, closed Sundays and the same six holidays [[sd-miramar]]. Self-dumping vehicles are accepted until 4:30 p.m. [[sd-miramar]], but there are **no hand unloads after 4 p.m.** [[sd-fees]].

### What it costs (July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027)

Cars, pickups and small trailers pay a flat rate per load based on the vehicle and the kind of waste [[sd-fees]]. For general refuse [[sd-feepdf]]:

- **Tier 1** (car, sedan, crossover, SUV, minivan, pickup, van, or any trailer up to 5 ft W x 9 ft L x 4 ft H): **$75** for waste from inside the City, **$86** from outside it.
- **Tier 2** (pickup with trailer, larger trailers, medium-heavy trucks): **$148** City, **$156** non-City.
- Construction and demolition debris costs more: **$108** per Tier 1 load.

### Rules to know before you go

- **Cover your load.** Uncovered loads are charged an extra **$50** [[sd-fees]].
- **No appliances, TVs, electronics or hazardous waste** in your landfill load. They must be disposed of properly before you enter the landfill [[sd-fees]]. See Option 3.
- **Payment:** checks, major credit cards and Visa or Mastercard debit cards with matching ID [[sd-fees]]. Cash is no longer accepted [[sd-feepdf]].
- **Bad weather:** some vehicles and trailers may be restricted in rain. Recorded information: **858-492-6100** [[sd-feepdf]].
- **Fee questions:** Miramar Recycling Center, **858-268-8971** [[sd-fees]].

## Option 3: Appliances, TVs and electronics

Take these to the **Miramar Recycling Center**, at the landfill entrance at **5165 Convoy St.** It's open Monday to Saturday, 7 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. [[sd-recycle]]. Posted prices, which the City says are subject to change [[sd-recycle]]:

- **Refrigerators, freezers, air conditioners** (Freon appliances): **$25 each**
- **Washers, dryers, dishwashers, stoves, microwaves, water heaters** and other appliances: **$12 each**
- **TVs, computer monitors, CPUs and laptops:** free
- **Small electronics** like vacuums and coffee makers: $0.25 per pound (49-pound limit per item)
- **Tires** (18 inches and under, no rims): $7 each

Call **858-268-8971** to confirm pricing and accepted items before you go [[sd-recycle]].

## Option 4: Community cleanup events

The City runs **nearly 100 community cleanup and recycling events a year**, organized with each City Council district, where residents can get rid of unwanted bulky items and other accepted materials [[sd-illegal]]. The City says council offices and staff pick the neighborhoods most in need, so check with your council district office for upcoming dates near you [[sd-illegal]].

## Option 5: Don't have a truck? Hire a hauler

If you can't haul it yourself, you'll need a private junk removal company. If you live on a private road, in a multifamily property, or anywhere already served by a private waste hauler, the City says to use a City-approved private franchise hauler [[sd-general]]; the City publishes a [franchised hauler list](https://www.sandiego.gov/sites/default/files/esd-franchised-hauler-list.pdf) [[sd-miramar]].

## Don't leave it on the curb

Putting furniture, mattresses, appliances or other waste on a street, sidewalk, park or someone else's property without permission is **illegal dumping** under San Diego Municipal Code section 54.0209 [[sd-illegal]]. Fines run **up to $1,000 per violation**, and each day the waste stays can count as a separate violation [[sd-illegal]].

If you spot dumped items, report them through the City's [Get It Done illegal dumping form](https://getitdone.sandiego.gov/TSWNewReport?type=Illegal%20Dumping) or call **858-694-7000**. After hours, on weekends or holidays, urgent hazards go to **619-527-7500** [[sd-illegal]].
