## The short version

If you live in the **City of San Diego**, paint, batteries, motor oil, pesticides, cleaners and other household hazardous waste go to the City's **Household Hazardous Waste (HHW) Transfer Facility** at the Miramar Landfill. Drop-off is **free**, but **every visit needs an appointment**, and it's open only **Wednesdays and Saturdays** [[sd-hhw]] [[sd-pr]].

**Electronics are different.** The HHW facility won't take them [[sd-hhw]]. Take TVs, monitors, computers and laptops to the **Miramar Recycling Center** next door, where they're free to recycle [[sd-mrc]].

## Option 1: The HHW Transfer Facility (by appointment)

### Where and when

[[sd-hhw]]

- **Address:** Miramar Landfill entrance, **5161 Convoy St., San Diego, CA 92111**, north of State Route 52
- **Hours:** **Wednesday and Saturday, 9 a.m.-3 p.m.**, except holidays
- **Upcoming closures:** **November 28, 2026**, **December 26, 2026** and **January 2, 2027**

Expect traffic delays: the facility shares its entrance with the landfill and recycling center [[sd-hhw]].

### How to book

1. Go to the City's **Get It Done HHW appointment page** ([sandiego.gov/HHW](https://getitdone.sandiego.gov/HazmatNewReportPage)) [[sd-pr]] [[sd-appt]].
2. Check off the types of waste you're bringing and enter the **residential address** where the waste is [[sd-appt]].
3. Pick a time between **9 a.m. and 3 p.m.** [[sd-appt]].

**Need help instead?** On the same form you can ask for help if you have **more than 15 gallons or 125 pounds**, **can't transport it**, don't know what it is, or have **industrial-grade chemicals**. The City says it will contact you within **3 business days** [[sd-appt]].

### Who can use it and what it costs

- **Free** for **City of San Diego residents** [[sd-hhw]].
- Bring **proof of City residency**: a picture ID or a recent bill showing your name and address [[sd-hhw]] [[sd-appt]].
- **No business waste** (contractors, landscapers, homeowner associations) and no waste from nonprofits [[sd-hhw]].
- **Not a City resident?** Unincorporated San Diego County residents can call **877-713-2784**; residents of other cities should contact their own city [[sd-hhw]].

### What it takes

Leftover or unused products labeled **Caution, Warning, Danger, Poison, Toxic, Flammable or Corrosive**, used for routine home, yard or vehicle maintenance [[sd-hhw]]. The City's list includes [[sd-pr]] [[sd-hhw]]:

- **Paint** (unused and surplus)
- **Motor oil** and oil filters, antifreeze
- **Batteries**: all types of home and automotive batteries
- Pesticides, pool and garden chemicals
- Household cleaners and aerosols
- **Fluorescent tubes, CFLs and LED bulbs** (incandescent bulbs can go in the trash)
- Needles, lancets and sharps
- Prescription and over-the-counter medications (**no prescription painkillers**)
- **Propane cylinders** and fire extinguishers

### What it won't take

[[sd-hhw]]

- **Electronics** and **appliances**
- Swollen or reacting batteries
- Treated wood, asbestos, lead paint waste
- **Containers larger than 5 gallons**
- Compressed gas cylinders other than propane and fire extinguishers (no oxygen, SCUBA, welding, refrigerant or CO2 tanks)
- Explosives (ammunition, fireworks, marine flares), radioactive material, PCB capacitors and transformers

For anything on that list, enter your information on the appointment form and an inspector will contact you within three business days [[sd-hhw]].

### Limits and what to expect

- **Maximum 15 gallons or 125 pounds per trip.** The 15 gallons means the actual contents, not the container size [[sd-hhw]]. You certify on the booking form that you won't exceed it [[sd-appt]].
- **Stay in your vehicle.** Staff unload for you [[sd-appt]] [[sd-pr]].
- Keep the materials **in the trunk or truck bed**, not the passenger cabin [[sd-hhw]].
- Call the Environmental Services Department at **858-694-7000** about loads over 50 pounds, leaky or open containers, products you can't identify, or **special services for elderly or disabled residents** without transportation [[sd-hhw]].

## Option 2: One-day collection events (oil, batteries, bulbs)

The City runs free one-day collection events, **9 a.m.-1 p.m.**, for a short list of items: **used oil, oil filters, auto batteries, household batteries and fluorescent bulbs**. No other waste is accepted [[sd-events]]. Upcoming dates [[sd-events]]:

- **Saturday, December 19, 2026:** Balboa Park, Inspiration Parking Lot (Park Blvd. and Presidents Way, 92101)
- **Saturday, February 13, 2027:** San Diego Miramar College, 10440 Black Mountain Rd., Parking Lot 2 (92126)
- **Saturday, March 13, 2027:** South Shores, 404 Sea World Dr. (92109)

## Electronics: the Miramar Recycling Center

The Miramar Recycling Center sits at the same landfill entrance, **5165 Convoy St.**, and is run for the City by Allan Company [[sd-mrc]].

- **Hours:** Monday-Saturday, **7 a.m.-4:30 p.m.**; closed Sunday and major holidays including Thanksgiving and Christmas [[sd-mrc]]
- **Free:** computer monitors, CPUs, laptops and **televisions** [[sd-mrc]]
- **Small electronics** (coffee makers, vacuums) and **small computer devices** (printers, keyboards, mice): **$0.25 per pound**, 49-pound limit per item [[sd-mrc]]
- **Appliances:** refrigerators, freezers and air conditioners **$25 each**; dishwashers, dryers, water heaters, microwaves, stoves and washers **$12 each** [[sd-mrc]]
- Prices can change; call **858-268-8971** to confirm [[sd-mrc]]

## Paint, batteries and medications: other drop-offs

- **Paint:** besides the HHW facility, you can drop off paint free at **[PaintCare California](https://www.paintcare.org/drop-off-locations/)** sites. Most take up to 5 gallons, in containers of 5 gallons or smaller, and won't take leaking, unlabeled or empty cans [[sd-hhw]].
- **Batteries:** the City points to **[Call2Recycle](https://www.call2recycle.org/consumers/)** drop-off locations, including for e-bike batteries. Store used batteries by type, tape the terminals, and bag any that leak [[sd-hhw]].
- **Medications:** the San Diego Police Department runs a [prescription drug drop-off program](https://www.sandiego.gov/police/community/prescription-drug-drop-off) [[sd-hhw]].

## Why you can't just toss it

Disposing of hazardous waste in the sewer, storm drain, ground or trash is **illegal**, the City says [[sd-hhw]]. Home-generated sharps have been banned from trash, recycling and green waste containers under California law since **Sept. 1, 2008** [[sd-hhw]].
