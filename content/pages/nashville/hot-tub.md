## The short version

Nashville's main official option for an old hot tub is the **Davidson County Sheriff's Office bulk item removal**. It's on the list of items crews remove, with one condition: the **hot tub must be in two pieces** [[dcso-bulk]]. Call **615-880-3897** to get a date [[dcso-contact]], and have both pieces at the **front curb by 6:30 a.m.** [[dcso-bulk]].

If you can't cut it up, can't get it to the front curb, or live somewhere crews can't go, a **junk-removal crew** is the usual answer. Metro's Convenience Centers charge for bulky loads by volume, but their pages don't mention spas, so call before you haul one there [[nws-cc]].

## Option 1: Sheriff's Office curbside pickup

1. **Drain it and cut it into two pieces.** The Sheriff's Office says a hot tub must be in two pieces for pickup [[dcso-bulk]].
2. **Call 615-880-3897.** You don't need the Sheriff's website contact form for bulk item removal [[dcso-contact]]. The homeowner or tenant has to make the request. You'll be asked to list the items, the service address, a phone number and the resident's name [[dcso-bulk]]. Mention the hot tub by name.
3. **Front curb by 6:30 a.m.** Crews remove items from the **front curb only** [[dcso-bulk]].
4. **Give it time.** Once you have a date, crews have **up to seven business days** from it to remove the items, not counting weekends and holidays [[dcso-bulk]].

### What the crews won't take with it

Keep these out of the pile [[dcso-bulk]]:

- **Household or hazardous chemicals**, including leftover spa chemicals
- **Glass or mirrored items**
- **Sheet rock/drywall** and **roofing**
- **Sand, dirt, rocks or concrete**, such as a concrete pad under the tub
- **Batteries** and **propane or gas tanks**
- **Anything in bags**

Wood from a deck around the tub may qualify: **lumber**, **fencing** and **construction debris** are on the accepted list [[dcso-bulk]]. List everything when you call.

### Who can't use it

- **Apartment, condo and townhome complexes, private roads, gated communities and business properties:** crews are prohibited there. Residents of complexes are asked to put items at the front curb by the main entrance [[dcso-bulk]].
- **After an eviction:** the Sheriff's Office is prohibited by law from removing bulk items that resulted from an eviction [[dcso-bulk]].

More on this service: [Nashville bulk trash pickup guide](/nashville/bulk-pickup/).

## Option 2: Haul it to a Convenience Center (call first)

Metro says items too bulky for your curbside cart should go to a **Convenience Center** [[nws-bulky]]. Its fee table doesn't list hot tubs. Items not listed by material type are **charged by volume** [[nws-cc]]:

- **Less than 1 cubic yard:** **$10**, no higher than the level of the truck bed
- **Between 1 and 8 cubic yards:** **$20 per cubic yard**. No construction or demolition material, no trailers longer than 12 feet, and no cargo vans or trailers at Anderson Lane

Because spas aren't named, call Metro's Waste Wizard line at **615-924-2633** before you load one [[nws-cc]].

**Before you go** [[nws-cc]]:

- Bring a **Tennessee driver's license** showing you live in Davidson County.
- You **unload everything yourself**.
- **No commercial vehicles** (including any with company advertising or commercial tags) and no commercial waste.
- Cash (not at Omohundro), check or card. Cards add **2.8%, minimum $1.95**.

**Locations,** all open **Tuesday to Saturday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.** and closed on Metro holidays [[nws-hours]]:

- **Anderson Lane:** 939A Anderson Ln, Madison, TN 37115
- **East:** 943A Doctor Richard G. Adams Dr, Nashville, TN 37207
- **Ezell Pike:** 3254 Ezell Pk, Nashville, TN 37211
- **Omohundro:** 1019 Omohundro Pl, Nashville, TN 37210

Tearing out a deck or patio along with the tub? Metro points larger amounts of construction and demolition material to private disposal sites. See [How to get rid of construction debris in Nashville](/nashville/construction-debris/) [[nws-bulky]].

## Leftover spa chemicals

**Pool chemicals** are on Metro's household hazardous waste list. Drop them off **at no charge** at the **East** or **Ezell Pike** Convenience Center, up to **15 gallons or 100 pounds** per household per month. Questions: **615-862-5000** [[nws-hhw]]. Full details: [Nashville hazardous waste and electronics drop-off](/nashville/hazardous-waste-electronics/).

## Can I donate it?

Not to Goodwill: Goodwill Industries of Middle Tennessee lists **outdoor playground and pool equipment, including hot tubs**, among items it can't accept [[gw-items]]. A working spa may be easier to sell or give away locally to someone who'll move it.
