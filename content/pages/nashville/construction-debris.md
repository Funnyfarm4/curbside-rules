## The short version

Construction debris is **banned from Metro Nashville trash carts**. Metro says residential construction waste must be collected by a **private hauler or contractor**, and only **small do-it-yourself amounts** can go to the Omohundro Convenience Center [[nws-trash]].

Your realistic options are: drop a **small DIY load at Omohundro** [[nws-cc]], set out the **few remodel items the Sheriff's curbside pickup takes** [[dcso-bulk]], take **bigger loads to a private C&D facility** [[nws-bulky]], **donate** reusable materials [[hab-donate]], or **hire a crew** (or rent a dumpster) to load and haul it. If a dumpster will sit on the street, see [Do you need a dumpster permit in Nashville?](/nashville/dumpster-permit/).

## Option 1: Omohundro Convenience Center (small DIY loads)

Of Metro's four convenience centers, only **Omohundro** takes construction and demolition material [[nws-cc]] [[nws-bulky]].

- **Where:** Omohundro Convenience Center, **1019 Omohundro Pl, Nashville, TN 37210** [[nws-hours]]
- **When:** Tuesday to Saturday, **8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.**; closed on Metro holidays [[nws-hours]]
- **Fee:** **$20**, one visit per day [[nws-cc]]
- **Size limit:** the bulky item page limits residents to **1 cubic yard** [[nws-bulky]]; the convenience center fee table says loads can't exceed 2 cubic yards [[nws-cc]].
- **Payment:** check or any major credit or debit card (cards carry a 2.8% fee, minimum $1.95). **Cash isn't accepted at Omohundro** [[nws-cc]].
- **Who can use it:** Davidson County residents with a **Tennessee driver's license** showing they live in the county. You unload your own material, and **commercial vehicles** (including any vehicle with business advertising or commercial tags) and commercial waste aren't allowed [[nws-cc]].

> **Heads-up on the limit:** Metro's two pages disagree (1 vs. 2 cubic yards). We show the stricter 1 cubic yard; call Metro at **615-862-5000** before hauling more [[nws-bulky]] [[nws-cc]].

**Not accepted at any convenience center** [[nws-cc]]: asbestos (shingles, flooring and so on), **brick, concrete, masonry, rock, rebar and dirt**, **roofing shingles**, **siding**, gutters, **plumbing**, pallets, posts, poles, chain-link and railroad ties.

The free daily allowance (3 bags of trash and 1 bulk item) **excludes construction and demolition material**, and the per-yard volume pricing for 1 to 8 cubic yards says **no construction or demolition material** [[nws-cc]].

## Option 2: Davidson County Sheriff's curbside pickup (some items)

The Davidson County Sheriff's Office removes bulk items from the **front curb**, and its authorized list includes **construction debris, lumber, fencing, pallets, shipping crates, toilets and water heaters**, plus a mailbox or fence post with its concrete footing [[dcso-bulk]].

It's **not authorized** to take [[dcso-bulk]]:

- **Sheet rock/drywall**
- **Roofing**
- **Glass or mirrored items**
- **Sand, dirt, rocks or concrete**
- **Paint cans** (even empty), household chemicals and tires
- **Anything in bags**

**How to book:** call **615-880-3897**; you don't need the online contact form [[dcso-contact]]. The homeowner or tenant must call and give the items, address, phone number and resident's name. Set items at the front curb by **6:30 a.m.**; crews have up to **seven business days** from your date [[dcso-bulk]]. Crews can't go onto apartment, condo or townhome complexes, private roads, gated communities or business property [[dcso-bulk]].

## Option 3: Larger loads to a private C&D facility

For more than a DIY load, Metro lists these sites and says to **contact each for fees** [[nws-bulky]]:

- **Triune Residuals Management (Hermitage),** 3516 Central Pike, Hermitage, TN 37076, at the MS-COT landfill, **615-573-8004**. Takes construction and demolition debris from building, remodeling, repair and demolition, including **roofing shingles, glass, lumber, brick, concrete and masonry, scrap metal, soil, rock and rebar** [[nws-bulky]].
- **American Farms,** 2132 Smith Springs Rd, Nashville, TN 37217, **615-573-0293**. Takes **dirt, clay, topsoil, rock, gravel, concrete (with or without rebar), brick and block**, plus trees, stumps, brush and other green waste [[nws-bulky]].
- **Middle Tennessee Environmental Center (MTEC),** 4651 Amy Lynn Drive, Nashville, TN 37218, at WM's Southern Services Landfill [[nws-bulky]].

Metro also links a list of other recycling processors for **source-separated** loads such as metal or concrete; some may take commercial loads only [[nws-bulky]].

## Option 4: Donate reusable materials to Habitat ReStore

Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville's ReStore takes building materials in good condition [[hab-donate]]:

- **Lumber 6 feet or longer** and **trim 4 feet or longer**
- **Plywood and sheetrock/drywall** in full or half sheets
- **Rolled roofing, metal roofing and bundled shingles** in their **original packaging** only
- **Doors** in good condition, and **kitchen cabinets and vanities** with doors and shelving and no damage
- **New** tile, hardwood, vinyl or ceramic flooring in original packaging (at least 100 square feet of one style)
- Full, unused cans of **water-based paint** less than a year old

It **won't take** building materials with **nails, screws, staples or hooks**, damaged or water-damaged doors, or countertops without their base cabinets [[hab-donate]].

**Drop off** at the Nashville ReStore, **414 Harding Place, Ste 110, Nashville, TN 37211** (615-942-1291), **Tuesday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.** [[hab-restore]]. **Free pickup** is offered for large or heavy items on the ground floor (disassembled); in Davidson County, book online or call the donation hotline at **615-942-1290**. Pickups generally happen within two weeks [[hab-donate]].

**Remodeling a kitchen?** Habitat's **DeConstruct** crews remove cabinets, claw-foot tubs, doors and other donated building materials **at no charge** in its five-county area. Email **deconstruction@habitatnashville.org** to schedule [[hab-restore]]. Metro's waste department also encourages deconstruction and reuse over demolition, and flags **lead and asbestos** as common hazards to identify first [[nws-decon]].

## Paint and other leftovers

- **Latex or water-based paint:** dry it out (leave the lid off or add cat litter) before it goes in the trash [[nws-trash]].
- **Oil-based paint, stains, solvents and gasoline:** free at Metro's two household hazardous waste sites (East and Ezell Pike), up to 15 gallons or 100 pounds per visit [[nws-trash]] [[nws-cc]]. See [Nashville hazardous waste and electronics drop-off](/nashville/hazardous-waste-electronics/).
- **Scrap metal and old appliances:** one bulk scrap metal item free at a convenience center [[nws-bulky]].
