## The short version

If you're putting a dumpster, roll-off, trailer or storage container in the **Metro Nashville right-of-way**, you need a permit from the **Nashville Department of Transportation and Multimodal Infrastructure (NDOT)**. It costs **$10 a day, up to $2,000**, and putting one out without a permit carries a **$30-a-day** violation fee [[ndot-tsc]].

You apply on NDOT's **ePermits** site at least **2 business days** before the container arrives [[ndot-permits]], and every application needs a **traffic control plan** [[ndot-tcg]].

## Which permit applies

NDOT lists a **Trailer / Dumpster** fee for "placing a trailer, dumpster, or storage unit in the Metro right-of-way (ROW)" [[ndot-tsc]]:

- **Permit fee:** $10 per day, not to exceed $2,000
- **Violation fee:** $30 per day

If the container also closes a **traffic lane, sidewalk or bike lane**, NDOT's closure permits apply. Each is **$160 for up to 5 days, plus $10 for each day after the 5th**. Closing without a permit triggers a **triple fee of $480** (plus $30 a day after 5 days), and permit holders who break the rules can be charged **$50 per violation per day** [[ndot-tsc]].

If your work needs more than one type of right-of-way permit, NDOT says you may qualify for a single **unified right-of-way permit**; email the NDOT permits office to ask [[ndot-tsc]].

## How to apply

1. **Apply on ePermits.** NDOT says all right-of-way permits must be applied for through ePermits (ePermits.nashville.gov) [[ndot-permits]].
2. **Apply early.** Permits must be applied for **at least 2 business days** before the actual start date [[ndot-permits]].
3. **Attach a traffic control plan.** NDOT requires one with **every** permit submittal, even when no lane is closed, plus pedestrian and cyclist control wherever a sidewalk, crosswalk or bike lane is affected [[ndot-tcg]]. NDOT's list of standard layouts includes a **Layout for a Dumpster**, showing a container in a parking lane and in a travel lane [[ndot-tcg]] [[ndot-layout]]. A site-specific, signed and sealed plan may be required if the standard layout doesn't match your street [[ndot-tcg]].
4. **Pay once approved.** Payment is due in full after the application is approved, and no permit is issued until it's paid. Fees are **non-refundable**. NDOT takes cash, check (payable to "Metropolitan Government"), Visa or MasterCard [[ndot-permits]], with a **2.3% convenience fee** on card payments [[ndot-permits]].

## What the layout requires on the street

NDOT's dumpster layout carries general notes that apply to all its standard plans [[ndot-layout]]:

- Channelizing devices (cones, drums and the like) must follow the federal Manual on Uniform Traffic Control Devices.
- Traffic control devices must have **working, visible warning lights**.
- The contractor must provide a sidewalk or crosswalk closure, or a walkway bypass, wherever pedestrians are affected.
- The City Traffic Engineer or inspectors may require more devices.

## Contact

- **NDOT permits office:** 615-862-8782, or use the email link on NDOT's permits page [[ndot-permits]]
- **NDOT main line:** 615-862-5000, Monday to Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. [[ndot-tsc]]

## Remodeling? Debris can't go in your Metro trash cart

Metro says **residential construction waste must be collected by a private hauler or contractor**, which is the usual reason people rent a dumpster. Small do-it-yourself amounts can go to the **Omohundro Convenience Center** [[nws-trash]], at **$20 for up to 2 cubic yards** of construction and demolition material [[nws-cc]].
