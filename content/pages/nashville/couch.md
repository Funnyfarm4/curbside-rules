## The short version

You have two free official ways to get rid of a couch in Nashville:

1. **Free curbside pickup from the Davidson County Sheriff's Office.** Sofas, couches and old furniture are on its list [[dcso-bulk]]. Call **615-880-3897** to schedule [[dcso-contact]].
2. **Drop it off at a Metro Convenience Center.** Your first bulk item each day is **free** [[nws-cc]].

Metro asks residents to consider **donating** usable furniture first, through secondhand stores or online marketplaces [[nws-bulky]].

## Option 1: Free Sheriff's Office pickup

1. **Call 615-880-3897.** You don't need the Sheriff's online contact form for bulk removal [[dcso-contact]]. You'll be asked what you're setting out, the address, a phone number and the resident's name. The homeowner or tenant has to make the request [[dcso-bulk]].
2. **Put it at the front curb by 6:30 a.m.** on your date. Front curb only [[dcso-bulk]].
3. **Nothing in bags.** Take cushions out of trash bags, and keep **glass or mirrored** pieces (like a glass coffee table top or a mirror) out of the pile, since crews won't take them [[dcso-bulk]].
4. **Give it time.** Crews have up to **seven business days** from your date, not counting weekends and holidays [[dcso-bulk]].

The same pickup also takes **lawn furniture, baby furniture, mattresses, rugs and carpet**, so you can clear out a room in one call [[dcso-bulk]].

**Apartment, condo or townhome?** Crews can't come onto private complexes, private roads, gated communities or business property. Put the couch at the front curb by the main entrance instead [[dcso-bulk]].

**After an eviction:** the Sheriff's Office is prohibited by law from removing bulk items that resulted from an eviction [[dcso-bulk]].

For the full list of what the Sheriff's crews take and don't take, see our [Nashville bulk trash pickup guide](/nashville/bulk-pickup/).

## Option 2: Drop it off at a Convenience Center

Metro lists **couches, chairs and sofas**, dressers, mirrors and tables as bulk items it accepts for disposal [[nws-bulky]]. All four Convenience Centers take bulk items and are open **Tuesday to Saturday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.**, closed on Metro holidays [[nws-hours]]:

- **Anderson Lane:** 939A Anderson Ln, Madison, TN 37115
- **East:** 943A Doctor Richard G. Adams Dr, Nashville, TN 37207
- **Ezell Pike:** 3254 Ezell Pk, Nashville, TN 37211
- **Omohundro:** 1019 Omohundro Pl, Nashville, TN 37210

### What it costs

[[nws-cc]]

- **Free:** one bulk item plus up to three bags of trash (30 gallons max each), once a day.
- **Extra furniture is charged by volume:** under 1 cubic yard (no higher than the truck bed) is **$10**; between 1 and 8 cubic yards is **$20 per cubic yard**. No trailers longer than 12 feet, and no cargo vans or trailers at Anderson Lane.
- Cash (not at Omohundro), check or card. Cards add **2.8%, minimum $1.95**. Metro says prices are non-negotiable.

### Before you go

[[nws-cc]]

- Bring a **Tennessee driver's license** showing you live in Davidson County.
- You **unload it yourself**.
- **No commercial vehicles,** including any vehicle with company advertising or commercial tags, and no commercial waste.

## Mattress going too?

Mattresses aren't covered by the free daily bulk item at Convenience Centers; they're **$12 each** [[nws-cc]]. The Sheriff's curbside pickup takes them free [[dcso-bulk]]. See our [Nashville mattress guide](/nashville/mattress/).
