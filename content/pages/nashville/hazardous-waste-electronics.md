## The short version

Davidson County residents can drop off household chemicals **free** at two Metro Convenience Centers, **East** and **Ezell Pike**, up to 15 gallons or 100 pounds per household per month [[nws-hhw]]. Old electronics go free to **East, Ezell Pike or Omohundro**, up to 100 pounds a day [[nws-ewaste]].

All four Convenience Centers are open **Tuesday to Saturday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.**, and closed on Metro holidays [[nws-hours]].

## Where to go

- **East Convenience Center:** 943A Doctor Richard G. Adams Dr, Nashville, TN 37207 [[nws-hhw]]
- **Ezell Pike Convenience Center:** 3254 Ezell Pk, Nashville, TN 37211 [[nws-hhw]]
- **Omohundro Convenience Center:** 1019 Omohundro Pl, Nashville, TN 37210 (electronics and cooking grease) [[nws-ewaste]] [[nws-hhw]]

The Anderson Lane center in Madison doesn't take hazardous waste or electronics [[nws-cc]].

## Rules at every center

- Show a **Tennessee driver's license** with a Davidson County address [[nws-cc]].
- **Unload it yourself** [[nws-cc]].
- **No commercial vehicles**, including any vehicle with commercial advertising or commercial tags, and no commercial waste [[nws-cc]].
- **One visit a day** for each of hazardous waste and electronics [[nws-cc]].

## Hazardous waste: what you can bring

Metro's accepted list includes [[nws-hhw]]:

- **Paint and finishes:** oil-based paint, stain, varnish, paint thinner, adhesives and caulk
- **Car fluids:** motor oil, antifreeze, brake fluid, transmission fluid, gasoline and windshield wiper fluid
- **Batteries:** rechargeable, automobile and lead-acid
- **Bulbs:** fluorescent tubes and CFLs
- **Yard and pest products:** pesticides, insecticide, weed killer, rat and mouse poison, flea collars, pet spray and dip
- **Household cleaners:** bleach, drain opener, oven cleaner, toilet bowl and tub cleaners, detergent, spot remover
- **Other:** pool chemicals, lighter fluid, mercury thermometers, mothballs, shoe polish, car wax and cooking grease

**Limit:** 15 gallons or 100 pounds per household per month [[nws-hhw]].

### What they won't take

- Latex or acrylic paint and water-based stains
- Propane tanks
- Unused or expired medications
- Medical waste (Metro says needles can go in a red sharps container or a lidded two-liter bottle in your trash)
- Ammunition and explosives
- Business or industrial hazardous waste, or anything in a commercial or work vehicle
- Waste from outside Davidson County [[nws-hhw]]

### How to transport it

Keep products in their original containers when you can. Otherwise use a plastic jug with a secure lid and label what's in it. Don't mix liquids. Pack containers in sturdy boxes or flat-bottom tote bags lined with newspaper to stop spills [[nws-hhw]].

## Latex paint goes in the trash (once it's dry)

Leave the lid off or mix in cat litter until the paint is dry, then put the container in your trash. Containers with liquid in them must be emptied first [[nws-trash]].

## Electronics: what you can bring

Up to **100 pounds a day** of household electronics, free, with valid Davidson County ID [[nws-ewaste]]. Per visit that can include [[nws-ewaste]]:

- 1 computer system (computer and monitor), limited to one a month
- 1 TV, no wider than 80 inches
- 1 microwave, coffee machine or other kitchen appliance
- 1 desktop copier, printer or scanner
- 1 radio, CD player, DVD or VCR player, or stereo (not a cabinet system)
- Phones, tablets, e-readers, keyboards, mice, cables, external drives, string lights, hair dryers and other small electronics

Electronics from businesses, truck or trailer loads, and electronics from out-of-county residents aren't accepted. Businesses should contact local e-waste recyclers, and some retailers run drop-off or take-back programs [[nws-ewaste]].

## Other things these centers handle

East and Ezell also take tires (free without rims, $5 each with rims, four a month), and East, Ezell and Omohundro take mattresses and box springs ($12 each, four a month) [[nws-cc]]. See our [Nashville mattress guide](/nashville/mattress/), [Nashville couch guide](/nashville/couch/) and [Nashville bulk pickup guide](/nashville/bulk-pickup/) for furniture and big items.

## If you have more than one carload

Every center lets you drop off 3 bags of trash and 1 bulk item free per day. Beyond that, loads are charged by volume: $10 for less than a cubic yard, or $20 per cubic yard for 1 to 8 cubic yards. Card payments carry a 2.8% fee (minimum $1.95) [[nws-cc]]. You still have to load, drive and unload everything yourself [[nws-cc]].
