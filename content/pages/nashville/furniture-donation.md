## The short version

Three Nashville organizations will send a truck for good furniture: **Habitat ReStore** and **Goodwill Middle Tennessee** both offer **free pickup**, and **ThriftSmart** takes free large-item pickup requests [[hab-donate]] [[gw-pickup]] [[ts-donate]]. The catch: free pickups are **ground-floor only**, can take **up to two weeks**, and the furniture has to be clean, undamaged and sellable [[hab-donate]] [[gw-pickup]] [[ts-donate]].

Metro itself says to consider donating furniture before sending it to the landfill [[nws-bulky]]. If no one wants it, Metro's convenience centers and the Sheriff's curbside pickup are the fallback [[nws-cc]] [[dcso-bulk]].

## Habitat for Humanity ReStore (free pickup)

Habitat for Humanity of Greater Nashville offers **free pickup for large or heavy items located on the ground floor**. Items must be **disassembled**, and the donation ambassador has final say on what's accepted [[hab-donate]].

- **Book in Davidson County** online or by calling the Nashville donation hotline at **615-942-1290** [[hab-donate]].
- **Timing:** scheduling depends on demand, but pickups **generally happen within two weeks** [[hab-donate]].
- **Priority paid pickup:** through Habitat's partner **ReSupply**, for items that need to go **within 48 hours**, need disassembly, or **aren't on the first floor**. ReSupply charges a fee that depends on the number of items [[hab-donate]].
- **Furniture it takes:** living room, dining room, bedroom and outdoor/patio furniture; **complete** bed frames (headboard, footboard, side rails and hardware); office furniture such as file cabinets, credenzas and office chairs [[hab-donate]].
- **Appliances:** 7 years old or newer, clean and fully working with all parts; **no gas appliances or dishwashers** [[hab-donate]].
- **Won't take:** cribs, highchairs and other infant items; headboards on their own; cubicles and office systems; and mattresses or box springs unless **new in the packaging** [[hab-donate]].

**Drop off** at the Nashville ReStore, **414 Harding Place, Ste 110, Nashville, TN 37211** (615-942-1291), **Tuesday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.** [[hab-restore]].

## Goodwill Industries of Middle Tennessee (free pickup with a minimum)

Goodwill offers **free pickup** from your home or business if you have a minimum of **5 boxes or bags** of home goods and clothing and/or **3-4 acceptable furniture pieces** [[gw-pickup]].

- **Schedule** online or call **615-425-0100** [[gw-pickup]].
- **Service area:** parts of Davidson, Williamson, Rutherford, Sumner, Wilson, Montgomery and Madison counties [[gw-pickup]].
- **Condition:** gently used or working, with no damage, breaks, tears, stains or pet hair [[gw-pickup]].
- **Moving or estate?** Goodwill also runs moving pickups and **free estate pickups** for families settling an estate or downsizing [[gw-pickup]].
- **Won't take** [[gw-items]]: mattresses, box springs, **sleeper sofas** and futon cushions; broken, damaged or stained furniture; cribs and other baby furniture; large corporate-style furniture (L- and U-shaped desks, conference tables, cubicles); hospital beds and waterbeds; pianos; standard-size pool tables; and hot tubs.

Goodwill says furniture acceptance at its drop-off centers depends on local space and needs, so call your nearest donation center before hauling a piece in [[gw-items]].

## ThriftSmart (free large-item pickup)

ThriftSmart takes **free large item or bulk donation pickup** requests through a form on its donation page and says it will reach out **within 24 hours** to schedule [[ts-donate]].

- **Furniture it typically takes:** couches, sectionals, recliners, futons, dressers, chests, armoires, dining tables and chairs, bookshelves, coffee and end tables, complete beds, **mattresses and box springs**, cribs (not drop-side), rugs and outdoor furniture [[ts-donate]].
- **Won't take:** sleeper sofas, large office desks and conference tables, L-shaped desks, pianos, TV armoires and large entertainment centers, hospital beds, gas appliances and dishwashers [[ts-donate]].
- **Pickup rules:** everything must be on the **ground floor, no more than five steps** into the home (apartment buildings need an elevator that fits everything), donations should be packed in bags or boxes before the driver arrives, and beds must be fully disassembled with hardware bagged. Drivers can refuse anything broken, stained, ripped, smelly or covered in pet fur [[ts-donate]].
- **Drop off** at the Tusculum store, **4890 Nolensville Road, Nashville 37211**, or Rivergate, **2117 Gallatin Pk N, Madison 37115**: Monday to Saturday 9 a.m. to 8 p.m., Sunday noon to 6 p.m. [[ts-donate]].

## If no charity will take it: the Metro options

### Free convenience center drop-off

Davidson County residents can bring **3 bags of trash and 1 bulk item a day free** to any of Metro's four convenience centers (mattresses are **$12 each**). Bring a **Tennessee driver's license** showing a Davidson County address, and plan to unload it yourself [[nws-cc]].

- **Anderson Lane,** 939A Anderson Ln, Madison 37115
- **East,** 943A Doctor Richard G. Adams Dr, Nashville 37207
- **Ezell Pike,** 3254 Ezell Pk, Nashville 37211
- **Omohundro,** 1019 Omohundro Pl, Nashville 37210
- **Hours:** Tuesday to Saturday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.; closed Metro holidays [[nws-hours]]

### Sheriff's curbside bulk pickup

The Davidson County Sheriff's Office removes **old furniture, sofas and couches, lawn and baby furniture, mattresses and rugs** from the front curb [[dcso-bulk]]. Call **615-880-3897** [[dcso-contact]], set items out by **6:30 a.m.**, and allow up to **seven business days** from your date. Nothing in bags, and no glass or mirrored pieces [[dcso-bulk]].
