## The short version

Nashville's curbside bulk pickup isn't run by the trash department. It's a free service of the **Davidson County Sheriff's Office (DCSO)**. Call **615-880-3897** to get a date [[dcso-contact]], put the items at your **front curb by 6:30 a.m.** that day, and crews have **up to seven business days** from that date to take them away [[dcso-bulk]].

If you'd rather not wait, Metro Nashville's **Department of Waste Services** runs four **Convenience Centers** where Davidson County residents can drop off **one bulk item free** each day [[nws-cc]] [[nws-bulky]].

## Option 1: Sheriff's Office curbside pickup (free)

### How to book

1. **Call 615-880-3897.** The Sheriff's Office says you don't need to fill out its website contact form for bulk item removal; just call [[dcso-contact]].
2. **Have this ready:** a list of the items, the service address, a phone number, and the name of the resident whose home it is. The request has to come from the **homeowner or tenant** of that property [[dcso-bulk]].
3. **Note your date.** Items must be at the curb by **6:30 a.m.** on it. Crews then have up to **seven business days** to come; weekends and holidays don't count. The Sheriff's own example: a date of Jan. 3 means pickup by Jan. 12 [[dcso-bulk]].

### Where to put it

- **Front curb only.** The service removes items from the front curbside only [[dcso-bulk]].
- **Apartments, condos and townhomes:** crews aren't allowed on private property such as apartment, condominium or townhome complexes, private roads, gated communities or business properties. Residents of those places can put items at the front curb by the **main entrance** [[dcso-bulk]].
- **Nothing in bags.** Bagged items aren't taken [[dcso-bulk]].
- **Eviction items:** by law, the Sheriff's Office can't remove bulk items that resulted from an eviction [[dcso-bulk]].

### What they take

From the Sheriff's list of authorized items [[dcso-bulk]]:

- **Furniture:** old furniture, sofas and couches, lawn furniture, baby furniture
- **Mattresses and box springs**
- **Appliances:** refrigerators, freezers, water heaters, ranges, washers and dryers, air conditioners, microwaves
- **Electronics:** computers and monitors, TVs, copy machines
- **Carpet, carpet pads and rugs**
- Exercise equipment, bicycles, kids' toys, toilets
- Lawn mowers, emptied of gas and oil
- Lumber, fencing, pallets, shipping crates, and mailbox or fence posts with concrete footings
- Construction debris (but see the exclusions below)
- Car parts such as fenders, hoods, bumpers, transmissions, radiators and brake rotors
- Swing sets, basketball goals and storage sheds, **taken apart first**
- **Hot tubs, cut into two pieces**

### What they won't take

[[dcso-bulk]]

- **Tires** and **motor oil**
- Household or hazardous chemicals, **paint cans (even empty)**, batteries
- Propane, gas or compressed air tanks
- **Glass or mirrored items**
- **Sheetrock (drywall)** and **roofing**
- Sand, dirt, rocks or concrete
- Trash cans, shopping carts or other businesses' products
- Boats, cars, motorcycles and jet skis
- **Brush and tree limbs**, which Metro picks up separately on a quarterly schedule

## Option 2: Drop it at a Convenience Center

Metro says items too bulky for your curbside cart should go to a Convenience Center [[nws-bulky]].

**Locations** (all open **Tuesday to Saturday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.**, closed Metro holidays) [[nws-hours]]:

- **Anderson Lane:** 939A Anderson Ln, Madison, TN 37115
- **East:** 943A Doctor Richard G. Adams Dr, Nashville, TN 37207
- **Ezell Pike:** 3254 Ezell Pk, Nashville, TN 37211
- **Omohundro:** 1019 Omohundro Pl, Nashville, TN 37210

**What it costs** [[nws-cc]]:

- **Free:** 1 bulk item plus 3 bags of trash (30 gallons max per bag), once a day. This free allowance **excludes mattresses**, tires on rims, and construction and demolition material.
- **Mattresses and box springs:** **$12 each**, up to 4 a month (East, Ezell Pike and Omohundro only).
- **Everything else by volume:** **$10** for under 1 cubic yard (up to the level of the truck bed), or **$20 per cubic yard** for 1 to 8 cubic yards.
- **Appliances and scrap metal:** one bulk scrap metal item, such as a refrigerator, washer, dryer or stove, is free [[nws-bulky]].
- **Paying:** cash (not at Omohundro), check, or card, with a **2.8% card fee, $1.95 minimum**.

**Rules** [[nws-cc]]:

- Bring a **Tennessee driver's license** showing you live in Davidson County.
- You unload your own load.
- **No commercial vehicles**, including any vehicle with company advertising or commercial tags, and no commercial waste.
- Brush, leaves, concrete, dirt, brick, rock, roofing shingles, siding, pallets and car parts other than tires aren't accepted.

## Construction and remodeling debris

Metro bans construction debris from the trash cart and says residential construction waste must be collected by a **private hauler or contractor**. Small do-it-yourself amounts can go to the **Omohundro** Convenience Center [[nws-trash]], for **$20** for up to 2 cubic yards [[nws-cc]]. For bigger jobs, Metro's bulky item page lists private disposal sites; call them for fees [[nws-bulky]].

## Donate first if you can

Before sending furniture to the landfill, Metro asks residents to consider donating it to secondhand stores or offering it on online marketplaces [[nws-bulky]].
