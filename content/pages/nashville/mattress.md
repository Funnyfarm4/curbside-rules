## The short version

You have two official ways to get rid of a mattress in Nashville:

1. **Free curbside pickup from the Davidson County Sheriff's Office.** Mattresses and box springs are on its list of items it removes [[dcso-bulk]]. Call **615-880-3897** to schedule [[dcso-contact]].
2. **Drop it off at a Metro Convenience Center** for **$12 per mattress or box spring** [[nws-cc]].

For anything too bulky to fit in your curbside cart, Metro points residents to its Convenience Centers [[nws-bulky]].

## Option 1: Free Sheriff's Office pickup

1. **Call 615-880-3897.** You'll be asked for the items you're setting out, the address, a phone number and the resident's name. The homeowner or tenant has to make the request [[dcso-bulk]] [[dcso-contact]].
2. **Put it at the front curb by 6:30 a.m.** on your date. Front curb only [[dcso-bulk]].
3. **Don't bag it.** The Sheriff's Office doesn't take anything in bags [[dcso-bulk]].
4. **Give it time.** Crews have up to **seven business days** from your date, not counting weekends and holidays [[dcso-bulk]].

**Apartment, condo or townhome?** Crews can't come onto private complexes, private roads, gated communities or business property. Put it at the front curb by the main entrance instead [[dcso-bulk]].

**After an eviction:** the Sheriff's Office says it's prohibited by law from removing bulk items that resulted from an eviction [[dcso-bulk]].

For everything else the Sheriff's crews take, see our [Nashville bulk trash pickup guide](/nashville/bulk-pickup/).

## Option 2: Drop it off ($12 each)

Metro takes mattresses and box springs at **three** of its four Convenience Centers, for **$12 each**, up to **four a month** [[nws-cc]] [[nws-bulky]]. All are open **Tuesday to Saturday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.** and closed on Metro holidays [[nws-hours]]:

- **East:** 943A Doctor Richard G. Adams Dr, Nashville, TN 37207
- **Ezell Pike:** 3254 Ezell Pk, Nashville, TN 37211
- **Omohundro:** 1019 Omohundro Pl, Nashville, TN 37210

The **Anderson Lane** center in Madison isn't listed for mattresses [[nws-cc]].

**Before you go** [[nws-cc]]:

- Bring a **Tennessee driver's license** showing you live in Davidson County.
- You unload it yourself.
- **No commercial vehicles,** including any vehicle with company advertising or commercial tags.
- The free daily bulk item **doesn't cover mattresses**, so expect to pay.
- Cash (not at Omohundro), check or card; cards add **2.8%, minimum $1.95**.

## Donate it if it's still good

Metro asks residents to consider donating furniture to secondhand stores or offering it online before sending it to the landfill [[nws-bulky]]. Many charities won't take used mattresses, so call first.
