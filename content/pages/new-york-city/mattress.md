## The short version

New York City will take your old mattress or box spring **for free**, right from the curb, but only if you follow two rules: **seal it in a plastic bag**, and put it out **on the right night** [[311-mattress]]. There's no appointment to book. DSNY stopped offering pick-up appointments for large items [[dsny-large]].

Get either rule wrong and the mattress stays where it is, and you may get a ticket [[311-mattress]] [[dsny-laws]].

## Option 1: Free DSNY curbside collection

DSNY gives NYC residents free curbside removal of large items, and mattresses and box springs are collected with your **trash**, not your recycling [[dsny-furn]]. This service is for **residential buildings** [[311-bulk]].

- **Cost:** free [[dsny-furn]].
- **Appointment:** none. Appointments are no longer offered and can't be scheduled [[dsny-large]].
- **Limit:** up to **6 bulk items** per collection day. If you're clearing out a lot, spread it over several collection days [[311-mattress]] [[311-bulk]].
- **Also accepted the same way:** futons. NYC311 lists mattresses, box springs and futons together as items you can put out with your trash [[311-mattress]].

### Find your night

1. Look up your address in DSNY's [collection schedule tool](https://www.nyc.gov/assets/dsny/forms/collection-schedule) [[dsny-lookup]] [[311-schedule]].
2. Find your **trash** (bulk or "large item") collection day. Mattresses go out the **night before that day**, **not** before your recycling day [[311-mattress]] [[dsny-furn]].
3. Check for holidays. Collection can change when your day falls on a Sanitation holiday. The rest of 2026's list includes Election Day (Tuesday, November 3), Veterans Day (Wednesday, November 11), Thanksgiving (Thursday, November 26) and Christmas (Friday, December 25) [[311-mattress]].

## How to prepare it

1. **Bag it completely.** Any mattress or box spring set out for collection must be **fully contained** in a plastic bag that's **securely sealed**, to stop the spread of bed bugs [[dsny-laws]]. DSNY doesn't collect uncovered or partly covered mattresses [[311-mattress]].
2. **Use a clear bag.** DSNY's collection laws say the bag must be clear [[dsny-laws]], while NYC311 says any color except red or orange [[311-mattress]]. A clear bag meets both.
3. **Buy the bag yourself.** The City doesn't provide mattress bags or covers. You can get them at department stores, home improvement stores and moving supply centers [[311-mattress]].
4. **Set it out between 6 p.m. and midnight** the night before collection [[311-mattress]]. All waste has to be out by midnight [[311-schedule]].
5. **Put it in the right spot.** Place it at the curb or in your alley if that's your collection area. Don't put it in the street, in front of another property, or where it blocks people walking. Items left on private property are "unserviceable" and won't be picked up [[311-bulk]].

### The bed frame

Bed frames are bulk items too. A frame that's mostly **metal or plastic** goes out the night before your **recycling** day. A **wood** frame goes out the night before your **trash** day. If it's in pieces, tie them in bundles no larger than **2 by 4 feet** [[311-mattress]]. If a frame or other bulk item has bed bugs, attach a sign saying so, so nobody takes it [[311-mattress]] [[311-bulk]].

## Fines

If you set out a mattress or box spring the wrong way, DSNY's collection laws list these fines [[dsny-laws]]:

- **$50** for the first offense
- **$100** for the second offense
- **$200** for the third and later offenses

Fines can increase with each violation within 12 months [[dsny-laws]]. DSNY's furniture page puts the top fine for incorrect mattress disposal at **up to $300** [[dsny-furn]].

## Missed pickup or a dumped mattress

- **Not collected?** Report it on NYC311's [missed collection page](https://portal.311.nyc.gov/article/?kanumber=KA-01788). First make sure it was bagged and out on the right night, because unbagged mattresses aren't collected [[311-mattress]].
- **Neighbor's unbagged mattress at the curb?** NYC311 has a Trash or Recycling Disposal Complaint page for that, and an Illegal Dumping page for mattresses dumped on the street [[311-mattress]].
- **Wrong schedule for your address?** Call **311** or **212-NEW-YORK (212-639-9675)** [[311-schedule]].

## Option 2: When DSNY pickup won't work

DSNY's free bulk collection is for residential buildings. NYC311 points non-residential buildings to its separate "Waste Disposal for Businesses" rules [[311-bulk]]. If you're a business, or you can't get the mattress bagged and down to the curb on the right night, a paid junk-removal crew can carry it out for you.
