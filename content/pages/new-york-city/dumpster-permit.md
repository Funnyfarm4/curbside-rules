## The short version

A dumpster on a New York City **street or sidewalk** needs a **NYC DOT permit**, and the container must be commercial grade [[311-dumpster]]. The part that surprises most people: **you don't apply for it.** Permits must be obtained by a **carting company or general contractor**, not by the property or business owner [[311-dumpster]].

So your job is to hire a carting company registered with DOT (or a registered general contractor) and confirm it's pulling the permit for your address.

## Which permit fits your job?

### Cleaning out a home or business: Commercial Refuse Container (up to 5 days)

- **Who it's for:** homeowners or businesses clearing out **bulk items** [[311-dumpster]].
- **Who applies:** the carting company, which must be registered with DOT. It places the container [[311-dumpster]].
- **How long:** each permit covers **one container** at one location for **up to 5 consecutive days** [[dot-crc]] [[311-dumpster]].
- **Swaps:** if the container fills up and is exchanged within the 5 days, no new permit is needed. A **second** container at the same spot needs its own permit [[dot-crc]].
- **How fast:** registered permittees can usually get one the **same business day or the next** [[dot-crc]].
- **What it can't hold:** perishable waste, or construction material from a job that needs a Department of Buildings (DOB) or DOT construction permit [[311-dumpster]] [[rcny]].

### Renovating with a DOB permit: Construction Debris Container (30 to 90 days)

- **Who it's for:** worksites, such as a major building renovation, that require a DOB permit (or a DOT construction permit) [[311-dumpster]] [[dot-crc]].
- **Who applies:** only registered general contractors or carting companies registered with DOT [[311-dumpster]] [[dot-crc]].
- **How long:** typically 20- or 40-yard containers, or several mini containers, on the street for up to **90 days**. The permit must be posted at the worksite for the whole project [[311-dumpster]].

### Moving: no storage pods on the street

Storage containers or "pods" used for non-construction activity, like temporary storage or shipping furniture and personal goods, are **not permitted** on City streets [[311-dumpster]]. City rules also bar placing a storage box or a metal shipping container (a CONEX box) on any street unless the DOT Commissioner authorizes it [[rcny]].

## Do you even need a dumpster?

DSNY picks up up to **6 large items** per collection day from the curb for free, with no appointment [[dsny-large]]. It also takes material from small do-it-yourself projects as bulk items. For a **major** construction or renovation project, DSNY says to arrange a dumpster through a private trash removal service, and construction waste from professional contractors can't go out as residential garbage [[dsny-cd]]. See our guides to [getting rid of a couch](/new-york-city/couch/) and [a mattress](/new-york-city/mattress/) in NYC.

## Where the dumpster can't go

City rules for commercial refuse containers say they can't be placed [[rcny]]:

- in any **No Stopping, No Standing, No Parking Anytime** or **Authorized Parking** area
- within **15 feet of a hydrant**
- in the **corner** area (the building line extended to the curb) or within 10 feet of either side of it
- on, or blocking, a **crosswalk or pedestrian ramp**
- within **5 feet of railroad tracks**

They also can't block subway entrances, utility access points, fire alarms, traffic signals, street signs, bus stops or shelters, or water and gas shut-off valves without permission [[rcny]].

The permit **doesn't reserve a parking space** or give any parking privileges, so the container still needs a legal spot at the curb [[dot-crc]].

## What to check when it arrives

- **Placement:** parallel to the curb, sticking out no more than **9 feet** into the roadway [[rcny]].
- **Street protection:** planks, skids, plating or tires under each steel wheel or roller, in place the whole time [[rcny]].
- **Markings:** the owner's name, address and phone number in letters at least **3 inches** high on both long sides, and reflective markings on all four sides [[rcny]].
- **Clear paths:** sidewalks, gutters, crosswalks, bike lanes and driveways must be kept clear, with spilled debris cleaned up promptly [[rcny]].

DOT also checks requested dates against special events and will flag a conflict to the applicant [[dot-crc]].
