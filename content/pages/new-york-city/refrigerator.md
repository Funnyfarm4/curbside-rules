## The short version

In New York City, a refrigerator can't just go out with the trash. Appliances that cool or chill contain CFC gas (Freon), and **trained DSNY staff must remove it** before the appliance can be collected and recycled [[dsny-appl]]. That service is **free for residents**, but you **must make an appointment** [[dsny-cfc]].

On the appointment day, DSNY removes the CFC and puts a tag with a six-digit number on the appliance. Sanitation workers then pick up tagged items on your **next recycling day**. Only tagged appliances are collected [[dsny-cfc]].

## Which appliances need an appointment

- Refrigerators and freezers with non-flammable Freon
- Air conditioners
- Dehumidifiers
- Water or wine coolers [[nyc311-cfc]]

You can book an appointment for a residential address or a non-profit institution serviced by DSNY [[nyc311-cfc]].

## How to book and set it out

1. **Schedule** online yourself or through **311** (212-NEW-YORK, 212-639-9675). DSNY doesn't do same-day appointments. You can book up to 10 items per appointment. Keep your appointment number [[nyc311-cfc]]. Appointments can't be made anonymously [[dsny-cfc]].
2. **Remove the doors, hinges and locks** from refrigerators and freezers. This protects children and animals [[nyc311-cfc]] [[dsny-cfc]].
3. **Put it at the curb between 6 p.m. and midnight** the evening before your appointment, with the **back facing the street** [[nyc311-cfc]].
4. **DSNY removes the CFC** on the appointment day and tags the appliance [[nyc311-cfc]].
5. **Collection workers pick it up** on your next recycling day [[nyc311-cfc]].

Got more items after booking, or someone already booked your preferred date? You need a separate appointment on a different date [[nyc311-cfc]].

### Cancel or reschedule

Use your service request number to cancel or reschedule, up to 24 hours before the appointment. If the appliance is already outside, bring it back in. If you don't cancel and DSNY can't find it, the appointment is closed as "Not On Location" [[nyc311-cfc]].

## When DSNY won't take it

- **Flammable refrigerant (R600a or R32).** Some newer appliances use it. Look for a warning sticker with a yellow triangle and black flames. DSNY tags these with a red sticker and won't collect them. Contact the manufacturer or a private carter [[dsny-cfc]] [[nyc311-cfc]].
- **Oversized or commercial-size appliances** won't fit in the garbage truck. You must hire a private removal service or carter [[nyc311-cfc]].
- **Businesses** must use a private carter. Appointments made by a business may result in a Notice of Violation [[dsny-cfc]].
- **Soda machines** need a special request by phone through 311 [[nyc311-cfc]].

## Washers, dryers, stoves and other big appliances

Dishwashers, microwaves, ovens and ranges, washers and dryers, and water heaters don't need a CFC appointment. They follow the large (bulk) item rules [[dsny-appl]]:

- Items that are **mostly metal or plastic** go out with your **recycling**.
- Other non-recyclable large items go out with **trash**, on your trash-only day, not your recycling day.
- You can set out **up to 6 large items** at the curb per collection day.

Small appliances like blenders, coffee makers, toaster ovens and corded vacuums go out with recycling if they're mostly metal or plastic. Take rechargeable batteries out of cordless tools and handheld vacuums first [[dsny-appl]].
