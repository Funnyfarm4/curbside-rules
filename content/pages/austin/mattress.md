## The short version

Austin's **Recycle and Reuse Center does not accept mattresses** [[atx-rrc]]. For homes with City curbside service, the City's route for large items is **on-demand bulk collection**, which you must **book first**: items left out without an appointment won't be picked up [[atx-od]].

One thing to know before you book: the City's list of accepted bulk items names **furniture** but **not mattresses**, and the City says that list is not complete. It tells you to use the Austin Recycles app or the *What Do I Do With* tool to confirm an item [[atx-od]]. We couldn't confirm a mattress rule on the City's pages, so check the scheduler before you set one out.

## Who qualifies

- **Single-family homes and buildings with up to four units** that get City curbside service [[atx-od]] [[atx-res]]
- Some homes in certain Limited Districts [[atx-od]]
- **Five or more units:** not covered. Those properties must use a private hauler [[atx-res]]
- Items set out **in an alley, in front of a vacant lot, or in front of a business** won't be collected [[atx-od]]

## How to book

1. **Schedule** through the Austin Recycles app, the City's online scheduler, or by calling **Austin 3-1-1 (512-974-2000)** [[atx-od]].
2. **Select every item** you'll set out. The scheduling system shows which items can be collected [[atx-od]].
3. **Watch your email** for a confirmation and a reminder before the date [[atx-od]].

You get **up to three bulk collections per calendar year**, so bundle the mattress with other large items [[atx-od]]. Appointments can't be changed. To move a date, cancel and rebook, using the same method you booked with or the link in your confirmation email [[atx-od]].

## Cost

The City's page lists no per-appointment fee for curbside customers. Bulk collection is bundled into the **$24.90 base fee** on the solid waste part of your City utility bill, and you pay it whether or not you use the service [[atx-fees]]. Bags set out with bulk items can be charged as extra trash [[atx-od]], so don't bag the mattress.

## How to set it out

- Put it at the curb by **5:30 a.m.** on the appointment date [[atx-od]].
- **Don't bag or box it** [[atx-od]].
- Keep it **5 feet** from carts, mailboxes, fences or walls, water meters, utility boxes, fire hydrants and parked cars, and out from under low tree limbs or power lines [[atx-od]].
- Bulk is sorted into piles: metal and rigid plastics, non-metal items, and passenger car tires. Ask the scheduler which pile your mattress belongs in [[atx-od]].

**Missed pickup?** If items aren't collected by 4 p.m. on your day, call 3-1-1 or email after 4 p.m. that day, and within 2 business days [[atx-od]].

## If it's not picked up, or you don't qualify

- **Larger buildings:** use a private hauler [[atx-res]].
- **Drop-off:** not at the City's center [[atx-rrc]].
- **Still in good shape?** The City asks residents to consider reuse and donation before bulk collection. Donation rules vary by charity, so call first [[atx-od]].
