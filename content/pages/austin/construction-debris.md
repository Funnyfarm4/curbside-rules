## The short version

Austin's curbside bulk collection is **not** a way to clear out a remodel: tile, mirrors, sheet glass, construction carpet and "other construction and remodeling debris" are all on its not-accepted list [[atx-od]]. The City's Recycle and Reuse Center doesn't list construction debris among what it takes either [[atx-rrc]].

Your realistic options are: put out the **few items bulk pickup does take** [[atx-od]], **haul the rest to a landfill** such as Texas Disposal Systems in Creedmoor [[tds-rates]], **recycle clean concrete free** [[tds-concrete]], **donate** what's reusable [[hab-guide]], or **hire a crew** to load and haul it.

## What Austin bulk pickup will and won't take

Bulk collection is for Austin Resource Recovery residential customers (single-family homes up to fourplexes), by appointment, up to **three times a calendar year** [[atx-od]]. Book in the Austin Recycles app, online, or by calling Austin 3-1-1 at **512-974-2000** [[atx-od]].

**Accepted** remodel-type items include [[atx-od]]:

- **Doors**
- **Nail-free lumber**
- **Pallets**
- **Carpet (household)**
- Appliances (remove doors)

**Not accepted** [[atx-od]]:

- **Tile** (whole or broken)
- **Mirrors** and **sheet glass and other construction and remodeling debris**
- **Carpet from construction or demolition**
- Hazardous materials, household trash, cardboard boxes and brush

**Set-out rules:** put items at the curb by **5:30 a.m.** on your appointment date, in separate piles for metal and rigid plastic items and for non-metal items such as carpet and nail-free lumber. Don't bag or box anything (bags are treated as extra trash and may cost extra), and keep piles 5 feet from carts, mailboxes, fences, meters, hydrants and parked cars [[atx-od]]. Full details: [Austin bulk collection guide](/austin/bulk-collection/).

## Option 1: Haul it to the TDS Landfill in Creedmoor

The **Texas Disposal Systems (TDS) Landfill** is a private facility at **3016 FM 1327, Creedmoor, TX 78610**, in southeast Travis County just outside Austin [[tds-contact]] [[tds-concrete]].

**Hours:** Monday to Saturday, **7 a.m. to 5:30 p.m. or sunset, whichever comes first**; closed Sunday, Thanksgiving, Christmas Day and New Year's Day [[tds-contact]]. Office: **512-421-1363**; customer care **800-375-8375** [[tds-contact]].

**Prices on the TDS gate-rate page** [[tds-rates]]:

- **Citizen drop-off (car, pickup or SUV):** **$66 minimum** plus $12 per yard over 5.5 yards, plus fees and charges
- **Construction & demolition plant, small personal loads:** **$12.50 per cubic yard** (businesses and large loads: $90 a ton, $375 minimum)
- **Untreated, unpainted lumber and brush** (no metal or trash): $10 per cubic yard, $20 minimum, for loads under 5.5 yards
- **Scrap metal, water heaters and appliances without freon:** no charge
- **Unsecured load:** **$35** (free tarp for pickup trucks), so tie down and cover your load
- Landfilled loads also carry a state fee, fuel surcharge, environmental charge and other percentage charges

TDS says to call for pricing on asbestos and other special waste, and on reusable items and construction materials [[tds-rates]].

> **Heads-up on hours:** the TDS concrete FAQ lists hours to 7 p.m., but its contact and gate-rate pages say 5:30 p.m. We show the earlier time; call 512-421-1363 before a late trip [[tds-concrete]] [[tds-contact]].

## Option 2: Recycle concrete, brick and rock for free

TDS accepts **clean concrete (with or without rebar)** and clean rock base such as **brick, granite, tile and asphalt** for recycling, and says it takes concrete and approved rock base **at no charge at this time** [[tds-concrete]]. The gate-rate page lists concrete, rock, gravel, pavers, asphalt and road base without trash or contamination as **no charge** [[tds-rates]].

- Keep it free of trash, wood, glass, plastic and hazardous waste, and **tell the gatehouse attendant** what you're bringing [[tds-concrete]].
- TDS may charge a fee if materials aren't properly prepared; for prep instructions call **800-375-8375** [[tds-concrete]].
- Concrete **mixed with trash** is landfilled at **$60 a ton, 2-ton minimum**, plus fees [[tds-rates]].

## Option 3: Donate reusable materials to Habitat ReStore

Austin Habitat for Humanity's ReStores take **new or gently used** building materials [[hab-guide]], including:

- **Drywall, insulation, shingles, roofing tin, plywood, siding and trim** [[hab-guide]]
- **Lumber over 36 inches** (not painted, not under 36 inches, and no nails, screws or holes) [[hab-guide]]
- **Cabinets** with drawers and doors intact, and countertops in good condition (no laminate or cultured marble) [[hab-guide]]
- **Interior and exterior doors** in good condition (no painted doors, 8-foot doors or water damage) [[hab-guide]]
- Unopened paint (less than a year old) and unopened Quikrete [[hab-guide]]

**Free pickup:** the ReStore offers free donation pickup if you can't drop off [[hab-donate]]. **Drop-off** at any of three stores [[hab-donate]]:

- **South Austin,** 500 W Ben White Blvd, 512-478-2165
- **Northwest Austin,** 13804 N US-183, 512-367-5795
- **San Marcos,** 2521 S Interstate 35, 512-738-8383

Habitat's page says the donation drive closes one hour before the store closes in one place and thirty minutes in another, so plan to arrive at least an hour before closing [[hab-donate]].

## Paint, metal and other leftovers

- **Paint, thinners and other household hazardous waste:** free drop-off for Austin and Travis County residents at the City's **Recycle and Reuse Center**, by appointment [[atx-rrc]]. Curbside customers can also book a household hazardous waste pickup [[atx-od]]. See [Austin hazardous waste and electronics drop-off](/austin/hazardous-waste-electronics/).
- **Scrap metal and water heaters** are also accepted at the Recycle and Reuse Center [[atx-rrc]].
- **Toilets** are on the center's not-accepted list [[atx-rrc]].
