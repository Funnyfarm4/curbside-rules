## The short version

Austin makes refrigerator disposal fairly easy if you live in a house or small building with City trash service:

1. **Book a bulk pickup.** Appliances are accepted **with the doors removed**, by appointment, up to **three times a year** [[atx-od]]. There's no extra charge; it's part of your utility bill's base fee [[atx-fees]].
2. **Drop it off** at the City's **Recycle and Reuse Center**, by appointment, free for Austin and Travis County residents [[atx-wdi]] [[atx-rrc]].
3. **Donate it** if it still cools: Austin Habitat ReStore takes working fridges and will pick them up free [[hab-guide]] [[hab-pickup]].

The City's What Do I Do With? tool actually lists **reuse first**: its "best option" for a refrigerator is the **Austin Reuse Directory**, which helps you find places to donate, sell or repair used items [[atx-wdi]].

## Option 1: City bulk pickup

Austin Resource Recovery's on-demand bulk collection takes **appliances (remove doors)** [[atx-od]]. The City recycles every appliance it collects this way, and says the recycling revenue helps keep customer fees down [[atx-od]].

### Who qualifies

- **Single-family homes and buildings of up to four units** served by Austin Resource Recovery, plus homes in certain Limited Districts [[atx-od]]
- Items set out **in an alley, in front of a vacant lot, or in front of a business won't be collected** [[atx-od]]

If you're in a building with five or more units, this service doesn't cover you; ask your property manager how the building handles appliances, or see the hauling option below.

### How to book

1. Schedule in the **Austin Recycles app**, online through the City's [My Schedule page](https://www.austintexas.gov/myschedule), or by calling **Austin 3-1-1 (512-974-2000)** [[atx-od]].
2. **Select every item** you'll set out. You'll get an email confirmation and, later, a reminder [[atx-od]].
3. **No appointment, no pickup** [[atx-od]].

You get **up to three bulk collections per calendar year** [[atx-od]]. Appointments can't be changed: cancel (using the same method you booked with, or the link in your confirmation email) and book again [[atx-od]].

### What it costs

Nothing extra per pickup. The **$24.90 monthly base fee** on the solid waste section of your City of Austin utility bill pays for bulk collection, along with recycling, composting, brush and household hazardous waste pickup, and you're charged it whether or not you use those services [[atx-fees]].

### How to set it out

[[atx-od]]

- **Take the doors off** the refrigerator or freezer.
- Put it at the curb **by 5:30 a.m.** on your appointment date.
- Sort your pile: the fridge goes with **metal items and rigid plastics** (appliances, electronics), separate from non-metal items and tires.
- Keep it **5 feet away** from carts, mailboxes, fences or walls, water meters, utility boxes, fire hydrants and parked cars, and not under low tree limbs or power lines.
- **Don't bag or box** anything.

**Missed?** Different trucks may come at different times. If everything isn't collected by **4 p.m.**, call 3-1-1 or email Austin Resource Recovery **after 4 p.m. that day and within 2 business days** [[atx-od]].

## Option 2: Drop it at the Recycle and Reuse Center

The City's What Do I Do With? tool lists the **Recycle and Reuse Center** as an option for refrigerators [[atx-wdi]].

- **Address:** 2514 Business Center Drive, Austin, TX 78744 [[atx-wdi]]
- **Phone:** 512-974-4373 (recycling and reuse) [[atx-wdi]]
- **Appointment required.** Austin and Travis County residents book online, in the Austin Recycles app, or by calling 3-1-1 [[atx-wdi]] [[atx-rrc]].
- **Cost:** free for Austin and Travis County residents, except tires. Non-residents pay fees under the City's fee schedule [[atx-rrc]].

The center's accepted electronics and appliances list also includes air conditioners, microwaves and other kitchen appliances, stoves, washers and dryers, and water heaters [[atx-rrc]]. It **doesn't** take furniture, mattresses or toilets [[atx-rrc]].

## Option 3: Donate a working fridge

- **Austin Habitat for Humanity ReStore** accepts used appliances in **good working condition**, clean and complete, but **not appliances with cooling components that don't work**, such as refrigerators and air conditioners [[hab-guide]]. Free donation pickup needs **3-4 days' lead time** [[hab-pickup]], or drop off at South Austin (500 W. Ben White Blvd., 512-478-2165) or Northwest Austin (13804 N. US-183, 512-367-5795) [[hab-pickup]].
- **Goodwill Central Texas** lists **large appliances (refrigerators, freezers, air conditioners)** among items it **can't accept** [[gw-ct]].
- The **[Austin Reuse Directory](https://www.austinreusedirectory.com/)** is the City's top suggestion for finding other places to donate or sell a fridge [[atx-wdi]].

## Is there a utility buyback?

Not right now, as far as Austin Energy's website shows. Its residential **Appliances and Equipment** rebate page lists rebates for heat pump water heaters, variable-speed pool pumps and Power Partner backup batteries, and **no refrigerator recycling offer** [[ae-app]].

## What about the Freon?

Under federal rules, the **final person in the disposal chain**, such as a scrap metal recycler or landfill, is responsible for making sure refrigerant is recovered before the appliance is disposed of. If an appliance arrives with no refrigerant left, that facility must keep a **signed statement** from whoever dropped it off [[epa-608]].
