## The short version

Austin's main drop-off for paint, chemicals, batteries and electronics is Austin Resource Recovery's **Recycle and Reuse Center**. It's **free for Austin and Travis County residents** (except tires), but **every visit needs an appointment**, booked in the Austin Recycles app or online [[atx-rrc]].

If you're a City curbside customer, you can skip the trip: Austin will **collect household hazardous waste at your home** up to **three times a year** [[atx-od]].

## Option 1: Drop it off at the Recycle and Reuse Center

### Booking

- **Appointment required.** Austin and Travis County residents schedule through the **Austin Recycles** app or the City's website [[atx-rrc]].
- **Can't change a booking.** To move it, cancel and book again, using the same method you booked with (app, the My Schedule web page, or the link in your confirmation email). Call Austin 3-1-1 if you have trouble canceling [[atx-rrc]].
- **Where:** the City's driving-directions map for the center points to **2514 Business Center Dr, Austin, TX 78744** [[atx-rrc]].

### Who can use it and what it costs

- **Free** for Austin and Travis County residents, **except tires**, which are **$6 per tire** [[atx-rrc]].
- **Non-residents** pay fees under Austin Resource Recovery's fee schedule [[atx-rrc]].
- **Businesses can't drop off hazardous waste.** For business disposal, call **512-974-4335** [[atx-rrc]].

### Household hazardous waste it takes

[[atx-rrc]]

- Aerosol cans
- Automotive fluids and oil filters
- Batteries (car and household)
- Cleaning products
- Cooking oil
- Fluorescent light bulbs
- Mercury
- **Paint and thinners**
- Pesticides and herbicides
- Pool chemicals
- Propane cylinders

**Not accepted:** any waste generated by a business, explosives (including ammunition), radioactive materials, and syringes or medical waste [[atx-rrc]].

### How to pack it

[[atx-rrc]]

- Keep products in their **original containers** and don't mix them.
- Use containers of **5 gallons or smaller**.
- Set small containers **upright in sturdy boxes**.
- If something leaks, put it in another container and soak up the spill with cat litter.
- **No trash bags.** Containers can break and mix, causing a reaction.

### Electronics and appliances it takes

[[atx-rrc]]

- **Televisions**, computers, printers and fax machines
- Cell phones and chargers
- CDs, DVDs and floppy disks (throw away the jewel cases)
- Microwaves and other kitchen appliances, stoves
- Washers and dryers, water heaters, air conditioners
- Lamps, exercise equipment, lawn mowers and weed eaters

The center also takes solar panels and battery storage systems, scrap metal, lawn furniture, clothing and housewares, and regular recyclables [[atx-rrc]].

### What the center won't take

Furniture, **mattresses**, non-recyclable trash, Styrofoam packing peanuts, **toilets**, and brush or yard trimmings. Brush can go to Austin Water's **Hornsby Bend Biosolids Management Plant**, with no appointment needed [[atx-rrc]].

### Free stuff while you're there

The center's **ReUse Store** gives away usable items other people dropped off, such as cleaning products, automotive fluids and art supplies. **Austin ReBlend**, a recycled flat paint made from paint collected at the center, is free too (appointment needed), and you can load free **mulch** yourself [[atx-rrc]].

## Option 2: Have it picked up at home

Austin Resource Recovery's residential customers, meaning **single-family homes and buildings of up to four units**, can schedule up to **three household hazardous waste collections a year**. Book through the Austin Recycles app, online, or by calling **Austin 3-1-1 (512-974-2000)** [[atx-od]].

**What crews collect:** aerosol cans, automotive fluids and oil filters, car and household batteries (lithium-ion only from laptops or smaller devices), cleaning products, cooking oil and grease, fluorescent bulbs, mercury, paint and thinners, pesticides and herbicides, pool chemicals, and propane cylinders **up to 1 pound**. Anything not on that list can't go through this service [[atx-od]].

**Set-out rules** [[atx-od]]:

- **Don't put it at the curb.** Leave it where crews can reach it, out of rain and direct sunlight and away from heat, sparks or flame.
- Containers must be **sealed and clearly labeled**; bag leaking containers separately in zip-top bags.
- If you can, put everything in a recyclable box or tub (it won't be returned).
- **No single container over 5 gallons**, and **30 gallons total** per collection. Have more? Book another pickup or use the center.
- Restrain your pets.
- No commercial or industrial materials.

**Electronics:** for TVs, computers and other electronics, curbside customers can schedule an **on-demand bulk collection** instead [[atx-hard]]. See our [Austin bulk collection guide](/austin/bulk-collection/) for the set-out rules.

## Batteries

Batteries should **never** go in a dumpster or any curbside cart. Take them to the Recycle and Reuse Center, book an at-home HHW pickup, or drop them at your nearest **Austin library branch**; the City says many branches accept them [[atx-hard]].
