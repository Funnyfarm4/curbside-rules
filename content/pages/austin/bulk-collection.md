## The short version

Austin Resource Recovery picks up bulk items **by appointment only**. Each address served by City curbside collection gets **up to three bulk collections a year** [[atx-od]]. Book in the **Austin Recycles app**, on the City's [My Schedule page](https://www.austintexas.gov/myschedule), or by calling **Austin 3-1-1 (512-974-2000)** [[atx-od]].

There's no extra charge per appointment: bulk collection is bundled into the **$24.90 monthly base fee** on the solid waste part of your City utility bill, which you pay whether or not you use it [[atx-fees]].

## Who qualifies

- **Single-family homes and buildings with up to four units** that get City curbside service [[atx-od]] [[atx-res]]
- Some homes in certain Limited Districts [[atx-od]]
- **Five or more units:** not covered. Those properties must use a private hauler [[atx-res]]
- Items set out **in an alley, in front of a vacant lot, or in front of a business** won't be collected [[atx-od]]

## How to book

1. **Schedule** through the Austin Recycles app, online, or by calling 3-1-1 [[atx-od]].
2. **List every item.** The scheduler shows what can be collected; select everything you'll set out [[atx-od]].
3. **Watch your email** for a confirmation and, closer to the date, a reminder [[atx-od]].

**Need a different date?** Appointments can't be changed. Cancel and book again, using the same method you booked with or the cancel link in your confirmation email. Call 3-1-1 if canceling gives you trouble [[atx-od]].

## What they take

**Accepted** [[atx-od]]:

- **Furniture**
- **Appliances** (remove the doors)
- Household carpet
- Doors
- Lawn mowers (drain gas and oil)
- Nail-free lumber
- Pallets
- Passenger car tires, rims removed, **8 per household max**
- Railroad ties (cut in half)
- Rolled fencing

**Not accepted** [[atx-od]]:

- Household trash and cardboard boxes
- Brush (it has its own separate appointments)
- **Mirrors, sheet glass, tile,** and other construction and remodeling debris
- Carpet from construction or demolition
- Hazardous materials
- Tires still on rims, and truck or tractor tires
- Boats, trailers, motorcycles, and car chassis or bodies

The City says these lists aren't complete. Check a specific item in the Austin Recycles app or the City's [What Do I Do With?](https://www.austintexas.gov/resource-recovery/what-do-i-do) tool [[atx-od]]. Mattresses aren't named on either list, so look them up before you book.

## How to set it out

Sort everything into **three separate piles** [[atx-od]]:

- **Metal and rigid plastic:** appliances (doors off), electronics and rigid plastics
- **Non-metal:** carpet, nail-free lumber and the like
- **Passenger car tires,** rims off

Then:

- **At the curb by 5:30 a.m.** on your appointment date [[atx-od]].
- **Keep 5 feet clear** of carts, mailboxes, fences and walls, water meters, phone and electric boxes, fire hydrants and parked cars. Nothing under low tree limbs or power lines [[atx-od]].
- **No bags, boxes or other containers.** Bagged items are treated as extra trash and may bring extra trash fees [[atx-od]].

**Missed pickup?** Items may be collected by different trucks at different times of day. If anything is still there after **4 p.m.** on your date, call 3-1-1 or email Austin Resource Recovery after 4 p.m. and **within 2 business days** [[atx-od]].

## Furniture: donate first if you can

The City says it currently has **no recycling or reuse option for furniture** collected through bulk pickup, and asks residents to rehome or donate usable furniture instead [[atx-od]]. A City reuse warehouse, funded with a $4 million EPA grant, is planned but not yet open [[atx-od]].

For donation options, the City points to the [Austin Reuse Directory](https://www.austinreusedirectory.com/); contact the listed groups to ask whether they offer home pickup [[atx-od]].

## Drop-off: the Recycle and Reuse Center

Austin and Travis County residents can drop items at the City's **Recycle and Reuse Center**, by **appointment only** (book in the app or online). It's free for residents, except tires at **$6 each** [[atx-rrc]].

- **Takes:** household hazardous waste, electronics, and appliances such as air conditioners, stoves, washers and dryers, water heaters and microwaves, plus scrap metal and many other recyclables [[atx-rrc]].
- **Doesn't take:** **furniture, mattresses, toilets** or non-recyclable trash [[atx-rrc]].

So for a couch, mattress or recliner, your choices are a bulk appointment, a donation pickup, or a hauler.
