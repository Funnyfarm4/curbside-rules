## The short version

Austin's own trash department asks you to **donate gently used furniture before using bulk collection**, because furniture picked up at the curb currently has **no recycling or reuse option** [[atx-od]]. Three big Central Texas charities can help:

1. **Habitat ReStore** offers a **free** donation pickup service, booked 3-4 days ahead [[hab-donate]] [[hab-schedule]].
2. **The Salvation Army** schedules truck pickups by phone at **512-416-0607** [[sa-store]].
3. **Goodwill Central Texas** picks up through its partner ReSupply, but **fees apply** [[gw-donate]].

All three want furniture that's clean, undamaged and sellable. If nobody will take it, the City's bulk collection is the fallback [[atx-od]].

## Austin Habitat for Humanity ReStore (free pickup)

If you can't drop off at a ReStore, Habitat offers **free donation pick-up** [[hab-donate]].

- **Book** with the online scheduler on Habitat's pickup page. Allow **3-4 days of lead time** [[hab-schedule]].
- **Condition:** all items must be **new or gently used** [[hab-donate]], and furniture must be in **good, working condition** [[hab-guide]].
- **Furniture it won't take** [[hab-guide]]: used mattresses and box springs, broken or soiled furniture, **armoires**, **entertainment centers**, tube TVs, baby furniture, large office furniture such as cubicles, waterbeds and **sofa beds**. Trundle, bunk and storage beds are accepted at management's discretion.
- **Appliances:** new appliances and used ones in good working order are accepted, except wall ovens; cooling appliances such as refrigerators or air conditioners must work [[hab-guide]].
- **Questions:** email procurement@ahfh.org [[hab-donate]].

**Drop off instead** at any of three ReStores [[hab-donate]]:

- **South Austin:** 500 W Ben White Blvd, Austin, TX 78704, (512) 478-2165. Mon-Sat 9 a.m.-8 p.m., Sun 11 a.m.-6 p.m.
- **Northwest Austin:** 13804 N US-183, Austin, TX 78750, (512) 367-5795. Mon-Sat 10 a.m.-7 p.m., Sun 11 a.m.-6 p.m.
- **San Marcos:** 2521 S Interstate 35, San Marcos, TX 78666, (512) 738-8383. Mon-Sat 10 a.m.-6 p.m., Sun 11 a.m.-6 p.m.

> Habitat's site gives two cutoffs: one section says the donation drive **closes one hour before** the store closes, while the store listings say donation doors close **30 minutes** before closing. Arrive at least an hour early to be safe. [[hab-donate]]

## The Salvation Army (truck pickup)

The Salvation Army's Austin Area Command, which serves **Travis and Williamson counties**, takes gently used clothing, furniture, appliances and household items, and says items **can be scheduled to be picked up** [[sa-store]].

- **Schedule** a pickup with one of its trucks by calling **512-416-0607** or toll-free **1-800-SA-TRUCK** (1-800-728-7825) [[sa-store]].
- **Condition:** drivers **don't pick up items deemed not resalable**. Mattresses and box springs, for example, must have no rips, stains or tears [[sa-store]].
- **Not accepted** (partial list) [[sa-store]]: furniture with rips, tears or stains; homemade furniture; baby cribs and strollers; office desks, office dividers and modular office furniture; hospital beds; pool tables; swing sets; metal bed springs; unframed mirrors; built-in stoves, built-in microwaves and dishwashers; hot water heaters; and central A/C units and furnaces.

**Drop off** at a Family Store + Donation Center, open Mon-Tue 10 a.m.-7 p.m., Wed 10 a.m.-8 p.m., Thu-Fri 10 a.m.-7 p.m. and Sat 10 a.m.-8 p.m. [[sa-store]]:

- **4216 S. Congress Ave.**, Austin, TX 78745, 512-447-4044
- **8801 B Research Blvd.**, Austin, TX 78758, 512-836-2700
- **601 W. Louis Henna Blvd.**, Round Rock, TX 78728, 512-246-7292

## Goodwill Central Texas (paid pickup or free drop-off)

Goodwill Central Texas offers **at-home pickup through its partner ReSupply** (fees apply). Book with the online form or call **812-408-3665** [[gw-donate]]. You can also drop off at a Goodwill donation site; use its store locator to find the nearest one [[gw-donate]].

- **Takes:** furniture in good condition, household and kitchen items, electronics (large and small), bikes, sporting goods and more [[gw-donate]].
- **Won't take** [[gw-donate]]: **mattresses, box springs and pull-out couches**; **damaged or stained furniture**; baby furniture (cribs, strollers, car seats); large appliances such as refrigerators, freezers and air conditioners; large exercise equipment; tube, CRT and plasma TVs; and home building materials such as doors and cabinets.

## If no charity wants it: the City options

### Curbside bulk collection

Furniture is on Austin Resource Recovery's accepted bulk list. Curbside customers (single-family homes up to fourplexes) can book **up to three bulk collections a year** in the Austin Recycles app, online, or by calling Austin 3-1-1 at **512-974-2000**. Nothing is picked up without an appointment, and items must be at the curb by **5:30 a.m.**, unbagged [[atx-od]]. See our [Austin couch guide](/austin/couch/) for the full set-out rules.

The City is direct about what happens next: there are **currently no recycling or reuse options** for furniture collected through bulk collection. It is working to open a **reuse warehouse**, funded by a **$4 million EPA grant**, that will accept drop-off furniture donations and pass furniture on to nonprofits furnishing homes for people leaving homelessness [[atx-od]]. No opening date is posted.

### What the Recycle and Reuse Center won't take

The City's Recycle and Reuse Center (appointment required) lists **furniture and mattresses** among items it **does not accept** [[atx-rrc]].

### More places to donate

For a full list of local reuse organizations, the City points residents to the **Austin Reuse Directory**. Contact the organizations listed to see whether they offer home pickup [[atx-reuse]] [[atx-od]].
