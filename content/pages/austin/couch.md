## The short version

Austin Resource Recovery will pick up a **couch or other furniture** as part of its **on-demand bulk collection** [[atx-od]]. You have to **book an appointment** first; items left at the curb without one won't be collected [[atx-od]].

Book in the **Austin Recycles app**, on the City's [My Schedule page](https://www.austintexas.gov/myschedule), or by calling **Austin 3-1-1 (512-974-2000)** [[atx-od]].

There's no extra charge per pickup: bulk is included in the **$24.90 monthly base fee** on the solid waste part of your City utility bill [[atx-fees]].

## Who qualifies

- **Single-family homes and buildings with up to four units** that get City curbside service [[atx-od]] [[atx-res]]
- Some homes in certain Limited Districts [[atx-od]]
- **Five or more units:** not covered. Those properties must use a private hauler [[atx-res]]
- Items set out **in an alley, in front of a vacant lot, or in front of a business** won't be collected [[atx-od]]

## How to book couch pickup

1. **Schedule** through the Austin Recycles app, online, or by calling 3-1-1 [[atx-od]].
2. **Select furniture** (and every other bulk item you'll set out). The scheduler shows what can be collected [[atx-od]].
3. **Watch your email** for a confirmation and a reminder closer to the date [[atx-od]].

**Need a different date?** Appointments can't be changed. Cancel and book again, using the same method you booked with or the cancel link in your confirmation email. Call 3-1-1 if canceling gives you trouble [[atx-od]].

You get **up to three bulk collections a year** per address [[atx-od]]. For the full bulk rules (tires, appliances, lumber and what isn't taken), see our [Austin bulk item collection guide](/austin/bulk-collection/).

## How to set the couch out

- **At the curb by 5:30 a.m.** on your appointment date [[atx-od]].
- **Put it in the non-metal pile.** Bulk set-out is sorted into three piles: metal items and rigid plastics; non-metal items (including carpeting and nail-free lumber); and passenger car tires [[atx-od]]. A couch belongs with the non-metal items.
- **Don't bag or box it.** Bagged items are treated as extra trash and may bring extra trash fees [[atx-od]].
- **Keep 5 feet clear** of carts, mailboxes, fences and walls, water meters, phone and electric boxes, fire hydrants and parked cars. Nothing under low tree limbs or power lines [[atx-od]].

**Missed pickup?** Different trucks may come at different times. If the couch is still there after **4 p.m.** on your date, call 3-1-1 or email Austin Resource Recovery after 4 p.m. and **within 2 business days** [[atx-od]].

## Donate it if it's still usable

The City says it currently has **no recycling or reuse option for furniture** collected through bulk pickup, and asks residents to rehome or donate gently used furniture when they can [[atx-od]]. A City reuse warehouse, funded with a $4 million EPA grant, is planned but not yet open [[atx-od]].

The City points to online marketplaces, neighbors and thrift or reuse stores, and to the [Austin Reuse Directory](https://www.austinreusedirectory.com/) for local reuse groups; contact them to ask whether they offer home pickup [[atx-od]].

## Drop-off won't work for a couch

Austin and Travis County residents can use the City's **Recycle and Reuse Center** by appointment, free except for tires at **$6 each** [[atx-rrc]]. It takes household hazardous waste, electronics and many appliances — but it **does not accept furniture or mattresses** [[atx-rrc]].

So for a couch, your official options are a bulk appointment, a donation, or a private hauler.
