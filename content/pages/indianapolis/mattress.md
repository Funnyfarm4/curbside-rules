## The short version

Indianapolis picks up old mattresses for free as **heavy trash**. Box springs and mattresses are on the City's heavy trash list, and the one rule specific to them is to **lay them flat on the ground**, not leaning against your cart, a tree or anything else [[indy-101]]. Homes on City trash service get heavy trash pickup **once a month** on an assigned day, with a limit of **two** heavy or bulky items a month [[indy-pickup]].

There's nothing to book. The catch is timing: if your heavy trash day just passed, or you're tossing more than two pieces, you'll need to drop it off yourself or pay someone to take it.

## Option 1: Heavy trash day (free, 2 items a month)

1. **Find your day.** Enter your address in the City's [trash day lookup](https://www.indy.gov/workflow/trash-pickup). It shows both your regular and heavy trash days [[indy-pickup]]. Or call the Mayor's Action Center (MAC) at **317-327-4622**, Monday to Wednesday and Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Thursday 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. and 3 p.m. to 5 p.m. [[indy-issue]]
2. **Count your items.** You can set out up to two heavy items, and that's your limit for the month [[indy-pickup]]. A mattress plus its box spring is two.
3. **Set it out flat.** Put the mattress at the curb **next to your trash cart**, lying **flat on the ground** [[indy-101]] [[indy-pickup]]. Collection starts at **7 a.m.** [[indy-pickup]], so have it out before then.
4. **Holiday weeks:** pickup slides one day later in weeks with an observed holiday, including Thanksgiving and Christmas. Holidays on a weekend aren't observed [[indy-pickup]].

If the crew can't take something, your service provider leaves a tag that explains how to meet the pickup requirements [[indy-pickup]].

### Missed pickup

Don't report it the same day. Crews have until **6 p.m.** and the MAC call center closes at 5 p.m. Call **317-327-4622** the next business day and allow **three business days** for service [[indy-issue]].

## Option 2: Drop it off yourself

**Marion County Citizens' Transfer Station** [[indy-cts]]:

- **Address:** 2324 S. Belmont Ave.
- **Hours:** Saturdays only, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. Closed the Saturday closest to Christmas Day and the Saturday closest to New Year's Day.
- **Cost:** **$2 per carload** or **$5 per SUV or pickup truckload**, cash only.
- **Limits:** up to two trips a day. Moving trucks, dual axles, trailers, haulers, stake-bed trucks and truckloads larger than 3/4 ton aren't permitted.

If the only way you can move your mattress is on a trailer or in a rented moving truck, the transfer station won't take that load.

**South Side Landfill**, 2561 Kentucky Ave., is the City's last-resort option. It's open Monday to Friday 5 a.m. to 7 p.m. and Saturday 5 a.m. to 3 p.m., closed Sunday, with pricing based on volume [[indy-101]].

## Option 3: Donate it

If the mattress is in good condition, the City suggests donating it and keeps a list of local donation centers at indy.gov/recycle [[indy-dropoff]]. Each charity sets its own rules, so call before you load it.

## Hiring a hauler

For a mattress stuck on an upper floor, several beds from a move, or anything past your two-item limit, the City's advice is to rent a dumpster or hire a **responsible** trash hauler [[indy-dumping]]. Before you pay:

- Ask to see their **Waste Hauler License** and **don't pay without a disposal receipt** [[indy-dropoff]].
- The City says properly disposing a truckload costs contractors **at least $60**, so be suspicious of a much lower price [[indy-dropoff]].
- Check references, whether they're licensed by the City, and how they plan to dispose of it. Some cheap removal offers end with the load dumped illegally [[indy-dumping]].

## Don't dump it

Mattresses are named on the City's list of things that count as illegal dumping when left at recycling drop-off sites [[indy-dropoff]]. Leaving one on public or private property without permission breaks Sec. 601-2 of the municipal code, with fines **up to $2,500** per instance [[indy-dumping]]. If someone dumps on your property, you're still responsible for the cleanup, and if a City contractor has to do it you're billed at least **$418** [[indy-dumping]].
