## The short version

Indianapolis doesn't make you book bulky item pickup. Homes on City trash service get **heavy trash pickup once a month** on an assigned day, and you can put out **up to two heavy or bulky items** next to your trash cart that day [[indy-pickup]]. The limit is **two items per month** [[indy-pickup]].

Monthly heavy trash pickup is one of the services paid for through property taxes and the **$32 a year** solid waste user fee, which is billed in two $16 installments on your property tax bill [[indy-cartfaq]]. The City doesn't list any separate charge for it.

## Step 1: Find your heavy trash day

1. Open the City's [trash day lookup](https://www.indy.gov/workflow/trash-pickup) and enter your address. It shows your regular trash day and your heavy trash day [[indy-pickup]].
2. Note the date. Your heavy trash day isn't the same every week; it comes once a month, and the day varies by address [[indy-pickup]].
3. Can't find it? The Mayor's Action Center (MAC) can look up your trash day. Call **317-327-4622**, Monday to Wednesday and Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., or Thursday 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. and 3 p.m. to 5 p.m. RequestIndy is available online and in the app around the clock [[indy-mac]] [[indy-issue]].

**Holidays:** pickup slides one day later in a week with an observed holiday: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas. Holidays that fall on a weekend aren't observed [[indy-pickup]].

**Who picks it up:** since January 1, 2026, Lakeshore Recycling Services (LRS) collects trash in solid waste districts 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 9 and 10, and DPW collects in districts 5, 7, 8 and 11. The City says heavy trash days and set-out rules did **not** change with the switch [[indy-cartfaq]].

## Step 2: Check that your items are accepted

Heavy trash means bulky items that won't fit in your cart [[indy-101]]. Common accepted items [[indy-101]]:

- **Household furniture:** couch, desk, dresser, table, chairs
- **Mattresses and box springs:** lay them **flat on the ground**, not leaning on other objects
- **Appliances without Freon,** such as a stove or water heater
- **Lawnmowers,** with gas and oil drained
- **Swing sets and disassembled bicycles** (an assembled bike can count if it's properly labeled)
- **Yard waste:** bundled limbs 3 feet in diameter and 3 feet long count as **one** item

The City's 2026 cart FAQ also lists a **TV shell**, with all electronic components removed, as acceptable [[indy-cartfaq]].

**Not accepted as heavy trash:**

- **Construction debris** [[indy-101]]
- **TVs with electronics still inside.** Haulers won't take them, and Indiana's e-waste law bans electronics from the trash [[indy-101]].
- **Tires.** Indiana landfills can't accept whole waste tires, so the City says you can't get rid of tires through curbside or heavy trash pickup [[indy-101]]. (One City FAQ lists tires mounted on rims as heavy trash [[indy-cartfaq]], but the City's main Trash 101 page says tires can't go out by law, so we follow the stricter rule. See our [appliance and tire guide](/indianapolis/appliance-tire-disposal/).)
- **Refrigerators, freezers, AC units** and other Freon appliances. These need a separate Freon pickup through the MAC [[indy-101]] [[indy-issue]].
- **Household hazardous waste and electronics** go to the City's ToxDrop program instead [[indy-101]].

## Step 3: Set it out the right way

1. **When:** on your heavy trash day. Collection starts at **7 a.m.**, and your cart has to be at the curb before then [[indy-pickup]]. The City doesn't list a separate set-out time for heavy items, so have them out by 7 a.m. too.
2. **Where:** at the curb, **next to your trash cart** [[indy-pickup]]. Carts go two to three feet from the curb, arrows facing the street or alley, at least five feet from other objects [[indy-pickup]].
3. **How many:** no more than **two** items [[indy-pickup]].
4. **If something is rejected,** your service provider leaves a tag explaining how to meet the pickup requirements [[indy-pickup]].

## Missed pickup

Don't report a missed pickup the same day. Crews have until **6 p.m.**, and the MAC call center closes at 5 p.m. Report it the next business day through the MAC at **317-327-4622**, then allow **three business days** for service [[indy-issue]].

## More than two items? Haul it yourself

The **Marion County Citizens' Transfer Station** takes extra household trash, heavy trash and bags of leaves [[indy-cts]]:

- **Where:** 2324 S. Belmont Ave.
- **When:** **Saturdays only,** 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. Closed the Saturdays closest to Christmas Day and New Year's Day.
- **Cost:** **$2 per carload** or **$5 per SUV or pickup truckload,** **cash only**.
- **Limits:** up to two trips a day. No moving trucks, dual axles, trailers, haulers, stake-bed trucks, or truckloads larger than 3/4 ton.

If that doesn't work, the City says you can take trash directly to **South Side Landfill**, 2561 Kentucky Ave., open Monday to Friday 5 a.m. to 7 p.m. and Saturday 5 a.m. to 3 p.m. Pricing is based on volume; the City links to the landfill for current rates [[indy-101]].

Still in good shape? The City suggests donating usable items first and lists local donation centers at indy.gov/recycle [[indy-dropoff]].

## Hiring a hauler

The City's own advice for bigger cleanouts is to rent a dumpster or hire a responsible hauler [[indy-dumping]]. Before you pay anyone:

- Ask to see their **Waste Hauler License** and **don't pay without a disposal receipt** [[indy-dropoff]].
- The City notes that properly disposing a truckload costs contractors **at least $60**, so be suspicious of anyone charging less [[indy-dropoff]].
- Ask how they plan to dispose of the trash. Some people who offer cheap removal dump it illegally [[indy-dumping]].

## Don't dump it

Leaving trash or unwanted items on public or private property without permission violates Sec. 601-2 of the municipal code, with fines of **up to $2,500** per instance [[indy-dumping]]. Furniture, mattresses and garbage left at City recycling drop-off sites count as illegal dumping too [[indy-dropoff]]. Report dumping through RequestIndy or the MAC at 317-327-4622 [[indy-dumping]].
