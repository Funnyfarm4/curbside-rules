## The short version

Indianapolis treats **leaves** and other **yard waste** differently depending on the season and how much you have.

- **Leaf collection season (2026: November 9–December 4):** put up to **40 bags of leaves** out each week with regular trash [[indy-leaf]].
- **The rest of the year:** bagged leaves and small yard waste go **inside** your trash cart with the lid closed; bags outside the cart aren't picked up [[indy-101]] [[indy-leaf]].
- **Brush/limbs:** a bundle **3 feet in diameter and 3 feet long** counts as one **heavy trash** item (two items a month) [[indy-101]] [[indy-pickup]].

Never rake leaves into the street, and don't burn them — burning in Marion County can mean fines **up to $2,500** [[indy-leaf]].

## Option 1: Fall leaf collection (40 bags a week)

The City's **2026 leaf collection season** begins **November 9** and ends **December 4** [[indy-leaf]]. During that window you can dispose of **40 bags of leaves each week** with your regular trash [[indy-leaf]] [[indy-101]].

### How to set leaves out

1. **Bag them.** Use **large plastic trash bags**. The City asks residents not to use paper bags because they fall apart in wet weather [[indy-leaf]].
2. **Place bags on the street** by **7 a.m.** on your collection day [[indy-leaf]].
3. Keep leaf bags **3–5 feet** from your cart collection area and **out of sidewalks and public rights-of-way** [[indy-leaf]].
4. **Never rake leaves into the street.** Clear nearby storm drain inlets to help prevent flooding [[indy-leaf]].

Find your regular trash day with the City's [trash day lookup](https://www.indy.gov/workflow/trash-pickup) or call the Mayor's Action Center at **317-327-4622** [[indy-pickup]] [[indy-issue]].

### Thanksgiving week 2026

There is **no** trash, heavy trash, leaf service or curbside recycling on **Thursday, November 26** (Thanksgiving). Thursday routes run **Friday, November 27**, and Friday routes run **Saturday, November 28** [[indy-leaf]]. **Tuesday, November 10** (Veterans Day) is **not** a solid-waste holiday; routes run as normal [[indy-leaf]].

## Option 2: Year-round yard waste in the trash cart

Outside leaf season — and for small amounts any time — you can put **bagged or bundled** yard waste in the **96-gallon trash cart** if it fits with the **lid closed**. That includes small twigs, leaves and grass [[indy-101]].

Rules that trip people up [[indy-101]] [[indy-leaf]]:

- **Bags outside the cart will not be picked up** after the leaf window.
- Leaves should **never** be placed **loose** inside the cart.
- **Loose yard waste** or yard waste that **doesn't fit** in the cart is on the City's "cannot go in your trash cart" list.

On pickup day the cart must be at the curb **before 7 a.m.**, two to three feet from the curb with arrows pointing to the street or alley, and at least five feet from objects and buildings [[indy-pickup]].

## Option 3: Bundled limbs as heavy trash

Yard waste that's too big for the cart can count as **heavy trash**. The City lists **bundled limbs 3 feet in diameter and 3 feet long** as one heavy trash item [[indy-101]]. You may set out **up to two** heavy or bulky items on your monthly heavy trash day, and the limit is **two a month** [[indy-pickup]].

Put heavy items at the curb **next to your trash cart** on that day; collection starts at **7 a.m.** [[indy-pickup]]. Look up your heavy trash day on the [trash day tool](https://www.indy.gov/workflow/trash-pickup) [[indy-pickup]]. For the full heavy trash list (furniture, mattresses, appliances without Freon, and more), see our [Indianapolis heavy trash guide](/indianapolis/heavy-trash-pickup/).

### Missed pickup

Don't report a miss the same day. Crews have until **6 p.m.** Report the next business day to the Mayor's Action Center at **317-327-4622** and allow **three business days** [[indy-issue]].

## Option 4: Drop-off and composting

### Citizens' Transfer Station

**2324 S. Belmont Ave.** — Saturdays **8 a.m. to 4 p.m.** (closed the Saturdays closest to Christmas and New Year's) [[indy-cts]].

- Takes **bags of leaves**, extra household trash and heavy trash [[indy-cts]] [[indy-101]].
- **$2 per carload** or **$5 per SUV or pickup truckload**, **cash only**; up to two trips a day [[indy-cts]].
- Moving trucks, dual axles, trailers, haulers, stake-bed trucks and loads larger than 3/4 ton aren't allowed [[indy-cts]].
- Leaves dropped here go to the **landfill**, not compost [[indy-leaf]].

### GreenCycle (compost)

The City says residents can take **brush, logs, leaves, grass and mixed yard waste** to **GreenCycle** at several Indy-area locations for a fee; those materials are turned into mulch, compost and soil blends [[indy-compost]] [[indy-101]]. For **leaves**, the City lists **$35** for pickup-truck drivers and **$80** for pickups with a trailer; **plastic bags are not accepted**, and leaves brought there are composted [[indy-leaf]]. Check current site hours and rates before you go (the City points you to GreenCycle's own rates page) [[indy-compost]].

### South Side Landfill

**2561 Kentucky Ave.**, phone **317-247-6808**. Hours listed on Trash 101: Monday–Friday **5 a.m.–7 p.m.**, Saturday **5 a.m.–3 p.m.**, closed Sunday [[indy-101]]. Resident leaf drop-off is composted and used as daily cover; pricing is by volume on their site [[indy-leaf]] [[indy-compost]].

### Home compost / mow in place

The City also suggests home composting and **mowing leaves in place** (mower height around **3 inches**) so they break down on the lawn [[indy-leaf]] [[indy-compost]]. See the City's [Composting](https://www.indy.gov/activity/home-composting) page for what to compost and local subscription options [[indy-compost]].

## If you need more hauled than the City will take

For a storm cleanup, a full yard of brush beyond two heavy-trash bundles, or a load that won't fit at the transfer station, the City's usual advice for oversized waste is a dumpster or a responsible hauler — same idea as other bulk cleanouts. Confirm they serve your ZIP and how they'll dispose of green waste before you book.

## Don't burn or dump leaves

Burning leaves is **illegal in Marion County** and may result in fines **up to $2,500** [[indy-leaf]]. Raking leaves into the street is also prohibited [[indy-leaf]].
