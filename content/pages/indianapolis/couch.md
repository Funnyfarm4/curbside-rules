## The short version

Indianapolis picks up couches for free as part of **heavy trash**. Household furniture such as a couch, desk, dresser, table or chairs is accepted [[indy-101]], and homes on City trash service get heavy trash pickup **once a month** on an assigned day [[indy-pickup]]. You can set out **up to two** heavy or bulky items, and the limit is **two a month** [[indy-pickup]].

There's no appointment to book. The catch is timing: if you just missed your heavy trash day, or you're clearing out more than two pieces, you'll need to haul the rest yourself or pay someone.

## Option 1: Heavy trash day (free, 2 items a month)

1. **Find your day.** Enter your address in the City's [trash day lookup](https://www.indy.gov/workflow/trash-pickup). It shows your regular and heavy trash days. The heavy trash day comes once a month and varies by address [[indy-pickup]]. You can also call the Mayor's Action Center (MAC) at **317-327-4622**, Monday to Wednesday and Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., or Thursday 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. and 3 p.m. to 5 p.m. [[indy-issue]]
2. **Count your items.** A couch counts as one item. A couch plus a recliner is your two for the month [[indy-pickup]].
3. **Set it out.** Put it at the curb **next to your trash cart** on your heavy trash day [[indy-pickup]]. Collection starts at **7 a.m.** [[indy-pickup]], and the City doesn't list a separate set-out time for heavy items, so have it out before then.
4. **Holiday weeks:** pickup slides one day later in a week with an observed holiday, including Thanksgiving and Christmas. Holidays that fall on a weekend aren't observed [[indy-pickup]].

If something isn't acceptable, your service provider leaves a tag explaining how to meet the pickup requirements [[indy-pickup]]. Building a sofa or deck from scratch? **Construction debris isn't accepted** as heavy trash [[indy-101]].

Getting rid of a mattress at the same time? Mattresses and box springs are heavy trash too, laid **flat on the ground**, not leaning on anything [[indy-101]], and each one counts toward the two-item limit. See our [Indianapolis heavy trash guide](/indianapolis/heavy-trash-pickup/) for the full list.

### Missed pickup

Don't report it the same day. Crews have until **6 p.m.**, and the MAC call center closes at 5 p.m. Report it the next business day at **317-327-4622** and allow **three business days** [[indy-issue]].

## Option 2: Drop it off yourself

**Marion County Citizens' Transfer Station** [[indy-cts]]:

- **Address:** 2324 S. Belmont Ave.
- **Hours:** Saturdays only, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. Closed the Saturday closest to Christmas Day and the Saturday closest to New Year's Day.
- **Cost:** **$2 per carload** or **$5 per SUV or pickup truckload**, cash only.
- **Limits:** up to two trips a day. Moving trucks, dual axles, trailers, haulers, stake-bed trucks and truckloads larger than 3/4 ton aren't permitted.

That last rule matters for couches: if it only fits on a trailer or in a rented moving truck, the transfer station won't take that load.

**South Side Landfill**, 2561 Kentucky Ave., is the City's fallback if nothing else works. It's open Monday to Friday 5 a.m. to 7 p.m. and Saturday 5 a.m. to 3 p.m., closed Sunday, and pricing is based on volume [[indy-101]].

## Option 3: Donate it

If the couch is still in good shape, the City suggests donating it first and keeps a list of local donation centers on indy.gov's [Recycling 101 page](https://www.indy.gov/activity/recycling-101) [[indy-dropoff]]. Call ahead, since each charity sets its own rules.

## Hiring a hauler

For more than two pieces, a whole-house cleanout or a couch stuck on an upper floor, the City's advice is to rent a dumpster or hire a **responsible** trash hauler [[indy-dumping]]. Before you pay:

- Ask to see their **Waste Hauler License** and **don't pay without a disposal receipt** [[indy-dropoff]].
- The City says properly disposing a truckload costs contractors **at least $60**, so be suspicious of a lower price [[indy-dropoff]].
- Check their background and references, whether they're licensed by the City, and how they plan to dispose of it. Some people who offer cheap removal dump the load illegally [[indy-dumping]].

## Don't dump it

Leaving furniture on public or private property without permission violates Sec. 601-2 of the municipal code, with fines of **up to $2,500** per instance [[indy-dumping]]. Couches and mattresses left at City recycling drop-off sites count as illegal dumping too [[indy-dropoff]]. If someone dumps on your property, you're still responsible for the cleanup, and if a City contractor has to do it you're billed at least **$418** [[indy-dumping]]. Report dumping through RequestIndy or the MAC at 317-327-4622 [[indy-dumping]].
