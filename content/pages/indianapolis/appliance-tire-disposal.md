## The short version

Indianapolis splits appliances into two groups. Appliances **with Freon or other refrigerants** (refrigerators, freezers, air conditioners) can't go in your trash cart [[indy-101]] and need a separately scheduled **Freon pickup** [[indy-issue]]. Appliances **without Freon**, like a stove or water heater, can go out as **heavy trash** [[indy-101]].

Tires are different again. Whole waste tires can't go to Indiana landfills under Indiana Code 13-20-14-1, so by law you **can't** put them out with curbside or heavy trash pickup [[indy-101]] [[idem-tires]].

## Refrigerators, freezers and AC units: schedule a Freon pickup

1. **Request the pickup** through the Mayor's Action Center (MAC). The City's trash request service covers scheduling refrigerator, freezer and AC unit pickup [[indy-issue]]. Call **317-327-4622**: Monday to Wednesday and Friday 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Thursday 8 a.m. to 2 p.m. and 3 p.m. to 5 p.m. RequestIndy works online and in the app 24/7 [[indy-issue]] [[indy-mac]].
2. **Wait up to 10 business days.** The City asks you to allow ten business days for a scheduled Freon pickup [[indy-issue]].
3. **Don't put it out on heavy trash day.** Heavy trash only covers appliances **without** Freon [[indy-101]] [[indy-cartfaq]].

**Cost:** the City pages we checked don't list a fee for Freon pickup, so ask the MAC when you book [[indy-issue]].

## Stoves, water heaters and other appliances: heavy trash day

Appliances **without** Freon, such as a stove or water heater, are on the City's list of accepted heavy trash [[indy-101]]. The rules:

- Put it at the curb **next to your trash cart** on your monthly heavy trash day [[indy-pickup]].
- It counts toward your limit of **two heavy items a month** [[indy-pickup]].
- Find your heavy trash day with the City's [address lookup](https://www.indy.gov/workflow/trash-pickup) [[indy-pickup]].

Full rules are in our [Indianapolis heavy trash guide](/indianapolis/heavy-trash-pickup/).

**Electronics are separate.** Indiana's e-waste law (IC 13-20.5) bans electronics from the trash, so they can't go out with curbside or heavy trash. Use the City's **ToxDrop** program or **RecycleForce**, 816 N. Sherman Drive, during its weekday hours [[indy-101]] [[indy-dropoff]]. Haulers also won't take TVs with electronics inside [[indy-101]].

## Tires: where to take them

### Liberty Tire drop-off

The City lists **Liberty Tire**, a national tire recycler with a collection site at **1009 W. Troy Ave.**, Indianapolis [[indy-101]]:

- **Open to the public:** Monday to Friday, **7:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.**
- **Passenger and light truck tires:** **$4 per tire**
- **Commercial truck tires** (semi tractors or trailers): **$12.75 per tire**
- **Other tires:** call **(317) 238-0388** for pricing

### Trade them in when you buy new tires

Indiana law requires stores that sell new tires to **accept at least one waste tire from you for each new tire you buy**, and they must post a sign saying so [[idem-regs]]. The City also points residents to this rule [[indy-101]].

### Annual mosquito-season tire drop-off

The Marion County Public Health Department's Mosquito Control Program holds an **annual tire drop-off event** during mosquito season. The City doesn't post dates, so contact the health department for details [[indy-101]]; its general line is **317-221-2000** [[indy-mac]].

### A note on the City's FAQ

One City FAQ about the 2026 hauler change lists "automobile tires mounted on their rims/wheels" as heavy trash [[indy-cartfaq]]. The City's main Trash 101 page says tires can't go out with curbside or heavy trash pickup by law [[indy-101]], so we recommend following that stricter rule. If you're unsure, call the MAC at 317-327-4622 before setting tires out.

## Too big a job to haul yourself?

If you hire someone, the City says to ask for their **Waste Hauler License** and not pay without a **disposal receipt** [[indy-dropoff]]. Check whether they're licensed by the City and ask how they'll dispose of it [[indy-dumping]].

## Don't dump it

Dumping tires or appliances on public or private property without permission violates Sec. 601-2 of the municipal code, with fines of **up to $2,500** per instance [[indy-dumping]]. Open dumping or open burning of waste tires is also illegal under Indiana law [[idem-tires]]. Report dumping through RequestIndy or the MAC at 317-327-4622 [[indy-dumping]].
