## The short version

If your furniture is clean and in good shape, two Indianapolis charities will pick it up for free: **Habitat for Humanity's ReStore** [[hab-donate]] and **SVdP Indy** [[svdp-give]]. Both are on the City's list of local donation centers [[indy-recycle]]. The catches are condition and timing. Each turns away stained, torn or damaged pieces, and free pickups are often about **two weeks out** [[hab-donate]] [[svdp-give]].

If you're moving this week, the pieces are upstairs, or a charity said no, skip to [what to do if they won't take it](#if-no-charity-will-take-it).

## Option 1: Habitat for Humanity ReStore (free pickup)

Greater Indy Habitat runs four ReStores and picks up donations **at no cost** [[hab-donate]].

- **What they take:** sofas, sectionals, chairs, recliners, tables, benches, trunks, bedroom furniture (including bunk beds, day beds and trundle beds), office furniture, patio furniture and consoles. They also take appliances such as refrigerators, freezers, stoves, washers and dryers, plus cabinets, lighting, tools and home decor [[hab-donate]].
- **Condition:** items must be in excellent working condition, with no rips, tears, stains, mold or odors, and no broken or missing parts. They can refuse donations at any point, **including on-site**, because of condition or disposal costs [[hab-donate]].
- **How to schedule:** call a store's donation line or fill out the online request form. A coordinator asks about condition, size and location [[hab-donate]]. Habitat suggests using the online form, since the phone lines often go to voicemail [[hab-donate]].
- **Lead time:** contact them about **two weeks** before the date you want. Shorter notice is still worth a call, since the schedule changes daily [[hab-donate]].
- **When:** routes typically run **Monday to Saturday, around 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.**, and each pickup gets a specific time. They aim to arrive within 15 minutes of it [[hab-donate]].
- **Receipt:** yes, listing your name, the date and the items [[hab-donate]].

**Donation lines** [[hab-donate]]:

- West Washington (4129 W. Washington St., Indianapolis): **317-969-8791** [[hab-stores]]
- Avon (1099 N. Avon Ave.): **317-707-7530** [[hab-stores]]
- Fishers (7998 Centerpoint Dr., Suite 100): **317-896-9423** [[hab-stores]]
- Greenfield (1141 W. US 40): **317-318-9458** [[hab-stores]]

**Dropping off instead?** All four stores take drop-offs **Tuesday to Saturday, 10 a.m. to 7 p.m.**, no appointment needed. Call ahead if you're bringing a big truckload [[hab-donate]].

## Option 2: SVdP Indy (free pickup, main level only)

The Society of St. Vincent de Paul gives donations first to neighbors in need, then sells the rest in its Mission 27 stores [[svdp-give]].

- **Free pickup:** typically within **two weeks**. Items must be **disassembled** and placed on the **main level, in a garage or outside**, and SVdP only takes items it can give away or resell. Small items must be in boxes, not bags [[svdp-give]].
- **How to schedule:** call **317-687-1006** [[svdp-give]]. SVdP's pickup post lists pickup days as **Tuesday to Saturday, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.** [[svdp-pickup]]
- **Faster, paid option:** SVdP's partner **ReSupply** picks up within **48 hours**, from any floor, will disassemble furniture, and accepts items in any condition. It charges a fee, which is **not tax deductible** [[svdp-give]]. Items SVdP can't use go to other local nonprofits or are disposed of [[svdp-pickup]].
- **Drop-off:** SVdP's Distribution Center, 1201 Maryland St., Dock Door 3 (south side of the building), Monday to Saturday 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. [[svdp-give]]

## Option 3: The Mustard Seed of Central Indiana (paid pickup)

This small nonprofit furnishes homes for families in need [[indy-recycle]]. It charges a **pickup service fee** to cover its trucks and warehouse, and it doesn't post the base amount on its pages [[ms-needs]].

- **Booking:** pickups are scheduled online only. They reply by email within about 24 hours, can't do same-day pickups, and don't send reminders [[ms-pickup]].
- **Distance:** they don't travel more than **25 miles from their Fishers location**, 10080 E. 121st St. [[ms-pickup]]
- **They take:** twin, full and queen mattresses and box springs with no stains, sofas, loveseats, armchairs, recliners, ottomans, dining tables and chairs, dressers, nightstands, and coffee and end tables [[ms-needs]].
- **They don't take:** glass-top dining tables, large appliances, filing cabinets or medical items. Sleeper sofas cost an extra **$100**, armoires **$100**, headboards and footboards **$25**, and like-new king beds **$50** [[ms-needs]].
- **Access:** no pickups from the third floor or higher without an elevator, and no items from homes with pests [[ms-needs]].

## Not on this list?

The City's donation list also includes Diversion Indy (high-quality, nearly new furniture and home goods), Thrifty Threads (furniture and home goods benefiting the Julian Center), Coburn Place and Exodus Refugee [[indy-recycle]]. Check each charity's own site for pickup options before you plan around them.

## If no charity will take it

1. **Heavy trash day (free):** put up to **two** pieces at the curb next to your trash cart on your monthly heavy trash day. Couches, desks, dressers, tables, chairs and mattresses (laid flat) are all accepted [[indy-101]]. See our [Indianapolis heavy trash guide](/indianapolis/heavy-trash-pickup/).
2. **Citizens' Transfer Station:** 2324 S. Belmont Ave., **Saturdays 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.**, **$2 a carload or $5 an SUV or pickup**, cash only, up to two trips a day. No trailers or moving trucks [[indy-cts]].
3. **Hire a hauler:** ask to see their Waste Hauler License, don't pay without a disposal receipt, and be suspicious of anyone charging under **$60** a truckload [[indy-dropoff]].

Don't leave furniture at a City recycling drop-off site. It counts as illegal dumping, with fines up to **$2,500** [[indy-dropoff]].
