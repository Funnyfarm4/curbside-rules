## The short version

If a dumpster, roll-off or storage container will sit on an Indianapolis **street, parking lane or sidewalk**, you need a **Right-of-Way Use Permit**. The City's right-of-way regulations say this permit is required for activities in the public right-of-way that aren't excavation, and they list **container placement** by name [[indy-row-regs]].

The permit comes from the **Department of Business and Neighborhood Services (DBNS)**, not the trash department. You apply online in the Accela Citizens Access Portal [[indy-row]], and DBNS says to allow **7 to 10 business days** [[indy-row-how]].

## Do you need one?

- **Street, curb lane, parking lane or sidewalk:** yes. A separate permit is required for each location or street [[indy-row-how]].
- **Your own driveway or yard:** the right-of-way rules are about public space, meaning traffic lanes, shoulders, sidewalks and parking lanes [[indy-row-how]]. We didn't find an indy.gov page that covers a container kept entirely on private property. If it's close to the line, or you're unsure where your property ends, call DBNS at **317-327-8700** before delivery [[indy-row]].
- **Next to parking meters:** don't remove or block meters without a permit. The application has to say if you'll restrict ParkIndy meters, IndyGo buses, the Cultural Trail or other trails [[indy-row-how]].

## What it costs in 2026

DBNS updated many of its fees on **January 5, 2026**, and applications received on or after that date use the new schedule [[indy-fees]]. On that schedule, every right-of-way permit has a **$40 application fee**, plus a daily review fee based on what you close [[indy-fee-sched]]:

- **Use of sidewalk:** $50 per day
- **Partial closure, regular road** (inside or outside the downtown Regional Center): $75 per day
- **Partial closure, thoroughfare:** $100 per day
- **Total closure, regular road:** $75 per day outside the Regional Center, $100 per day inside it

The 2026 schedule doesn't have a separate line for dumpsters or containers [[indy-fee-sched]], so DBNS decides which category fits when it reviews your application. The City's 2021 right-of-way regulations listed container placement at $118 a week outside the Regional Center and $166 a week inside it [[indy-row-regs]], but those amounts predate the 2026 fee update, so treat the 2026 schedule as current and confirm with DBNS.

**What's the Regional Center?** Roughly downtown: bounded by 16th Street on the north, I-65/I-70 on the east, Morris Street on the south and the Belt Railroad on the west, plus half a block on each side of Meridian Street from 16th to 30th Street [[indy-row-how]].

## How to apply

1. **Book the dumpster first, then ask who pulls the permit.** A licensed contractor isn't required for a use permit, but contractors usually make the request [[indy-row]] [[indy-row-how]].
2. **Create an Accela account.** An account is required to apply, and no paper application is needed [[indy-aca]]. All requests go through the online system [[indy-row-how]].
3. **Fill in the details DBNS asks for** [[indy-row-how]]:
   - the exact legal address of the parcel nearest the container
   - the exact date or date range
   - the area you're restricting: traffic lane, shoulder, sidewalk or parking lane, and which side of the street
   - whether you'll affect parking meters, bus stops, bike lanes or trails
4. **Be ready for a traffic plan.** DBNS may ask for a Maintenance of Traffic (MOT) plan showing signs, barrels and how pedestrians, bikes and wheelchairs get around the container, especially if you close a sidewalk [[indy-row-how]]. An unlicensed applicant on a large job that uses several lanes may need a site plan [[indy-row]].
5. **Wait for review and pay.** Plan on 7 to 10 business days [[indy-row-how]]. Permit updates come by email, and the permit is emailed to you once it's issued [[indy-aca]].

**Need more time?** You can extend a right-of-way permit **once**, before it expires, with the Amendment / Extension Form. More fees may apply, and after that you need a new permit [[indy-row-how]].

## Questions to ask DBNS

- **General permit questions:** DBNS at **317-327-8700**, 9 a.m. to 4 p.m. Monday to Friday, 200 E. Washington St. [[indy-row]], or permitquestions@indy.gov [[indy-fees]].
- **Right-of-way office:** 317-327-8583 or row@indy.gov [[indy-enc]].
- **Portal trouble:** ACA.Admin@Indy.gov [[indy-aca]].

## Picking a dumpster company

The City's advice for big cleanouts is to rent a dumpster or hire a responsible hauler, and to make sure they dispose of the waste properly. Check their background, references and whether they're licensed by the City, and ask for a copy of the disposal receipt [[indy-dumping]].

Only have a carload or a pickup load? The Citizens' Transfer Station, 2324 S. Belmont Ave., takes extra household and heavy trash on Saturdays from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. for **$2 a carload or $5 an SUV or pickup**, cash only. Trailers and moving trucks aren't allowed [[indy-cts]].
